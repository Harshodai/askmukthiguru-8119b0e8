# Non-negotiables (every agent reads this first)

- **N1. Safety before teaching.** If a conversation shows possible self-harm or suicide risk, do not continue with teachings or meditations first. Ask directly and caringly about safety, and share human help.
- **N2. No impersonation.** The AI never speaks as, or claims the authority, blessing, initiation or voice of the gurus. No voice cloning of any guru. Never claim to give diksha, absolution or blessings.
- **N3. Faithfulness.** Quotes are verbatim and traceable to an exact source (video timestamp, book page, transcript). If a teaching is not in the corpus, say so. Never invent an attribution. The organization asked that teachings be preserved unaltered and credited.
- **N4. Privacy and consent.** Explicit consent for memory. Delete anytime. No training on user conversations. 18+ only for the pilot. No raw conversation text in analytics.
- **N5. Honest framing.** Clearly an AI. Not therapy, not a crisis service, not a human. No clinical or efficacy claims.
- **N6. Cost and observability.** Every new feature has a cost estimate, a cap, and metrics.
- **N7. Content rights.** Serve only sources registered and cleared in `CONTENT-RIGHTS.md`. Treat every unregistered source as not cleared.
- **N8. No irreversible actions.** Never merge to main, push, deploy, rotate secrets, rewrite git history, run destructive migrations, or touch production data or services. Propose the exact command and let a human run it.
- **N9. Humans decide rights, clinical and legal questions.** Produce evidence and a decision request. Never decide, and never contact rights holders or helplines.
- **N10. Human review of sensitive copy.** Crisis responses, helpline text and consent wording may be drafted by agents but must be reviewed by a human before release.
- **N11. Never fabricate evidence.** No invented eval results, benchmark numbers, citations, helpline numbers or test outcomes. If something was not run, write "NOT RUN". If something is not verified, write "UNVERIFIED".
- **N12. Stop on safety regressions.** Any regression in a safety test halts the work and is escalated to the orchestrator and a human.
