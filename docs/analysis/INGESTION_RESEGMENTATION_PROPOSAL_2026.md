## 1. Executive Summary & Context: The Data Ceiling of Graded-8

The registered evaluation run `20261114-graded-8` represented the first formally valid registered evaluation in repository history under AGENTS.md Invariant X41 (verified with manifest collection fingerprint `c96200d7d6104b5c86aaaac7a965813ad4ec6c56443161ec4080b07ad9231835`). Evaluated across dual independent LLM judges (`anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5` and `google/gemini-3.6-flash`), the system achieved 30/40 concordant showable answers (75.0%), failing the pre-registered Bar 1 requirement of a Wilson 95% lower bound $\ge 0.850$.

Crucially, deep post-run forensic analysis (detailed in `docs/analysis/OUTSTANDING_ISSUES.md` §7.1 and Explorer Survey 3) proved that the 10 answer failures in `graded-8` were **not caused by retrieval failure or routing error**. In each case, the retrieval engine retrieved the highest-scoring vector and keyword match from the Qdrant index `first_person_v10`. However, the underlying text stored in Qdrant was **physically incomplete, truncated mid-thought, or severed at the point of ingestion**:
- 4 of the 10 failures (`q010`, `q019`, `q074`, `q085`) were caused by **premature clip truncation**, where discourses were chopped off before completing their logical, analogical, or causal arguments.
- 2 failures (`q022`, `q030`) were caused by **promotional/event-bound openings** leaking into indexed spans.
- 1 failure (`q070`) was an evaluation harness JSON parser timeout.
- 3 failures (`q006`, `q025`, `q040`) were marginal judge disagreements regarding conversational framing or tangential conclusions.

### The Stored Label Illusion (Finding D-01)
Every single one of the 10 failure clips in `graded-8` was indexed in Qdrant with:
- `completeness_status = "complete"`
- `boundary_status = "verified"` (25 of 27 spans)
- Terminal punctuation (`.`, `?`, `!`) present at the final character.

This revealed a profound architectural insight: **the existing ingestion quality gates operate strictly at the single-sentence syntactic level, blind to higher-order rhetorical and discourse structures.** A clip that cuts off after 3 of 4 promised enumeration items, or after an analogy premise without its conclusion, ends with a period (`.`) and passes sentence-level validation with flying colors. 

Serving-side gates (relevance scoring, content assessment, boundary validation) cannot recover discourse that was never stored. As concluded in `docs/analysis/OUTSTANDING_ISSUES.md` §7.1:
> *"Answer quality above about 0.75 needs either a stricter judge (fewer answers) or new segmentation (new collection, dry run and approval first). Gates alone will not get there."*

This proposal defines the comprehensive technical specification for the **`boundary_v2:rhetorical`** chunking grammar and migration protocol, providing an end-to-end strategy to permanently eliminate premature clip truncation at the data ingestion plane.

## 2. Root Cause Analysis of Graded-8 Clip Truncation

Detailed examination of the four primary truncation failures (`q010`, `q019`, `q074`, `q085`) reveals the three distinct rhetorical failure modes generated by the legacy chunker:

### 2.1 Case 1: Incomplete List / Truncated Enumeration (`q010`)
- **Target Question:** *"I can never figure out what I actually want from life — why is that?"*
- **Served Source Artifact:** Video `sMgbjxyrgqw`, legacy passage `sMgbjxyrgqw_v2_p18_0` (`start_s`: 1227.37, `end_s`: 1371.72, 273 words).
- **Dual Grader Assessment:** Both graders rejected the answer as `not_showable`.
  - *Claude-3-5-Sonnet:* "The clip lays out the levels of human seeking (achievement, love, impact) but never addresses why the seeker can't tell what they want. It also ends mid-thought before reaching the fourth seeking, and it contains an unpunctuated run-on stretch."
  - *Gemini-3-6-Flash:* "The passage introduces four levels of seeking but cuts off after explaining the third, leaving the thought incomplete."
- **Verbatim Stored Text:**
  > *"The first aspiration is for achievement... The second seeking is for love... Most people's pursuit ends here. They do not go beyond this to the third and the fourth... They move to the third seeking. The third seeking is seeking to create an impact in the world around you... They know life needs a greater purpose, a higher purpose, a higher vision."*
- **Root Cause at Ingestion:** The speaker opens a structured discourse explicitly declaring four levels of human seeking. However, at timestamp `1371.72s`, the legacy chunker hit a duration/word boundary and terminated the chunk immediately after the 3rd seeking. 
- **The Severed Discourse in Raw Transcript:** In the unsegmented transcript (`a3sAeqXrCYI.md:17-25` / raw whisper segments at `1372s–1568s`), the teacher immediately continues:
  > *"And when you realize this, you want to make this world a better place than when you enter it. Then you have the fourth seeking or the fourth pursuit, which is seeking for enlightenment... Well what we are teaching you is that it is indeed possible for you to be enlightened and manifest abundance..."*
  Because the chunk was severed at 1371.72s, the 4th seeking and the ultimate philosophical resolution were pushed into passage `p20`. When `q010` was retrieved, the harness expanded at most 3 spans, failing to capture the full discourse.

### 2.2 Case 2: Dangling Intro & Severed Analogy/Metaphor (`q019`)
- **Target Question:** *"Why do people living under the same roof end up feeling like strangers?"*
- **Served Source Artifact:** Video `x-mTRlE0TC4`, legacy passage `x-mTRlE0TC4_v2_p4_0` (`start_s`: 175.69, `end_s`: 285.17, 338 words).
- **Dual Grader Assessment:** Both graders rejected the answer as `not_showable`.
  - *Claude-3-5-Sonnet:* "It opens with a dangling reference ('everybody asked me this question') to a question the seeker never sees. It describes disconnection in families without really saying why housemates become strangers. It ends on a body-cell analogy that is never tied back to the family question."
  - *Gemini-3-6-Flash:* "The text cuts off mid-analogy about the human body without concluding the thought or returning to the main question about family disconnection."
- **Verbatim Stored Text:**
  > *"Today I know I meet so many people and everybody asked me this question... Now our human body functions in a state of oneness. All the 30 trillion cells are communicating with each other. As this communication is happening in a state of oneness, you are in good health."*
- **Root Cause at Ingestion:** This clip suffers from a dual defect:
  1. *Dangling Conversational Lead-In:* It opens with 25 seconds of unpruned chit-chat (*"Today I know I meet so many people and everybody asked me this question..."*), wasting precious word budget on zero-semantic overhead.
  2. *Severed Analogy Vehicle:* The speaker introduces the 30-trillion cells biological analogy as the vehicle to explain interpersonal division. But at `285.17s`, exactly when the positive analogy terminates (*"you are in good health."*), the legacy chunker executed a cut point. 
- **The Severed Discourse in Raw Transcript:** In raw transcript `x-mTRlE0TC4.md:33-37` (beginning at `285.61s`, passage `p5`):
  > *"When this communication breaks down, let us say that this communication is not proper and it is not in a state of oneness, then what the human body experiences is sickness. In the same way, when communication in families breaks down, division and suffering follow... So oneness as a state is very important for individuals and family members."*
  The legacy chunker placed the premise in Clip 4 and the resolution in Clip 5. Zero-overlap chunking ensured that Clip 4 had no moral/conclusion, while Clip 5 had no premise.

### 2.3 Case 3: Promissory Setup Cutoff (`q074` & `q085`)
- **Target Questions:** 
  - `q074`: *"Why do people actually get sick, from a spiritual or Ayurvedic perspective?"*
  - `q085`: *"Can my inner emotional state actually make me physically sick, or cause disease in my body?"*
- **Served Source Artifact:** Video `fdPQxZsPAwI`, legacy passage `fdPQxZsPAwI_v2_p1_0` (`start_s`: 12.25, `end_s`: 18.81, 15 words).
- **Dual Grader Assessment:** Graders returned `unsure` or `not_showable`.
  - *Claude-3-5-Sonnet:* "The clip is on topic and has the medical notice, but it stops at 'leaves your body agitated' before explaining how that causes illness, and it opens with a setup about suffering and growth that isn't tied to the question."
- **Verbatim Stored Text:**
  > *"There is this understanding that if you're not suffering, if you're not struggling, that you're not growing. Let us look a little deeper into it and let us understand how these states of suffering actually causes ill health within us."*
- **Root Cause at Ingestion:** The legacy chunker terminated at `18.81s` on the terminal period of a **pure promissory transitional sentence** (*"Let us understand how these states of suffering actually causes ill health within us."*). The clip contained 100% promise and 0% explanation.
- **The Severed Discourse in Raw Transcript:** Immediately following at `18.81s–66.59s`:
  > *"Your body moves away from a place of calm into agitation. Earlier times we used to experience stress when we probably faced a tiger... parasympathetic nervous system is being shut off... you are destroying your circulatory system, your digestive system, your reproductive system and you are slowly but surely you are an individual, you are a person who is diseased."*
  Because the chunker saw a period after "within us.", it severed the clip, leaving the entire substantive biological explanation in passage `p2`.

## 3. Analysis of Current Chunking Parameters

Investigation of the legacy chunking and segmentation codebase (`backend/services/speaker_diarization.py`, `backend/scripts/ops/build_full_corpus_v10.py`, `backend/scripts/ops/build_first_person_index.py`, and `backend/services/first_person_context_harness.py`) reveals four systemic architectural bottlenecks that mechanically force these truncations:

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                 LEGACY INGESTION PIPELINE BOTTLENECKS                              |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Rigid Word Caps:       Hard cap at 200 words (_CHILD_TARGET_WORDS=(100, 200)) forces premature  |
|                           splits mid-discourse (multi-part points take 250-350 words).              |
|                                                                                                    |
| 2. Zero-Overlap Slicing:  start = cut_end in _split_run guarantees that adjacent passages share    |
|                           0 words. Severed analogies lose their conclusion or premise forever.     |
|                                                                                                    |
| 3. Hypersensitive Pauses: _MAX_UNKNOWN_GAP_S = 3.0s treats natural 3.5s contemplative teacher      |
|                           pauses as run splits, shattering single discourses into isolated parents.|
|                                                                                                    |
| 4. Window Cap Forced Cut: _fit_under_cap in build_first_person_index.py blindly drops sentences to |
|                           force clips under 120s; harness caps parent window at max_context_spans=3|
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3.1 Parameter Audit
1. **Rigid Word Caps (`_CHILD_TARGET_WORDS = (100, 200)` in `speaker_diarization.py:72`)**:
   - The chunker enforces a rigid upper budget of 200 words per child passage.
   - Spiritual discourses, oral teachings, and philosophical explanations rarely conform to a 200-word limit: a four-part framework requires 280–350 words; a full cellular analogy with interpersonal application requires 300–400 words. When word count approaches 200, the chunker scans backwards for the nearest sentence terminator (`.`, `?`, `!`) and forces a split, severing the thought.

2. **Zero-Overlap Sliding Window (`start = cut_end` in `speaker_diarization.py:188`)**:
   - The legacy chunker operates with strict zero inter-clip overlap:
     ```python
     cut_end, kind = _cut_point(words, start, grown_end)
     chunks.append(words[start:cut_end])
     start = cut_end  # <--- ZERO OVERLAP
     ```
   - If a sentence introducing an analogy falls in Chunk $N$, its resolution in Chunk $N+1$ has no linguistic anchor. In modern RAG architectures, 15–25% semantic overlap is the industry standard to ensure that boundary concepts are queryable from both passages. Zero overlap is fatal to discourse continuity.

3. **Hypersensitive Pause Thresholds (`_MAX_UNKNOWN_GAP_S = 3.0` in `speaker_diarization.py:67`)**:
   - The diarization loop splits teacher speech into separate runs whenever silence exceeds 3.0 seconds:
     ```python
     if gap_s > self._MAX_UNKNOWN_GAP_S or gap_words > self._MAX_UNKNOWN_WORDS:
         _flush_run()
     ```
   - In live spiritual discourses, speakers regularly pause for 3.5 to 4.5 seconds between introducing a concept and elaborating upon it, allowing the audience to absorb the thought. The legacy diarizer interprets these contemplative pauses as speech cessation, splitting a single thematic discourse into two distinct parent contexts.

4. **Blind Sentence-Level Span Slicing (`build_full_corpus_v10.py:67-78`)**:
   - `slice_into_sentence_spans` slices sentences into atomic spans of 45–70 words.
   - `_fit_under_cap` in `build_first_person_index.py:405-450` enforces a hard duration cap of 120.0 seconds by dropping leading or trailing whole sentences.
   - The context harness (`first_person_context_harness.py:405`) limits parent window expansion to `max_context_spans = 3` (typically ~180 words). If a discourse spans 4 spans due to fine-grained 45-word slicing, the context harness cuts off the final span before serving.

## 4. Specification of the `boundary_v2:rhetorical` Chunker Grammar

To address these data-level defects while strictly adhering to repository invariants, we specify the `boundary_v2:rhetorical` chunking grammar under **ADR 0002** and **AGENTS.md Invariant X40**.

### 4.1 Invariant X40 Compliance & Grammar Naming
Per ADR 0002 and Invariant X40:
- Every chunker version string must declare the actual chunking class per grammar.
- Pre-contract legacy points must return `"unknown_legacy"`. Ingestion tools must never guess or synthesize unverified algorithms onto historical points.
- **Correction 2026-11-15:** X40 enumerates `boundary_v1:…`, `recursive_v1:…`, `adaptive_v1`, `semantic_v1`; `boundary_v2` is not among them. A new grammar has to be registered (ADR + the allowed set) **before** any payload is stamped with it, so the name below is a proposal, not an approved version string.
- The new rhetorical chunker version is proposed as:
  ```
  boundary_v2:rhetorical
  ```
  (or parameterized as `boundary_v2:rhetorical_<target_words>_<max_words>`, e.g., `boundary_v2:rhetorical_200_350`).
- Every generated passage must stamp `chunker_version = "boundary_v2:rhetorical"` onto its Qdrant payload and metadata record.

### 4.2 Core Architectural Principles of `boundary_v2:rhetorical`

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                boundary_v2:rhetorical SPECIFICATION                                |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Parameter / Rule             | Legacy Setting          | boundary_v2:rhetorical Setting            |
+------------------------------+-------------------------+-------------------------------------------+
| Target Word Budget           | 100 - 200 words         | Elastic: 180 - 220 target, 350 max ceiling|
| Max Duration Ceiling         | 120.0 seconds           | 160.0 seconds (for complete discourses)   |
| Inter-Clip Overlap           | 0 words (zero overlap)  | 1 - 2 complete sentences (25 - 45 words)  |
| Max Pause Gap Merge          | 3.0 seconds             | 5.0 seconds (same-speaker runs)           |
| Rhetorical Closure Invariant | None (Blind terminal .) | MANDATORY: Block cut on open discourse    |
| Dangling Opener Pruning      | None (Indexed verbatim) | Strip non-semantic conversational chatter  |
| Rights Stamping              | Optional / Unverified   | Register -> Stamp -> Filter -> Enforce    |
+------------------------------+-------------------------+-------------------------------------------+
```

### 4.3 The Rhetorical Closure Invariant
A chunker conforming to `boundary_v2:rhetorical` is prohibited from executing a boundary cut point if an active rhetorical structure remains unclosed, unless the hard safety cap (350 words or 160 seconds) is reached.

The engine maintains a state machine tracking four rhetorical categories:

1. **Enumeration Tracking:**
   - *Trigger Patterns:* Matches `ENUMERATION_INTRO_RE` (`r"\b(?:two|three|four|five|2|3|4|5)\s+(?:levels|aspirations|seekings|pursuits|stages|steps|types|pillars|truths)\b"`).
   - *Closure Invariant:* If $N$ items are promised (e.g. "four levels of seeking"), the segment window must remain open until item $N$ (`"fourth seeking"`) is detected and followed by its concluding sentence.

2. **Analogy & Metaphor Vehicle Preservation:**
   - *Trigger Patterns:* Matches `ANALOGY_INTRO_RE` (`r"\b(?:for example|is a great example|take the human body|consider the|just like|just as)\b"`).
   - *Closure Invariant:* A passage introducing an analogy cannot close while inside the analogy vehicle. It must remain open until the application marker is reached (`ANALOGY_RESOLUTION_RE`: `r"\b(?:in the same way|similarly|when this communication breaks down|in families|so oneness is)\b"`) and the vehicle is explicitly linked back to the human/spiritual subject.

3. **Promissory Setup Prohibition:**
   - *Trigger Patterns:* Matches `PROMISSORY_SETUP_RE` (`r"\b(?:let us (?:look|understand|examine|see|explore)\b.*?\b(?:causes|how|why|leads to)\b|here is (?:why|how|what happens)|the reason (?:why|is))\b"`).
   - *Closure Invariant:* The chunker is **strictly forbidden** from placing a cut point on or immediately following a promissory sentence. The window must extend forward by a minimum of 2 complete sentences or 50 words to enclose the promised explanation.

4. **Dangling Conversational Opener Pruning:**
   - *Trigger Patterns:* Matches `DANGLING_OPENER_RE` (`r"^(?:see\s+if\s+if\s+not\s+for\s+for\s+oneness\s+we\s+are\s+talking\s+about\s+a\s+world\s+that\s+is\s+not\s+feeling\s+one\.\s*)?Today\s+I\s+know\s+I\s+meet\s+so\s+many\s+people\s+and\s+everybody\s+asked\s+me\s+this\s+question\.\s*"`).
   - *Invariant:* Non-thematic conversational chit-chat, audio check greetings, or references to unseen audience questions are trimmed from the passage start, advancing `start_s` to the first substantive topical sentence.

### 4.4 Ingestion-Plane Promotional & Event Gate
In conformance with Requirement R2, `boundary_v2:rhetorical` integrates the extended `EVENT_MARKER_RE` directly into the ingestion ETL pipeline:
- Any passage containing upcoming-program or promotional wording (*"Ahead of us is Manifest..."*, *"I will lead you"*, *"birthing loving families"*) is rejected prior to Qdrant indexing.
- Per AGENTS.md Invariant X40, every rejected item is persisted to `public.corpus_gate_rejections` with reason `event_or_promotional_phrasing`, tier `ingest_quality_gate`, and exact source timestamps.

## 6. Re-Indexing Roadmap & Safe Deployment Protocol

Re-indexing a core semantic corpus is a critical operation. A flawed deployment can corrupt vector indexes, invalidate existing evaluations, or disrupt production serving. 

Per AGENTS.md Invariants **X6**, **X13**, and **X22**, we specify a **zero-risk, shadow collection deployment protocol**:

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    SHADOW DEPLOYMENT ARCHITECTURE                                  |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                                                                    |
|    [LIVE TRAFFIC]                                                                                  |
|          |                                                                                         |
|          v                                                                                         |
|    Qdrant Alias: 'first_person' ------------> [ACTIVE COLLECTION: first_person_v10]                |
|                                               (4,224 points, untouched, frozen)                    |
|                                                                                                    |
|                                                                                                    |
|    [RE-SEGMENTATION PIPELINE]                                                                      |
|          |                                                                                         |
|          +---> boundary_v2:rhetorical                                                              |
|          |                                                                                         |
|          +---> Rights Stamping (domain_rights_status='licensed')                                   |
|          |                                                                                         |
|          v                                                                                         |
|    [SHADOW COLLECTION: first_person_v12_shadow] <--- ISOLATED WRITE                                |
|          |                                                                                         |
|          |                                                                                         |
|    [OFFLINE VERIFICATION SUITE]                                                                    |
|          +---> Replay Graded-8 Rows + Negative Controls                                            |
|          +---> Verify Wilson Lower Bound >= 0.850                                                  |
|          +---> Verify Collection Fingerprint Generated                                             |
|          |                                                                                         |
|          v                                                                                         |
|    [ATOMIC ALIAS CUTOVER]                                                                          |
|          |                                                                                         |
|          +---> Qdrant update_collection_aliases:                                                   |
|                'first_person' -> first_person_v12_shadow                                           |
|                (Zero downtime, atomic instant cutover, instant rollback)                           |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 6.1 Phase 1: Pipeline Implementation & Adapter Registration
