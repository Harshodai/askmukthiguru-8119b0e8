## 3. UX-011: show remaining quota before the limit

Today the quota is visible only after it is used up (`QuotaAuthPrompt.tsx`, "Resets in N hours"). The 429 body carries `remaining`, `total_limit` and `retry_after_seconds` (`backend/app/api/chat.py:114-127`). A successful reply carries none of them (no quota header was found in `chat.py`).
Not implemented, because it needs a backend contract change and a UI decision:
1. Backend: add `X-Quota-Remaining` and `X-Quota-Limit` response headers on `/api/chat` and the stream route for anonymous sessions (the `QuotaResult` from `_enforce_anon_quota` already holds them). CORS must expose both headers.
2. Frontend: read the headers in `src/lib/chat/streaming.ts` (it already has `quotaRemaining` and `quotaTotalLimit` on errors) and show "N of 5 free questions left" from the last two questions on. Do not show it to signed-in users.
3. Tests: header present on 200 for anonymous sessions and absent for signed-in users; the UI line appears at 2 left or fewer.
Owner decision needed: show the count from the first question, or only near the limit.
