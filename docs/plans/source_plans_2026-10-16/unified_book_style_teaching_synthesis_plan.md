# Master Plan: Unified Book-Style Teaching Synthesis & Dual-Boundary Pipeline (Ingestion + Retrieval)

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

When a seeker asks a question, the response should not read like disconnected fragments sliced arbitrarily by an ASR transcriber, nor like generic AI paraphrasing. It must read like an authentic chapter from ***The Four Sacred Secrets***—where the living spoken words of **Sri Krishnaji** and **Sri Preethaji** are woven into **one unified, flowing spiritual discourse**, supported by elegant book-style citations and direct audio playback in the masters' authentic voices.

### The Two Non-Negotiable Directives
1. **Zero Paraphrase / Zero Regeneration Invariant**:
   > *"i dont want the answer to be given or generated by LLM, what I mean is that LLM should review and fix and not regenerating"*
   The LLM acts strictly as an **Elite Spiritual Manuscript Editor**, not a content author. 100% of the substantive spiritual teachings, vocabulary, metaphors, and parables remain the Gurus' authentic spoken words.

2. **Dual-Boundary Defense (Ingestion + Retrieval)**:
   > *"make sure that we need to fix these in the pipeline for ingestion itself and also handle this in retrieval itself"*
   We enforce hygiene at **both** ends of the system:
   - **At Ingestion Time (Write-Time)**: The ingestion pipeline prevents stutters (`carried carried`, `her her`), Whisper decoder loops (`arise eyes.`), severed relative clauses (`from which you perform`), and retreat announcements (`In February when we meet`) from ever entering Qdrant or Memgraph.
   - **At Retrieval Time (Serve-Time)**: For existing corpus data (the 1,854 clips currently indexed in `first_person_v7`), on-the-fly pre-scrubbing, discourse context enrichment, and LLM manuscript editing ensure that all legacy data is immediately perfected and presented as a unified book chapter.

## 3. The Four Core Pillars of the Unified Experience

### Pillar 1: The Sacred Arc of Awakening (Book-Chapter Narrative Flow)
In *The Four Sacred Secrets*, Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji do not speak in detached, competing blocks. Their teachings complement each other like two movements of a symphony:
1. **The Seeker's Predicament**: Validating the emotional reality, conflict, or division in consciousness (Sri Preethaji / Sri Krishnaji).
2. **The Foundational Law**: Revealing the universal truth (The Two States / Inner State determining destiny / The Nature of Consciousness).
3. **The Living Metaphor & Parable**: Illuminating through sacred allegories (The Hollow Bamboo / Ravana's Ten Heads).
4. **The Awakening Shift**: Living presence, dropping resistance, somatic stillness.
5. **Atma Vichara Inquiry**: 2–3 sharp contemplative questions (`*...*`) turning attention back to the observer.

### Pillar 2: Book-Style Citations & Multi-Voice Audio Typography
- **In-Text Footnote Markers**: Citations appear as clean markers `[1]`, `[2]`, `[3]` at natural thought boundaries, eliminating disruptive raw URLs in the middle of sentences.
- **Sources of Wisdom Footer**:
  ```markdown
  ---
  *Sources of Wisdom:*
  [1] Sri Krishnaji — [The Beautiful State](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hLg4WPG4ehE&t=72s) (01:12 – 02:45)
  [2] Sri Preethaji — [Healing Relationships](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1_-cZz8YRFw&t=790s) (13:10 – 14:30)
  ```
- **Dual Voice Audio Playback**: Packaging audio clips so the seeker can listen to both Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji speaking the exact passages.

### Pillar 3: Contextual Situating & Antecedent Grounding
- **Discourse Window ($N-1 / N+1$)**: Retrieved clips are accompanied by the video title (e.g. *"Peace - The Greatest Gift: Sri Krishnaji on Ravana's Ten Heads in the Ramayana"*).
- **Antecedent Grounding**: The LLM editor resolves vague spoken pronouns without altering doctrine (`"what sort of an experience it must have been for him to live with 10 heads..."` $\rightarrow$ `"...for Ravana to live with ten heads..."`).

### Pillar 4: Dual-Boundary Defense in Depth
- **Ingestion Pipeline**: Guarantees that any new video ingested into the system is pre-cleaned and boundary-perfect at write-time.
- **Retrieval Pipeline**: Guarantees that all existing clips in the live collection (`first_person_v7`) are cleaned and enhanced at serve-time.
