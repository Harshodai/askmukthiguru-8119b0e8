# What the 18 requests missed: how seekers actually use this (2026-10-17)

These are gaps in the asks, not in the code. Each is now a row in the task list (A25-A33) or a task in the master plan.

1. **Most questions get no clip.** Frozen-29 serves 9; only 15/29 have even one showable clip in the top 10 (CLAUDE.md invariant 24). Two in three seekers see an abstain. That outweighs layout. Missing: what the abstain screen offers (closest related clips marked "related", the full talk, a topic list, "ask a faculty member"), and which topics to record or ingest next. -> A25
2. **Safety inside compound questions.** "How do I stop wanting to die and find love?" must hit the crisis rail on the whole question before any facet split. No request said so. -> F1 rule; A26
3. **Practice steps.** Rev 1 had LLM-written "Daily Sadhana" steps. Practice instructions must come from recordings or the templated Serene Mind flow, never generated. -> A27
4. **Follow-ups.** "Tell me more", "say it simpler", "what did he mean by that" have no defined behaviour on a verbatim system. Options: next clip from the same talk, the full video at the timestamp, related clips. -> A28
5. **Indic seekers.** Quotes stay English (never translated). A Hindi seeker gets English quotes with Hindi framing. Nobody asked how that reads or whether the Listen button matters more for them. -> A29
6. **Listening is the product.** The ▶ button and the YouTube embed are the proof that it's real. Blocked embeds, slow mobile networks and offline use have no fallback. -> A30
7. **You can't know what users want without telemetry.** Telemetry writes fail (A8). Without it "tuned to the user" and "what users want" are guesses. Clip report button (`ClipReportsPage`, uncommitted on the Mac) plus a thumbs signal feed the listening audit. -> A31
8. **Personalisation consent.** "Tuned to users' interest" needs opt-in (T-B14 exists), a way to see and delete what is used, and no cross-user caching (caching invariants). -> A32
9. **The label "Related, not a direct answer" on every answer.** It is honest but may read as "this app can't answer". Copy needs a test with faculty. -> A33
10. **Faculty are the first users.** Faculty answer labels (migration `20261007000000_faculty_answer_labels.sql`) are not applied, so faculty feedback has nowhere to go. Already in the human gates, not in the 18 asks.
11. **Sri Amma Bhagavan onboarding** (the project's stated goal) has no rights evidence on file and no task in any plan.
