## 1. The goal

A seeker asks a question and gets the teacher's own recorded words: a verbatim clip of Sri
Preethaji or Sri Krishnaji, with the right speaker and the exact seconds, labelled honestly, or an
honest "I don't have that". Never generated text in the teacher's voice. Crisis messages are
handled before anything else.

Milestones, in order (section 15 of the list defines the evidence for each):
1. **Gate 1, locally proven** (E-1 safety, E-2 serving).
2. **Gate 2, Railway started** (E-3). Costs money from the minute it starts; owner's explicit go.
3. **Gate 3, faculty testing** (E-4): faculty test as normal signed-in users (owner ruled the
   faculty code is not needed; set `FACULTY_GATE_DISABLED=true` on Railway), listening audit of
   about 200 clips, 30+ rated answers, disclosed limits signed.
4. **Gate 4, public users** (E-7, with E-5 measured).
Later phase: calibrated "direct answer" label (E-6) and sub-second answers (E-8), both need human
labels.

Owner rulings that shape the work (list section 22): general chat is dropped as a default; the
general graph runs only when a signed-in seeker clicks Personalize. Serve the newest collection
through `FIRST_PERSON_COLLECTION` (today `first_person_v8`). Word floor is 15. Risk target
recommendation: 1% at delta 0.05 (owner may override).

## 6. What was learnt, with results

- **Selection is at its ceiling; data is the limit.** On the 29-question diagnostic set
  (AI-labelled), production serves 7-8 against a ceiling of 7 at relevance 3. Of the 14
  questions with no showable clip, 8 have a relevant clip cut badly and 6 have nothing relevant.
- **Interview answers were lost in the build:** 96 of 107 `passages_B` passages carry the host
  question, 0 of 62 in `passages_C_s1`; v8 stores 0 question texts.
- **Embedding prefix:** removing the instruction prefix on clips lifted good-versus-bad
  separation from 0.62 to 0.78 and on-topic versus out-of-corpus from 0.84 to 1.00 (AI labels;
  not re-run).
- **Speaker verification counts clip-edge windows:** counting inner windows only roughly doubles
  verified coverage (estimate).
- **Calibration arithmetic:** 299 consecutive error-free confident items for 1% at delta 0.05;
  150 labelled served clips supports roughly a 2-5% claim.
- **Latency:** current config p50 7.48 s, p95 13.74 s (29 questions x 3, uncached); the floor is
  the sequential gate calls.
- **Safety defects found and fixed in code:** crisis messages were rate-limited (WhatsApp) and
  load-shed (503) before any crisis check; a withdrawn helpline number was still sent; defaults
  failed open; the preview gate was silently off; the 18+ check skipped Google sign-in.
- **Platform:** the admission cap did not bound streaming or queued work and two workers doubled
  it (both fixed); Redis can evict rate limits under pressure (open).
- **Process:** one writer per shared file; append-only with a pre-write copy and a diff; grade
  every number; trace the served stage first; human gates are the critical path; a green CI check
  can mean "skipped" (golden-25).
