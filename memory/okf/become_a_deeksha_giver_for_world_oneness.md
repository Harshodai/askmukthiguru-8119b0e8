# Become A Deeksha Giver For World Oneness

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Join us on the 13th of February to contribute to World Oneness with your Tapas. For that will be your greatest Dharma towards healing Mother Earth. On the 13th of February, Shri Pritriji and I will be initiating Deeksha Givers. For giving Deeksha for Oneness for the next 21 days, until the 7th of March, it will be a part of your Tapas. I invite you to join Shri Pritriji and me in this Noblest of Causes.
