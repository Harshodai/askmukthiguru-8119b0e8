# The Nature Of Suffering And Anxiety

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
In fact, because of the Suffering State anxiety, you are erecting too many blocks on this invisible plane of consciousness. You are communicating to the universe and intelligence a very wrong message that you are not in a good state and since you are communicating this to the universe, you are going to create further problems in life. For having a beautiful life, you must understand that we will have to live in a Beautiful State and you will have to send out beautiful signals into the universe so that you can conquer any challenge and come out of your problems.

Namaste. We are in our fourth day together since we began this evolution series and I hope life is beautiful for all of you. Today let us talk about anxiety because anxiety is the state that most of the humanity is living in today with the coronavirus crisis. You know sometimes crisis builds but most of the times crisis comes. It comes as an emergency situation. It can come as a health crisis like the coronavirus now.
