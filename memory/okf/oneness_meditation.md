# Oneness Meditation

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
We will now learn the abundant life practice and everyday practice to nurture the three dimensions of the Beautiful State, the calm, connected and creative dimensions. As you do the practice every day, we are awakening the mystical Brahma-Garbha and nurturing the seeds that were sown in your consciousness during the limitless field meditation. Brahma-Garbha or the womb of limitless consciousness is the seat of awakening, which leads one to effortlessly access the higher realms in consciousness.

It is a seat in consciousness from which transcendence and abundance flow unendingly. Please sit still and close your eyes. Bring attention to your inner state. Recognize the exact emotion that is arising within you. It could be frustration, anxiety, sadness or it could be joy, love or calm. Take your observation deeper to see the thinking that is giving rise to these emotions.
