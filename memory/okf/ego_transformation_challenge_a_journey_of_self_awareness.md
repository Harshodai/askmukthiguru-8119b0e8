# Ego Transformation Challenge A Journey Of Self Awareness

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Shri Sri Preethaji's creation, the Oneness Yoga Challenge, is inspired by ancient Himalayan yogis' pursuit of enlightenment and the mystique experiences from her own consciousness. This unique yoga experience is all about awakening, body bliss. It also includes Yoganitra, which cleanses the brain of its toxins. It is a seven-day challenge that starts on June 15 and culminates on the International Day of Yoga, June 21. This yoga challenge will let you access hidden realms of your own consciousness and experience true bliss.

The greater purpose of yoga is often lost in the modern world. Yoga is not just about achieving good health or youthfulness. Yoga emerged from the ancient sages of India who were seekers of enlightenment. Yoga was created as a means to awaken the mystique fire, kundalini, in the consciousness of seekers and sages who were passionately pursuing enlightenment.
