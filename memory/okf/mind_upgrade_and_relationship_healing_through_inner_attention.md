# Mind Upgrade And Relationship Healing Through Inner Attention

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Our mind is made up of thoughts and in our mind we are thinking around 15,000 to 60,000 thoughts a day of these 80 to 85 percent of them are repetitive which means you're thinking the same thing again and again of these 70 percent are negative which means you're constantly scared of something you're angry with someone you're hurt with someone we are carrying a huge debris of thoughts in our consciousness imagine what we are signaling to the universe with such a negative state i think this is a real problem we need to find solution for this problem we should start by bringing attention to our inner state and we should hold a spiritual vision which is to connect with the other in a day at least for two minutes in our inner state learn to connect with someone i think if we can start there that'll be fantastic if you can even take two minutes a day and reflect on your inner state and if you can move into a Beautiful State of connection and of dissolving your anxiety or your stress if you can do this your blood flow will increase to the prefrontal cortex which means you'll have more intelligence and also to the anterior cingulate which will bring about more connection into your relationships this will be an extraordinary practice that each and every one of us can do
