# The Impact Of One S State On A Child S Life

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
And if the child is suffering and problems, who is going to suffer? Parents? So that is how karma flows in consciousness, so it is a flow in consciousness. Your state, your actions, your disconnected state that you are transmitting through your genes, everything is going to come and hit you. It will hit you every single second of your life, as you're living here, as you look into your children, as you look into your grandchildren, as you look at life around. It will keep hitting you.

Now, if you are going to live in that bad state and assume that you are going to have a child from that state, so what are you going to contribute to the child's life? Exactly, suffering. So that is how karma translates itself. And when the child is born, when the child is growing, if the child is, if the child's consciousness is infused with suffering by the parents, then the child is going to have suffering and problems.
