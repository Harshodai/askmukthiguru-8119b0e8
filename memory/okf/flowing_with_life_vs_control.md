# Flowing With Life Vs Control

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Then the dimension of great connection with the universal intelligence. And the last and the most important thing is the dimension of a great state of consciousness from which every moment of life is celebrated. I first want to break a common thinking that is found everywhere today. In the name of balancing the various spheres of life, let us see what is actually happening.

What is actually happening is a desperation and an ambition to be number one in every sphere of life, right? You are so ambitious that every sphere of your life you have to be number one. Well, if you have to live a successful life, a whole and a meaningful life, like a Buddha driving a Benz with your loved ones, you have to learn to flow with life. Right now, there is so much of struggle.
