# Future Of Gender And Sexuality Embracing Oneness

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Only that in some cultures they were stigmatized. They were seen as freaks of nature. But in ancient India, they were accepted as a part of society. In fact, coming to your own question of why do we find an increased number of them now? One, because of the reduction of social stigma, people are showing themselves up for who they are. Two, who knows if it is a part of the evolution of nature's design, that a few thousand years from now, gender would have no meaning at all. And there would be a new kind of a human race living on earth that is bisexual. However, know this, when a human being is different from you, do not judge them. Who gave you the authority to decide that you are the norm and those different from you are abnormal. To stop separating people on the basis of gender, sexual inclination, race, color, religion, nationality, all this must end. Instead, focus on the oneness of our humanity, then there will be respect. There will be harmony and there will be a natural goodness in the society.

Human beings have been all kind throughout history. Today they have been given names, they've actually been given legal recognitions in many countries in the world. But if you will take India, we had two of our most popular gods, Vishnu and Shiva, who produced a baby. And Shiva is often portrayed as a transgender god, as an Adhanari Shwara, half male and a half female. You look at every religion, every ancient scripture, you will find references to these beings.
