# Ekam Health Practice For Immunity

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
This is Ekam health practice. It is best done in the morning, so as to keep your immunity high throughout the day. You may even do it three times a day. As part of Ekam health practice, you are going to do Parikrama Pranayama, which means cyclic breathing. So in the 42 cycles of Parikrama Pranayama or cyclical breathing, you will do regular inhalation and Ujjayi exhalation or ocean exhalation.

Hold your breath. Listen to Hamsa. Now lie down in Shavasana or the corpse pose. Let your entire body rest on to the earth. Kill your blue Prana, your golden Ojas and your fiery white Tejas. Flow from the heart into all the 72,000 nadis or energy channels, like rivulets. Whatever sensations may arise, let them be. Feel that your body is in a space of great immunity and health. Thank Ekam for the blessings of health you have received today and slowly open your eyes.

- Feel that your body is in a space of great immunity and health. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
