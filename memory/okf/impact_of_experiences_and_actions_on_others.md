# Impact Of Experiences And Actions On Others

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Actions will follow your expressions and your actions then precipitate into your destiny, be it a positive or a negative one. That is why, Where do you see yourself in the cycle? Do you see yourself ascending to a great cycle of a great bhagya or a great destiny? Or do you see yourself hurtling down or a path of a painful destiny? Look back to history or look at contemporary leaders. Be it someone like Steve Jobs in his later years or like an Emperor Ashoka after the Kalinga war, every one of them made a shift in their life from self-centricness to heart-centricness because they had an awakening experience. Realization began to dawn on them, what am I becoming? Is this who I want to be as a human being? How are my experiences and actions impacting others around me? Is this what I want to do to my loved ones? Is this the impression that I want to leave for future generations?

Thoughts tend to repeat over and over again. That is 80% of your thoughts that you have today are the thoughts that you thought yesterday. Bhava means emotion. It is derived from the root word bhava, which means you become that. That is, you become that around which your thoughts gravitate, you become your emotions. These emotions lead to expressions, expressions that get communicated to others. So here, Natya Shastra stops, but when you bring this wisdom into real life, you see the impact goes very far.
