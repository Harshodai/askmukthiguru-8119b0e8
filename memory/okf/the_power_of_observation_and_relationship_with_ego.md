# The Power Of Observation And Relationship With Ego

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
If not, the same kind of thinking can repeat again and again. Same kind of indifference, same kind of hesitation, same kind of reluctance and then you go on and land in the same kind of failure. This failure pattern can also be an impression at the energy body level, at the pranamaya kosha. It could have blocked energies in the nadis in your energy body and then every time you approach a project, you approach a relationship, you approach something, you approach life, that energy gets blocked.

The most successful people also have their list of failure. You call them successful not because they did not fail. You call them successful because they have succeeded at some things. But do they not have failures on their list? Definitely they have. And then there are people who do not see themselves as being successful at all because they too have experienced failures in life.

## Key Teachings
- It could have blocked energies in the nadis in your energy body and then every time you approach a project, you approach a relationship, you approach something, you approach life, that energy gets blocked. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- Spiritual courage is the gift you will receive in the coming manifest so that you are able to break free or fail your patterns in your life. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- I call this the gift of spiritual courage because it is not about being bold, because it is about being. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
