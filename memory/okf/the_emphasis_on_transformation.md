# The Emphasis On Transformation

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Spirituality is about your inner journey, it's about your inner world, spirituality is about transformation, spirituality is about a massive change that can happen, spirituality and the mundane have to go together, mundane world is what you do externally, spirituality is how you live internally. Enlightenment means absence of suffering, enlightenment means an experience that arises as you become free of your suffering. Enlightenment state, Aham Drammasman, where there is no birth, there is no death, there is no beginning, there is no ending, there is no separation between you, matter, consciousness, energy is where you understand that there is absolutely no division and no separation and that you and the other are one and the same.

It is about your experience of life, it is about you, it's about you as a parent, you as a husband, you as a wife, as a grandparent, as a friend, as a leader, it's about you. How do you see yourself living as an unenlightened person or as an enlightened person? Do you want to just say to yourself, oh it's okay, this is how life is, this is how everyone are, it's okay, why should it change? Why is Krishnaji so much emphasizing on this transformation? It's okay, I have lived 30-40 years quite comfortably, I have sailed through all the challenges, I have somehow survived, it's okay, believe me that is what the whole world thinks, there's nothing unique about you thinking in that. It needs a seeker to be born to make that happen, understand what does a seeker mean? A seeker is one who is in search for truth, a tapasvi is one who is in search for truth, who is in search for making sure that suffering never arises and even if it arises you will never act, you will never speak a word from that state, you will first dissolve it. I'm sure most of you or all of you would have had this question, why this life, why this life at all, you've had this question right? And as you immerse in the state of Mahasamodha, the great delight, where your entire being comes awake and align, where every sense experience is aligned, then you would have found the answer for that question.
