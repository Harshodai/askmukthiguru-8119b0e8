# Preventing Depression Through Ancient Wisdom

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
If you want to prevent depression from arising either in yourself, in a loved one or in a colleague, there are five yukthis you should follow. These yukthis have a deep spiritual wisdom and also an incredible amount of neuroscience as a basis. Listen to me to the end because depression does not consider age, class or gender. It can affect a child as easily as it can affect a teenager or an elderly person.

The fifth yukti is connecting to the universe or the divine, san nidhi. Sleep and depression are closely linked to each other. Some people experience trouble with sleep before it becomes depression. While others move into depression and then experience sleep problems. Let me teach you an ancient way to quiet a noisy and a disturbed mind so you can slide into sleep.
