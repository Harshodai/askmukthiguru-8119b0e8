# Sustainable Living And Interconnectedness

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
We are attempting to learn new ways of sustainable living. It may appear hard to begin with, but if you remember the greater good your decision serves, you will find this shift rather easy and comfortable. Please remember, we are all connected. You cannot separate your well-being from that of Mother Earth's. Wish you all the very best in your endeavor to make a difference. Document your progress, first in your groups, so you may inspire more of your kind. Start practicing sustainability at home and be a one earth citizen.

A collective, inclusive action is the key to sustainable health and longevity of both ourselves and all other life-forms on earth. If you do not separate your longevity from that of other life-forms, our lives are intertwined and interconnected. Their well-being is your well-being. If rightly understood, the realization of interconnection and inseparability, it will be a great source of power. It will be great power and strength. If you're a movie buff, you should be familiar with the saying: "With great power comes great responsibility." And it is true. You become a true leader with the spirit to make a difference.

## Key Teachings
- We are attempting to learn new ways of sustainable living. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- A collective, inclusive action is the key to sustainable health and longevity of both ourselves and all other life-forms on earth. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- Spend a moment contemplating the living forms that survive the pitch, dark, and the pressures up to 15,000, 750 psi in the very deep Mariana Trench of the Pacific. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
