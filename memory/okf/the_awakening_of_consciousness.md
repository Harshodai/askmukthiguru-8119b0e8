# The Awakening Of Consciousness

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
During the Deeksha, I gave him the crust around his pineal gland to fill away and his brain was flooded by bliss molecules. This spiritual bliss is the greatest healer of the brain. It erased addiction from his brain. That is why Shri Sri Preethaji and I believe that every young person must become enlightened. To have a brain that is healthy, to have a consciousness that is awake, to experience true power within oneself, you must become enlightened at a very young age.

Enlightenment is not only for addicts. Don't mistake me. It is better that you have a brain that is not prone to addiction. It is great to have a brain that is awake. It would be phenomenal to have a consciousness that does not succumb to suffering. It is that vision that we hold for you. Towards this vision, we have three doors open for you. The door of manifest, the door of tapas and the door to oneness changemakers training.
