# Dignity And Spiritual Power Of Women

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
We are completely connected in our consciousness and ACOM has been built for the purpose of connecting and uplifting human consciousness. Everyone who is connected right now from across the globe, all of you are meditating and consciously awakening for 10 most important things that need immediate attention before we lose this world. We can contribute towards world peace only when we dissolve the violence that we experience towards women.

If we are able to see a woman with total dignity, if we are able to see a woman for her immense spiritual power that she generates, not merely as an object, then we would impact our world consciousness towards great peace. When millions have found this inner peace, the world will be a peaceful place.
