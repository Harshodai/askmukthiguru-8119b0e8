# Seeing The Truth Of Self

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
And when we have that awareness to being, there's this freedom, this joy, and it's something genuine that comes and makes an impact to the world. And that's what this is all about. And for me, that is the biggest transformation. It's very special. This course in particular has been so amazingly impactful by seeing the truth in its most concrete form. It's like these pillars have shown up, showing my truth straight in front of me where there's absolutely no denying this is what I need to look at.

Imagine being free of that inner chatter, experiencing inner stillness, experiencing silence, that is to go beyond, or that is to declutch from the mind. Then the awakening would happen where you awaken to the witness consciousness. I have no words to describe then other than beautiful, how beautiful that this course is. It's to look inside yourself. There's nothing more magical than having a journey inward because it's all about being.
