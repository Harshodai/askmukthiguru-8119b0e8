# The Nature Of Suffering And Death

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Is death only the death of the physical body. One of the biggest pursuits of humankind for several thousands of years has been to conquer death. Ancient health and metallurgic sciences in India have left traces of their extensive research on this. Modern science too is continuing its research to conquer death through genetic engineering. Stem cell research and so on and so forth.

Even spirituality since ancient times has been grappling with the issue of death. And its ultimate answer to the challenge of death has been enlightenment. To conquer the fear of death and to celebrate every moment of living that is enlightenment. To transcend the idea of death itself. To realize that cessation itself is an illusion. To see that death is only a comma and not a full stop to life.
