# Emotional Connection And Growth

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
The Ikemen Light'em and festival was, I'm so grateful that I went. It was such a beautiful experience. Being immersed in that love energy, the grace and the sacredness and the just beautiful intention, overall intention of the festival was just absolutely divine. It really did something to my heart and since returning home, I feel different. I've said so many times to my friends, something happened to me at the Ikemen Light'em festival and what I've found is I really feel a stronger connection with people and I've been really surprised at times at my ability to respond in times of conflict in a way that was from a Beautiful State and in a way that created calm where in the past I wouldn't have done that, I would have created more conflict.

My husband was having a lot of uncertainty in his business and he didn't know if it was going to continue, he didn't know if his lease was going to continue, he didn't know if it was worth continuing. He's in retail, he didn't know if it was worth continuing. Profits were down and many of the things that I was worrying about before I went away completely dissolved by the time I got back.
