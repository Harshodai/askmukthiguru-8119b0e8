# Childhood As Purity And Forgiveness

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
It is because a child is inside each one of us. That child should be protected. For me, childhood is not an age. Childhood is purity of soul, purity of heart. Childhood is forgiveness. We always keep bridges in our minds when something goes wrong around us. We have a lot of animosity and other things, hatred. But in case of children, children forget and forget those things.

If, as grown-ups, we have difficulty handling these painful experiences, how can children handle them? Children are innocent. They can't handle judgment. They can't handle abandonment. They cannot bear negligence. They cannot bear prejudice. And least of all, violence. Their brains cannot handle seeing violence at home. Do you know that more than 1.7 billion children have seen violence in their homes before they became 18 years old?

## Key Teachings
- Childhood is purity of soul, purity of heart. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- We can keep that childhood inside us, but we should also take one action tonight or in the morning in other countries, just make an unknown child smile. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- Think of your own childhood, keep that child inside yourself, protect, nurse that child, don't let him or her die and same thing could be done with an unknown child, think how can you make a smile on the face of that child. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
