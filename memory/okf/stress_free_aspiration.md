# Stress Free Aspiration

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
See aspiration every one of us must have you should have I should have the country should have all of us must have But unfortunately every aspiration we have is fueled by comparison and what is causing the stress is comparison Comparison that one is comparison to his judgment So comparison that person is doing better. I should beat that person. I should be better than that person I should be better than my father many young men have this problem that I should be better than my father You know, especially as you go into successful families. That's a huge pressure So you have that massive comparison that's taking place and then you have judgment What is society going to talk about me? What are my parents going to talk about me?

And you judge yourself in comparison if you can handle comparison and judgment That is why I said aspiration is possible. Unfortunately the fuel we are using to move towards aspiration is a wrong fuel It'll just struggle and struggle and struggle. It'll never start So you can I change my fuel as you can change your fuel and the way you change your fuel is a Beautiful State You need to aspire and really fall in love with what you want to do Not by comparing yourself or judging yourself with what others are thinking of me What is going to happen to me if I fail what is going to happen to my life?
