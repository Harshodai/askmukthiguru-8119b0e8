# Observing Emotions For Mental Health

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
The human brain has a tremendous attraction for the negative. It is like a well-crow for anything that is negative. Finally, it is also like a deflon. It is like a non-stick for the positive. Brain is trained to remember something that is unpleasant. It is trained that way for millions of years to ensure survival. But our mental health today is getting affected because of this very habit.

Can you do this simple practice for your mental health? With this simple practice, you will cut through negativity and wire your brain to positivity.
