# Visualization Practice for Rising Above Emotional Wounds

## Summary
A guided breathing and visualization practice led by Sri Krishnaji to help practitioners cultive calm and see themselves remaining unaffected by emotional hurts and past conflicts.

## Practice Steps
- **Breathing:** Regulate your breath so that your exhalation is longer than your inhalation.
- **Spiritual Vision:** Cultivate your spiritual vision by visualizing yourself completely unaffected by emotional hurts.
- **Visualizing Interactions:** Picture the people who have hurt you in your mind, and see yourself remaining calm and unimpacted in their presence.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Krishnaji guides practitioners to use breathwork—specifically lengthening the exhalation—alongside visualization to cultivate emotional immunity and freedom from emotional wounds.

## Quotes
> "Your exhalation longer than your inhalation. See your spiritual vision. See yourself being unaffected by emotional hurts." — Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- **Emotional Immunity:** The state of remaining calm and unaffected by past or present emotional hurts.
