# Transcending the Need to Please Others and Overcoming Suffering

### Overview

Sri Krishnaji reveals the inner contradiction of the people pleaser, who is caught between hurt and fear. Using the humorous parable of the washerman, his son, and the donkey, he illustrates how trying to satisfy everyone leads to losing one's inner peace.

---

**Key Teachings:**
- People pleasing is rooted in an inner contradiction between hurt and fear of losing love.
- Pleasing has little to do with the other person's happiness; it is driven by egoic need for approval.
- Transcending the compulsive need to please allows one to become a true force for peace.

**Quotes:**
> "To be a pleaser means you are someone whose single focus is to not upset or disappoint people. You are caught between hurt and fear."
- Sri Krishnaji

> "When your responses come from a fearful space, you cannot cause harmony. Transcend the compulsive need to please."
- Sri Krishnaji
