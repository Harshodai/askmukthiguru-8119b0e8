# Rising Above Emotional Wounds and Holding a Passionate Vision

## Summary
At the conclusion of a meditation practice on overcoming emotional hurts, Sri Krishnaji encourages practitioners to hold their vision with deep passion and offers his blessings.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Krishnaji guides practitioners to hold their inner vision with deep passion following meditation on emotional healing.
- Sri Krishnaji offers his blessings for all the wonderful things practitioners aspire to bring into their lives.

## Quotes
> "Now hold this vision with deep passion as I give you my blessing." — Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Beautiful State: An inner state of passion, healing, and freedom from emotional hurts.
