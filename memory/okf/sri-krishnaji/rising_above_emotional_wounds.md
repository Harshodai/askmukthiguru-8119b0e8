# Rising Above Emotional Wounds

## Summary
Sri Krishnaji guides practitioners to set a clear intention to live free of emotional hurts while preparing to receive a blessing.

## Key Teachings
- **Intention for Healing:** Sri Krishnaji emphasizes holding a clear intention to live free of emotional hurts during the blessing.
- **Posture and Breath:** Practitioners are directed to close their eyes, sit erect, and breathe slowly and deeply to prepare for the practice.

## Practice Steps
1. Close your eyes and sit erect.
2. Breathe slowly and deeply.
3. Hold the intention to live free of emotional hurts as clearly as possible.

## Quotes
> "Today I want you to hold the intention to live free of emotional hurts as clearly as possible when I bless you." — Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- **Ekam Blessing:** Receiving spiritual grace with a clear, focused intention.
