# Rise Above Emotional Wounds

## Summary
Sri Krishnaji guides practitioners to sit erect, breathe deeply, and hold a clear intention to live free from emotional hurts during a blessing session.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Krishnaji instructs practitioners to hold a clear intention to live free of emotional hurts while receiving a blessing.
- The practice involves closing your eyes, sitting erect, and breathing slowly and deeply.

## Quotes
> "Today I want you to hold the intention to live free of emotional hurts as clearly as possible when I bless you." — Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Intention: Holding a clear inner focus during a blessing to live free of emotional wounds.
