# Visualization Practice to Rise Above Emotional Wounds

## Summary
A guided visualization and breathing practice led by Sri Krishnaji to help cultivate emotional immunity by seeing oneself unaffected by emotional hurts and past conflicts.

## Key Teachings
- **Breathing Technique**: Keep your exhalation longer than your inhalation during the practice.
- **Spiritual Vision**: Sri Krishnaji instructs practitioners to visualize themselves remaining entirely unaffected by emotional hurts.
- **Transforming Past Hurt**: Visualize the people who have caused hurt while seeing yourself standing unaffected in your spiritual vision.

## Practices / Steps
1. **Regulate Breathing**: Make your exhalation longer than your inhalation.
2. **Access Spiritual Vision**: Connect with your spiritual vision.
3. **Visualize Emotional Immunity**: See yourself being completely unaffected by emotional hurts.
4. **Confront Past Hurts**: Bring to mind all the people who hurt you and see yourself remaining calm and unaffected in their presence.

## Quotes
> "Your exhalation longer than your inhalation. See your spiritual vision. See yourself being unaffected by emotional hurts." — Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- **Emotional Hurts**: Inner wounds from interactions with others that can be transcended through spiritual vision and breathing practice.
