# Respecting History and Culture


## Summary
Sri Preethaji emphasizes the importance of respecting each country's unique history and culture.

## Key Teachings
- Every country possesses its own history and culture that deserves respect. (Sri Preethaji says)

## Quotes
> "In that same way, every country has their history, their culture, which has to be respected." — Sri Preethaji
