# Transcending Division to Heal Family


## Summary
To heal family relationships, it is essential to move beyond focusing on differences and instead recognize the common experiences that bridge divisions, leading to a space of oneness.

## Key Teachings
- To heal your family, stop focusing on how different you are from them. (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji)
- It is important to transcend division and duality to a space of oneness, even with family members. (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji)

## Related Concepts

- Judgment: a label or perception that can lead to suffering and separation.
