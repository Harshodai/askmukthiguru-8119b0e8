# Transcending Division To Heal Family

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
If you want to heal your family, stop focusing on how different you are from them or how superior you are to them. Namaste. For the first time in human history, perhaps we are experiencing one global crisis. No nation, no section of society, no geography is an exception to this global crisis. Hitherto when we thought of globalization, we thought of a flat world, a borderless world.

Can we go beyond division and duality to a space of oneness even with our family members as you sit across the dining table as you go about your daily chores amidst the four walls? Pause for a moment. Center yourself in your heart and reflect. It's not your child's longing for acceptance the same as yours even though you may express it differently. It's not your grandmother's clinging to life the same as your clinging to some position at work.

## Key Teachings
- If you want to heal your family, stop focusing on how different you are from them or how superior you are to them. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- Can we go beyond division and duality to a space of oneness even with our family members as you sit across the dining table as you go about your daily chores amidst the four walls? — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- In any form or name you choose, ask the divine to help you see that you and the members of your family are one. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
