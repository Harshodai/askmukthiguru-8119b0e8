# The Impact Of Actions On Family And Society

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
If you bring attention to the actions that you perform - the actions either that you perform in your family, the actions that you perform in your organization or in your community - those actions would create more suffering, more pain and remove joy and happiness out of many more people's lives. You have to be an individual who is contributing towards a more happier society, more holistic society, more joyful society. It is extremely important you as an individual learn to nurture a beautiful state in consciousness.

If we look at the problem that we see in the world around us, if you want to see the entire planet, we can see it as a planet that is diseased. A planet that is diseased, and if you want to call it, the organism, or as bacteria or a virus, and the virus of the bacteria that is diseasing this planet is a sense of separation which is caused by states of suffering.
