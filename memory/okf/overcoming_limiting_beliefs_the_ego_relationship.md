# Overcoming Limiting Beliefs The Ego Relationship

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
For a few moments, let me take you on a journey of field of abundance. On day one, I will reveal to you the Four Sacred Secrets. The Four Sacred Secrets you will need to manifest greatness in any aspect of life. On day two, you will silence the ghosts of your past. You will end the war within yourself. And I will lead you into the mystic dimension of calm.

On day three, you will immerse yourself in an experience of love. You will discover the true meaning and true purpose of love. You will no longer struggle to be loved. You will no longer struggle to love another. I will lead you into the mystic dimension of connection. On day four, you will free yourself of the obstacles that hinder your wealth creation and prosperity.
