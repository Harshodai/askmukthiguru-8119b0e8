# Understanding Addiction And Suffering

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
To escape a Suffering State you get addicted to pleasurable fantasizing but sadly all pleasurable fantasizing leads you into suffering states again. Some of you are speaking of being addicted to anger inferiority or depression. Some of you are speaking of being addicted to an activity like internet gaming or Netflix. A couple of you are speaking of being addicted to an external substance. Addiction is a monster to which you become a slave and gradually this monster begins to suck you. You will ignore the most pressing tasks of life and succumb to your addiction.

You will disconnect from people and engage in this addiction. You don't mind people suffering around you. You will become selfish and engage in addiction. You will be failing at life in so many areas but still you will engage in this addiction. Well, addictions are two kinds. There are physical addictions, addictions that are external to you and there are mental addictions or inner addictions, addictions to suffering states of mind. Physical addictions means you are dependent on an external resource to feel good. You are dependent on alcohol. You are dependent on smoking.

## Key Teachings
- Behind every external addiction, there is an addictive Suffering State. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- To escape a Suffering State you get addicted to pleasurable fantasizing but sadly all pleasurable fantasizing leads you into suffering states again. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- Addiction is a monster to which you become a slave and gradually this monster begins to suck you. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
