# Reflecting on Past Desires

## Summary
A reflection inviting individuals to look back at the various things they have desired throughout their lives, such as success, companionship, and recognition.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Preethaji prompts us to reflect on the nature of our lifelong desires.
- Throughout life, human beings hold desires for success, companionship, and the longing to be seen by others.

## Quotes
> "You have desired for so many things throughout your life. You have desired success. You have desired companionship. You have desired to be seen." — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- **Desire for Recognition**: The deep human longing to be seen and acknowledged by others.
- **Desire for Companionship**: The desire for connection and relationship throughout one's life.
