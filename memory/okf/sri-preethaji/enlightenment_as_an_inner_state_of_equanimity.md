# Enlightenment as an Inner State of Equanimity

## Summary
Enlightenment involves facing life's challenges from a deep inner state of equanimity, accompanied by the collapse of the illusion of separation.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Preethaji teaches that enlightenment allows one to navigate challenges from an inner state of equanimity.
- It is characterized by the collapse of the illusion of separation.
