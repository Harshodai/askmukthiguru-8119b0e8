# Realizing Oneness

## Summary
Enlightenment is defined as going beyond the illusion of separation and realizing fundamental oneness with all of existence.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Preethaji teaches that enlightenment is the dissolution of the illusion of separation.
- Realizing oneness means recognizing one's connection with all that is.

## Quotes
> "...illusion of separation and it is realizing the one is with all." — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- Oneness: The realization of being interconnected and undivided from all of existence.
