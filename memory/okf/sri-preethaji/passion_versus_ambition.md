# Passion versus Ambition

## Summary
For those pursuing education and building a career, success comes not from chasing the status of being number one, but from truly falling in love with what you are learning and doing.

## Key Teachings
- Do not chase after being number one.
- Truly fall in love with what you learn and pursue in your education and career building.
