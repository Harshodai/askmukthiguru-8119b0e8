# Conquering Fear of Failure

# Conquering Fear of Failure

## Summary
True success and humanity arise when individuals prioritize an other-centric passion—nurturing a drive to make a positive difference in the lives of others—rather than remaining caught in self-centric pursuits of profit and position.

## Key Teachings
- **Other-Centric Passion**: Sri Preethaji emphasizes the importance of nurturing a passion focused on making a difference in the lives of others.
- **Self-Centricity vs. Other-Centricity**: There is a profound difference between living a self-centric life focused solely on personal profit or position and living an other-centric life.

## Quotes
> "But nurture a passion with which you can create a difference with the lives of others. There is a world of difference between self-centricity and other" — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- **Other-Centricity**: Directing one's passion and energy toward creating a positive impact on the lives of others rather than focusing exclusively on self-centric goals.
