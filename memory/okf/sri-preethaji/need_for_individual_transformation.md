# Need for Individual Transformation

## Summary
Personal transformation and cultivating a positive inner state enable an individual to positively influence others. Through individual transformation, one is able to offer others the gift of connection and the gift of living in a Beautiful State of consciousness.

## Key Teachings
- An individual's personal transformation allows them to offer others the gift of genuine connection.
- Transformation enables one to share the gift of living in a Beautiful State with those around them, rather than relying on external rules or religions.

## Quotes
> "That you are able to give them a gift of connection. That you are able to give them the gift of living in a B..." — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- Beautiful State: A transformed inner state of consciousness that allows one to offer connection and positive influence to others.
