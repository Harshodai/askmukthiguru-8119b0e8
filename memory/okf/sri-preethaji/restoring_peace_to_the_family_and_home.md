# Restoring Peace to the Family and Home

## Summary
When love is absent within a home, families broken by domestic violence transform from potential sanctuaries of safety into environments of suffering, pain, and agony.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Preethaji highlights that without love, homes and families become places of pain and agony rather than environments of safety and peace.
- Domestic violence breaks families, causing severe suffering where there ought to be refuge and love.

## Quotes
> "They are places of pain and agony." — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- Absence of Love: The profound loss of safety and peace that occurs when love is missing in family relationships.
