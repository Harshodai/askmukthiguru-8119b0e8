# Letting Go of Attachments and Judgments

## Summary
This teaching illustrates the importance of releasing attachments and judgments through a fable of two monks, Yesmi and Nomi, and transitions into a meditation practice emphasizing breath and a gentle smile to cultivate calm.

## Key Teachings
- Letting go of attachments and judgments is essential for inner peace. Sri Preethaji illustrates this through the story of Yesmi, who carries a distressed woman across a river and later releases the experience, contrasting with Nomi, who holds onto judgment.
- Cultivating calm through meditation practices like focusing on breath and maintaining a gentle smile can help in embracing inner wisdom and awareness.
