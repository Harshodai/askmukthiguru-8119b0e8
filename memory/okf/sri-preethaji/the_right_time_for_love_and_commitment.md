# The Right Time for Love and Commitment

## Summary
Sri Preethaji discusses the concept of timing in relationships, emphasizing that there is no specific "right time" to enter a relationship other than age. She highlights the importance of one's state of being over external timing and encourages finding joy and passion in life rather than focusing on societal expectations of commitment.

## Key Teachings
- There is no specific time to enter a relationship other than age. Love is experienced from a young age, and the nature of loving relationships evolves as one grows older. (Sri Preethaji says, )
- Commitment and staying in one job are societal expectations, but ultimately, one must find what brings joy and passion. (Sri Preethaji says, )
- The state of being is more important than external timing. In the right state, time feels fluid and fast. (Sri Preethaji says, )
