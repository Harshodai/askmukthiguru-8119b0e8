# Enlightenment and Equanimity

## Summary
Enlightenment involves facing life's challenges from an inner state of equanimity and experiencing the collapse of the illusion of separation.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Preethaji teaches that enlightenment is meeting challenges from an inner state of equanimity.
- Enlightenment involves the collapse of the illusion of separation.

## Quotes
> "...from an inner state of equanimity. It is a collapse of..." — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- Enlightenment: An inner state of equanimity used to overcome challenges, marked by the collapse of the illusion of separation.
