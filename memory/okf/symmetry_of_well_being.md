# Symmetry of Well-being


## Summary
Peace is experienced through sensory details like the sounds of birds and children's laughter, and by being touched by experiences that give life meaning. This experience of peace is connected to the interconnectedness of all life and the importance of caring for others.

## Key Teachings
- Peace is felt through experiences such as hearing birds and children's laughter, and through meaningful life experiences. (Unknown Channel)
