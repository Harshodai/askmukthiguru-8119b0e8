# Cellular Memory Of Hurt

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
To feel accepted. And acceptance comes when you heal. Basically, you're feeling not accepted because you're hurt. Just like cancer comes when you eat too much sugar. If you stop eating sugar, there's a great possibility that you can avoid cancer. In the same way, you're feeling non-acceptance because of the hurt. There has been a damage that has happened.

Acceptance, non-acceptance begins there, you know. And in India there's this huge girl child, boy child and the child is, you know, all this nonsense, all rubbish thoughts. So understand that this is a problem that humanity has created, you know. And you need to heal from this. So again, you need to go through a transcendental journey to heal and to understand that it is that hurt could be stored in your genes, could be stored in your cells, could be stored in your brain, in your mind that you need to heal from.
