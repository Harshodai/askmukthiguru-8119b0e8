# Self Reflection And Transformation

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
So Enlightenment as a state has always been there. It's not new. It's probably the most awesome experience that ancients ever got to. But what is stopping us from experiencing these powerful states? Our self-centeredness and self-obsession. One of the greatest obsessions of humankind is the body. As you obsess with your body, you become a limited self. You move away from being one with everything to become a small little self.

As you become obsessed with your mind, the emotional states, states of fear, anger, craving, loneliness, you become self-obsessed. Your sense of self becomes narrow and separate. As you become obsessed with your past, this should not have happened. This should have happened this way. I should have met this man. I should have done the right decision in my business.
