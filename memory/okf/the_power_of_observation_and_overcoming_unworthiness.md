# The Power Of Observation And Overcoming Unworthiness

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
See, you are suffering, right? When you are suffering, save your heart. The heart is caused because of the self-image, okay? That is the truth you need to see, the truth that self-image is getting hurt. As you see the truth, you will know that the problem is different, whereas your suffering is arising from your self-image. So for example, you went and you wrote your exam, you expected you will score A for A plus and you scored B.

I might not get into this university. I need an A or A plus to get into this university. So fear, if I don't get into this university, what's going to happen to my life? Again, it goes back to self-image, all the future plans that you built, what's going to happen to that? So you are moving away from the problem of making sure that next time you can score A or A plus and really find out what went wrong in scoring a B.
