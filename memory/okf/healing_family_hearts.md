# Healing Family Hearts

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
If you want to heal your family, stop focusing on how different you are from them or how superior you are to them. Focus on the oneness of your human experience. From that space relate, converse, do things together. You will heal their hearts. For a few moments, let us meditate and experience the divine. Please sit still. Close your eyes. Center yourself in the heart.

Feel the divine who is the Antaryaman in all our hearts. In any form or name you choose, ask the divine to help you see that you and the members of your family are one. Their experience of life now is just the same as yours. May the divine bless you to accept and celebrate this oneness and togetherness with your family. You may now listen to the powerful Ekam mantra and chant along with it.

## Key Teachings
- If you want to heal your family, stop focusing on how different you are from them or how superior you are to them. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- Can we go beyond division and duality to a space of oneness even with our family members as you sit across the dining table as you go about your daily chores amidst the four walls? — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- Feel the divine who is the Antaryaman in all our hearts. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
