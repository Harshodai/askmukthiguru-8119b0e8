# Handling Disappointment

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Namaste, Shivpeetaji. This is Samyukta from Gujarat. I'm 14 years old. So my question is that sometimes it happens to me that people say something which I don't like and I get really angry, sad or disappointed and then I move into a Suffering State. So like I don't know how to overcome it. Like then yeah, thank you. Love you people. Love you. To have people not respond the way you want them to respond or not love you the way you want them to love you is very normal part of life. But the question is how are you addressing your hurt and your disappointment? That becomes the biggest question. To start with I would say do the serene mind practice. Don't allow the suffering or that anger or the disappointment or the hurt to continue to build. Usually what happens is you get hurt and thereafter response to that person is only from that hurt. You get hurt thereafter you believe that other person exists to hurt you. That becomes your perception, that becomes your idea of the other. But when you do the serene mind practice and dissolve that hurt you begin to live life, begin to live every moment, moment to moment and that's very beautiful. So do the serene mind practices again for everyone of you. Every time you get hurt, every time you get disappointed, bring attention to your inner state and dissolve that Suffering State. If you do not dissolve that suffering state please know that suffering is continuing to build in your body, in your mind and your consciousness.
