# State Of Expansion And Oneness

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Right before your exhalation begins. Continue to keep your eyes closed and observe the pause. Do not try to force a pause. Let's observe this pause for eight breaths. Let us now move beyond calm to a state of expansion. For the next eight breaths, inhale and exhale. And as you exhale, say quietly within yourself, aham or I am or I am the limitless consciousness.

We will say aham within ourselves together for the next eight cycles of breath. A gentle touch between your thumb and your index finger. Imagine, feel your body, all objects, all the people around you expanding into light. There is no separation. There is no division in the state of consciousness. In this field, thought and matter are one. Imagine all forms disappearing into one ocean of light.

## Key Teachings
- Let us now move beyond calm to a state of expansion. — Sri Preethaji
- There is no division in the state of consciousness. — Sri Preethaji
- Experience the state you would be in when you actually live your dream. — Sri Preethaji
