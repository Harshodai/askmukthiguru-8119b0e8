# Swadharma And Its Relationship With Ego

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
When you follow your swadharma, you feel that you are like a whistle on the scene that is favored by the winds to move towards its destination. The divine will be with you spurring your onward journey with synchronicities and breakthroughs. Fridaji and I are very passionate about awakening millions of young people to their swadharma. For only those who are living their swadharma can create social change, political change, cultural change and environmental change.

Today I want to speak to young people and the parents of young people. Let me begin with the real life story of one student who attended our courses a few years ago. He was a brilliant student but was slowly sinking into depression because of his troubled relationship with his father. In our youth course, he came upon a deep forgiveness towards his father and set himself free internally.

## Key Teachings
