# The Power Of Observation And Peace For Healing

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
You have no idea how your body can take care of itself when you connect with its intelligence. There are 30 to 40 trillion cells in your body and every second you are alive, these cells work tirelessly to achieve equilibrium, bringing you back to an intelligent balance. This intelligent balance is peace, which is also the natural state of healing for the body.

Only in a state of peace can any organism sustain life longest and the humans are no exception to this. Let us now understand how to let peace heal your body. The human body experiences two states. One state of the body is the survival state. The other state is the thriving state. In survival state your body does not care for your beauty, for your skin, for your hair because these are not important for survival or for your vigor.

## Key Teachings
- This intelligent balance is peace, which is also the natural state of healing for the body. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- And peace is the state you need to be in if you want to connect with your body intelligence and manifest health. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- Only in a state of peace can any organism sustain life longest and the humans are no exception to this. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
