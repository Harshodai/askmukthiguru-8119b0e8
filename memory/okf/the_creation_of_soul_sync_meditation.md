# The Creation of Soul Sync Meditation

## Summary
Soul Sync was created by Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji with a stated sacred
intention: to awaken people to the mystical dimension of consciousness. In the
same discourse the teachers describe abundance as flowing from the power of
one's own consciousness rather than from strategy alone.

## Key Teachings
- Soul Sync was created with the sacred intention of awakening people to the
  mystical dimension of consciousness, so they could manifest a beautiful life
  for themselves and their loved ones.
- Abundance has been a way of life for the teachers, and the key to it "has not
  been strategy alone but has been the power of our own consciousness."

## Quotes
> "When Sri Krishnaji and I created the Soul Sync meditation years ago, we did
> it with a sacred intention to offer humanity this gift of awakening people to
> the mystical dimension of consciousness so they could manifest our beautiful
> life for themselves and their loved ones."

> "For Sri Krishnaji and me, abundance has been a way of life and the key to
> this has not been strategy alone but has been the power of our own
> consciousness."

Both quotes are first-person from the teacher speaking in this discourse. The
corpus records the speaker only as "Ekam / O&O Academy", so they are attributed
here to the source discourse and are deliberately NOT assigned to a named
individual teacher — the recording does not establish which of the two is
speaking, and guessing would put words in a living teacher's mouth.

## Related Concepts
- Soul Sync: a meditation practice created by Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji.
- Abundance: presented as an outcome of consciousness, not of strategy alone.
