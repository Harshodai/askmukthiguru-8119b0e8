# The Repetitive Nature Of Thoughts

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Thoughts tend to repeat over and over again. That is 80% of your thoughts that you have today are the thoughts that you thought yesterday. Bhava means emotion. It is derived from the root word bhava, which means you become that. That is, you become that around which your thoughts gravitate, you become your emotions. These emotions lead to expressions, expressions that get communicated to others. So here, Natya Shastra stops, but when you bring this wisdom into real life, you see the impact goes very far.

Where do you see yourself in the cycle? Do you see yourself ascending to a great cycle of a great bagya or a great distance? Or do you see yourself hurtling down atogati or a path of a painful distance? Namaste. My father-in-law Sri Bhagavan would say, every country has its genius. India's greatest contribution to world consciousness is enlightenment or total cessation of ignorance and separation. That is why every art form, be it Indian dance, music, architecture, astrology, everything had one single direction, enlightenment. Let me take you from there. Let us take a saying from Natya Shastra or India's classical dance form, a 2,000-year-old dance form.
