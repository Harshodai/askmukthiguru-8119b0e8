# Guru Purnima And Sage Vyasa

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Guru Purnima is an ancient tradition. It is celebrated in the honor of Sage Vyasa, who's supposed to have completed the compilation of Brahma Sutras on that specific full moon day in the lunar calendar, more than three thousand years ago.

When you celebrate Guru Purnima, you are celebrating Sage Vyasa's stupendous work. You're also celebrating the potential of your own consciousness to rise above the limited life, to rise to glory, estates of enlightenment.
