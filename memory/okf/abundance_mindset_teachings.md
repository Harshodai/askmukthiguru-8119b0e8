# Abundance Mindset Teachings

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
See, there is no equivalent, exact equivalent for Lakshmi in English. Lakshmi means sacred, positive, noble, abundant, joyful. All these put together, that is Lakshmi. And this abundance consciousness, the ancients in India, said has eight facets to it. And financial abundance is just one of it. So what are these eight facets to wealth? Financial abundance, courage abundance, knowledge abundance, health abundance, victory abundance, love abundance, and wonderful children.

This is all this. We have only seen seven of them so far. So what is the eighth? Apart from these seven forms, there is the first of the abundance dimensions, which is called the Adi Lakshmi or the first abundance. Who or what is this first form of abundance? Who is Adi Lakshmi? Who is the source of all other forms of abundance? Even Hindus are wondered who that first Lakshmi is.
