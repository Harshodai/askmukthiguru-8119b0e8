# Addressing Depression Early

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Coming back to your question, every possibility is if you allow yourself to be depressed for this much time, then more depressed you're going to be. So the more you are getting into depression, the more difficult it is for you to get out of depression. So that is why it is important to recognize very early because when you recognize it very early, it's easy to get help and to walk out of depression as well.

Does time cure depression? Like sometimes people say, oh, I'm just going to sit in my room and in the darkness and in a month I'll talk to you. You probably get more depressed at the end of the day. Time does not cure depression. See, what is happening in depression is your body, your mind, your consciousness. Everything is leading you into that space where you are separating yourself from the rest of the world.
