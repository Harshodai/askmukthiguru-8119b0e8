# Understanding Suffering And Peace

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Conflict and violence are as old as humanity itself, they are the result of our self-centric consciousness. We separate ourselves from others and hence want to become bigger and more powerful than others. Whether we are a modern society, a globalized society or a primitive society, unless and until we brought attention to our fundamental self-centric thinking, there cannot be peace in the world.

Humanity has been a violent race. Earlier we fought with big naxes and arrows. Now we fight in automated machinery and weapons of mass destruction. World War II was supposed to be a war to end all wars. The Second World War officially ended in 1945. Do you know that since then there have been more than 250 major wars in the world? If you looked at the global map and asked which nation is experiencing peace inside its borders and externally with its neighbors, most of the world would be red.
