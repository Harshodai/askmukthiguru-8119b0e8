# States Of Being

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
The biggest problem we have in today's world is none of us have really learned to live in a Beautiful State. We have this constant state of anger, greed, frustration, fear. And it is only from these states that we are constantly trying to make our lives beautiful, become successful, have wonderful relationships with our families, and want the divine to be magic for us.

What do you think, if you take human beings, what do you think is their problem? Simple stress. At a basic level, we are all stressed. Each and every one of us experiences stress easily in our lives. We get stressed if our husband behaves in a particular way. We get stressed if our wife behaves in a particular way. We get stressed if we don't find the job going our way.
