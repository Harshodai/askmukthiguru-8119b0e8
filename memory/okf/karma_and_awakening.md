# Karma And Awakening

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
I would say yes and no. No nation can completely escape the consequences of karma. But you can break free of it if there is an awakening. If there is a breaking away from the very thinking that made you accumulate the karmic debt in the first place. America needs to move beyond its prejudice built on race, gender, ethnicity and more towards oneness. It is only with such an awakening to oneness and consciousness that the dense fog of negative karma will clear.

Now most of you know karma means our actions as also the merit and demerit of our actions. It is an energetic accumulation of the pain or the joy we may have given to others. The ancients in India speak of one of the mysterious ways in which karma operates. They say, Buddha karma nusare. Which means your karma becomes a huge force that will generate thoughts and desires in you and make you act in a particular way and the consequences flow.
