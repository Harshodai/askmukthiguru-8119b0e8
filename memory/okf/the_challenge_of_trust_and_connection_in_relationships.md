# The Challenge Of Trust And Connection In Relationships

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
When your partner or child moves into an anger outburst in the middle of a conversation, most of the times they are not asking for answers or for solutions. What they are actually looking for is an experience of connection and compassion. They want you to listen. They want you to feel them. Something magical happens when you listen with compassion for another.

It is like a constant game of you be in my life but maintain a distance, you be with me but don't get too close. Why is it so hard for two people who know each other very well to live together peacefully? Common sense says it must be easy. Common sense also says we simply need to be helpful and civil to each other. That is all. But is it that simple? If it were so, why do parents and children incessantly argue?
