# God as Universal Intelligence and Consciousness


## Summary
God is described as an intelligence and consciousness that is universally present throughout the entire universe. Modern science supports the idea that everything in the universe is interconnected.

## Key Teachings
- God is an intelligence that is present everywhere. (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji)
- God is consciousness that is present everywhere throughout this universe. (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji)
- Today's science is very clear that everything in this universe is interconnected. (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji)

## Quotes
> "So God is intelligence that's present everywhere. God is consciousness that's present everywhere throughout this universe." — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
