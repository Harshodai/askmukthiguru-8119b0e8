# Prayer And Namaste Mudra

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Would you like to join us in peace, Preyar? Preyar is beyond religion. It is a simple and a beautiful experience. Everyone must have once in a way. So what is Preyar? Preyar is a conversation with the universe. Preyar is an asking from the deepest recesses of the heart and a grateful receiving. It is a state of openness and trust. One of the best postures for Preyar is the pranamudra or the namaskara mudra.

The namaste posture is known to align the left and right hemispheres of your brain. So what else happens in a Preyar? A sincere and an unhurried asking. A calm and trusting conversation with the universal. So let me ask you a question. You have desired for so many things throughout your life. You have desired success. You have desired companionship. You have desired to be seen.
