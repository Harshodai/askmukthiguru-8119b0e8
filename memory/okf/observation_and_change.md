# Observation And Change

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
We have already wounded the present and hurt the future. Emotionally brouhous people would be leading the future of the earth. Do you realise that the ways of a parenting and education have to go through a massive revolution? Children need your loving presence. They need your attention. If you are a parent, do you really feel your child? As we awaken to peace consciousness, we will weaken the force of divisive consciousness that is manifesting as brutality and insensitivity towards children.

Parents, teachers and elders, please awaken into a Beautiful State and allow children to have their childhood. We will collectively bless the children of the world to have a beautiful life on our planet.
