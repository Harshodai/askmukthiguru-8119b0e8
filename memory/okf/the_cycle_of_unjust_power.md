# The Cycle Of Unjust Power

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
So one of the things that I think has happened over time is that we haven't listened to each other openly in that way. We haven't acknowledged injustice and so the division keeps going. When one group gets power they assert themselves in a way that may be unjust against another and the cycle continues. And so the idea is how do we stop the cycle of conflict?

Every form of division is first created in thought before it has translated into speech or action. If we intend to manifest a world free of division, we need to live with a consciousness that is free of boundaries. Right perception emerging from an awakened consciousness holds the key to a world free of discrimination. I sincerely doubt if we can ever chance upon any one common belief on which our humanity can stay united.
