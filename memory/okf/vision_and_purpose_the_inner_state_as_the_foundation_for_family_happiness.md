# Vision And Purpose The Inner State As The Foundation For Family Happiness

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
We are trained into having a vision for what we want to achieve. If we can turn our attention inward, if we can hold a vision for our inner state and if we can hold a vision for how we want to experience our family, how we want to experience togetherness in our family, how we hold a vision for each other's being, for their inner state. Then we become extremely powerful, not only in creating extraordinary achievement in the external world.

Recently I heard a guru in India say that a woman is responsible for a happy family. But I would like to think about it and why should a woman be alone responsible for creating a happy family? A happy family is created by an individual who has learnt to live in a Beautiful State of being. If an individual is not living in a Beautiful State of being, they become individuals capable of creating greater friction and conflict in the outside world because they are experiencing a lot of conflict and friction within.
