# The Impact of Disconnected States and Actions

## Summary
One's internal state, actions, and disconnected state, particularly when transmitted through genes, will inevitably manifest as problems and suffering, potentially affecting future generations like children and grandchildren.

## Key Teachings
- Your state, your actions, and your disconnected state transmitted through your genes will have consequences that affect you. (Unknown Channel)

## Quotes
> "Your state, your actions, your disconnected state that you are transmitting through your genes, everything is going to come and hit you." — Unknown Channel

## Related Concepts
- karma: The concept that one's state and actions determine their life, and that these can manifest as problems and suffering for future generations.
