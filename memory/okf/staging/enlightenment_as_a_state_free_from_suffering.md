# Enlightenment as a State Free from Suffering

## Summary
Enlightenment is described as a state of profound understanding and consciousness where one is free from suffering. This state is characterized by intense awareness and the absence of compulsive attachment to the past or future.

## Key Teachings
- Enlightenment is a state of profound understanding.
- This state is characterized by freedom from suffering.
- Enlightenment involves intense awareness.
- In an enlightened state, there is no compulsive attachment to the past or future.

## Related Concepts
- Enlightenment: Enlightenment is a state of profound understanding, characterized by a state of consciousness where one is free of suffering, with intense awareness and no compulsive attachment to the past or future.
- Suffering States: Suffering States refer to emotional states such as regret, sadness, and loneliness that disconnect individuals from life.
- Pain: Pain is a feeling of suffering or distress caused by physical or emotional harm.
