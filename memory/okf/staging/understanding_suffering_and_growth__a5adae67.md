# Understanding Suffering and Growth

## Summary
Suffering and growth are interconnected concepts. Understanding these allows for a deeper appreciation of both. Suffering can be seen as a necessary part of the growth process, as it helps individuals learn and evolve. By embracing suffering, one can grow and become stronger.

## Key Teachings
- Suffering is not the end but a part of the growth process.
- Embracing suffering can lead to personal growth and development.

## Quotes
> "Sri Preethaji says, 'Suffering is a necessary part of the growth process.'"
