# The Nature of Suffering: Complexion-Based Prejudice

## Summary
The dialogue in India regarding complexion-based prejudice, or colorism, is a significant issue that can lead to societal harm. Historically, all complexions were considered beautiful, as exemplified by figures like Lord Jagannath. However, an obsession with fairer skin has become prevalent, contributing to suffering.

## Key Teachings
- India is currently engaged in a dialogue about complexion that has far-reaching implications for society ("India, I believe, is in the middle of a dialogue now that can have far-reachin").
- There is an obsession with complexion, specifically with fairer skin ("sessed with complexion. This obsession with fairer s").
- Ancient beliefs honored all complexions as beautiful, a concept that influenced figures like Lord Jagannath ("Lord Jagannath as an example of a god influenced by the original ethos of honoring people of all colors").
