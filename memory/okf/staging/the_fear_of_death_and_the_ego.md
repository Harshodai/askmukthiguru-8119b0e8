# The Fear of Death and the Ego

## Summary
The fear of death is not merely the fear of the physical body's demise but extends to the loss of one's entire identity, including innumerable memories and the sense of respectability. Enlightenment is presented as liberation from this fear, not exclusively for spiritual seekers but for anyone troubled by their current life experiences.

## Key Teachings
- The fear of death encompasses the "death of an entire identity and innumerable memories" — Sri Preethaji
- Enlightenment is the liberation from fear — Sri Preethaji

## Quotes
> "Death of an entire identity and innumerable memories," — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- identity: The sense of self, which includes one's memories and perceived respectability.
- enlightenment: Liberation from fear.
