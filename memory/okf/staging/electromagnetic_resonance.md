# Electromagnetic Resonance

## Summary
Earth has a measurable electromagnetic field that vibrates at 7.83 Hertz, and human beings also emit electromagnetic fields. For global transformation to occur, these fields must align through the principle of oneness.

## Key Teachings
- Earth's electromagnetic field vibrates at 7.83 Hertz.
- Human beings emit their own electromagnetic fields.
- Alignment between the Earth's field and human fields is necessary for global transformation.

## Quotes
> "It's called the electromagnetic field, and it vibrates at 7.83 Hertz."

- oneness: The principle of alignment and unity required for human and Earth fields to resonate together.
- Cosmic Energies: Forces that flow into the body and consciousness to facilitate movement into states of divinity and enlightenment.
- Enlightenment: A profound state of spiritual awakening and the ultimate goal of the human spiritual journey.
