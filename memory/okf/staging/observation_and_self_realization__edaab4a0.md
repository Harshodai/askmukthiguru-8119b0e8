# Observation and Self-Realization

Observation and Self-Realization

The teachings emphasize the distinction between ambition and passion as states of becoming successful. Passion leads to reduced self-obsession and greater success, while ambition is self-obsessive and focused on personal gain. The key difference lies in the state from which one is becoming successful. Passion and vision lead to a reduced self-obsession, while ambition is driven by self-obsession and focuses on personal gain. The teachings suggest that the primary focus should be on the state of mind that leads to success, rather than the outcome itself. This shift in focus can lead to a reduced self-obsession and greater success.
