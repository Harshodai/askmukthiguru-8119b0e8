# The Mind and Suffering

# The Mind and Suffering

## Summary
The mind is composed of thoughts, with individuals experiencing a vast number of thoughts daily. Suffering states are emotionally painful states that disconnect individuals from life and can lead to a downward spiral of chaos and problems.

## Key Teachings
- The mind is made up of thoughts. Individuals think approximately 15,000 to 60,000 thoughts a day. — Sri Preethaji
- Suffering States are emotionally painful states of being that disconnect individuals from life. — Sri Preethaji
- Suffering States can lead to a downward spiral of chaos and problems caused by accumulated poisons and limiting beliefs. — Sri Preethaji

## Quotes
> "Our mind is made up of thoughts and in our mind we are thinking around 15,000 to 60,000 thoughts a day..." — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- Suffering States: emotionally painful states of being that disconnect individuals from life, potentially leading to a downward spiral of chaos and problems.
- Overcoming Suffering: a concept related to the alleviation of suffering.
