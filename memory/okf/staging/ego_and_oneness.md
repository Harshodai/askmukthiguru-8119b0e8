# Ego and Oneness

## Summary
The concept of Ego is linked to a state of self-centeredness and separation. The third level of oneness involves the disappearance of separation and division, leading to profound interconnectedness and unity with all aspects of life, which is described as the highest form of oneness and associated with enlightenment.

## Key Teachings
- Ego is a state of self-centeredness and separation that can lead to conflict and violence. (concept: Ego)
- The third level of oneness is characterized by the disappearance of separation and division. (Source: Three Levels of Oneness | Evolution Series 107)
- This third level of oneness leads to a profound sense of interconnectedness and unity with oneself, others, and the universe. (Source: Three Levels of Oneness | Evolution Series 107)
- This highest form of oneness is associated with the experience of enlightenment. (Source: Three Levels of Oneness | Evolution Series 107)

## Related Concepts
- Ego: A state of self-centeredness and separation that can lead to conflict and violence. It is also a self-centered attitude that can influence a leader to make decisions that benefit themselves alone. Ego is a concept referring to the sense of self, identity, and the tendency to identify with thoughts and emotions. In Hindu philosophy, it represents the egoistic nature of the self. Ego is the sense of self that separates one from others and the world.
- Peace: A multifaceted state of being characterized by harmony, love, and unity.
- Suffering: The experience of pain, distress, or discomfort in one's life, which can be overcome by connecting with universal intelligence.
