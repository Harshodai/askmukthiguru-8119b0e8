# Love as a Universal Principle

## Summary
The concept of love is presented as a universal principle that applies to both receiving and giving. This principle is said to manifest in various contexts, including human interactions and the natural world.

## Key Teachings
- Love is a universal principle that applies to both taking and giving. (Unknown Channel)

## Quotes
> "That is the meaning of love, and this applies anywhere, even when we take love and support from each othe" — Unknown Channel
