# Gratitude as an Awareness from Contribution

## Summary
Gratitude is described as an awareness that arises from recognizing the contributions of everyone in one's existence.

## Key Teachings
- Gratitude is an awareness that you exist because of everyone's contributions (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji).

## Related Concepts
- The Power of Observation: The ability to observe and understand the world around us, which can lead to recognizing contributions.
