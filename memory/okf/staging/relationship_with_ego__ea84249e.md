# Relationship with EGO

## Summary
The video discusses the contrast between ambition and passion as states of being that influence one's success, emphasizing that ambition is driven by self-obsession and is focused on personal gain, while passion is driven by a vision and impacts others, leading to a reduced self-obsession and a more altruistic approach to success.

## Key Teachings
- **Ambition vs. Passion**: Ambition is driven by self-obsession and is focused on personal gain, while passion is driven by a vision and impacts others, leading to a reduced self-obsession and a more altruistic approach to success.

## Quotes
> "Ambition is driven by self-obsession and is focused on personal gain, while passion is driven by a vision and impacts others, leading to a reduced self-obsession and a more altruistic approach to success."
> - Sri Preethaji

- **Self-obsession**: The state of being driven by one's own desires and interests.
- **Altruism**: The practice of putting the needs and desires of others before one's own.
- **Vision**: A clear and inspiring goal or purpose that guides one's actions and decisions.
- **Self-care**: The practice of taking care of oneself and one's emotional and physical well-being.
- **Enlightenment**: The state of understanding one's true nature and realizing one's potential.
- **Transformation**: The process of changing oneself or one's circumstances for the better.
