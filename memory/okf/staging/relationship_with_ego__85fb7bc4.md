# Relationship with EGO

## Summary
The video discusses the concept of "constant companionship" and how it affects one's happiness and well-being, emphasizing the importance of maintaining a positive and supportive environment around oneself. It highlights the significance of having a constant companion that uplifts and encourages one's growth.

## Key Teachings
- **Teaching Point 1:** The speaker emphasizes the importance of having a constant companion that uplifts and encourages one's growth. This companion should be someone who is always there to support and inspire you, rather than a person who constantly criticizes or makes you feel bad.
- **Teaching Point 2:** The video suggests that having a constant companion who uplifts and encourages you can significantly enhance your happiness and well-being. It is crucial to surround yourself with people who are positive and supportive, rather than those who make you feel bad or bring negativity into your life.

## Quotes
> "Constant companionship is the key to happiness and well-being. It is important to have a constant companion who uplifts and encourages you."
> — Sri Preethaji
