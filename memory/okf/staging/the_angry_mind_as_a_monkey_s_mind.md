# The Angry Mind as a Monkey's Mind

## Summary
Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji describe an angry mind using the metaphor of a monkey's mind, highlighting the connection between anger and the loss of respect and opportunities in life. They emphasize the importance of recognizing and letting go of one's own anger to regain freedom and peace.

## Key Teachings
- An angry mind is like a monkey's mind (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji).
