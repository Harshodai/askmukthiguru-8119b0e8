# Understanding Anger and Blame

## Summary
This teaching emphasizes that anger is a habit rooted in blaming others. It highlights that while someone might hurt you once, the deeper and more lasting hurt often comes from within, through self-blame or continued external blame. The core message is to achieve freedom from anger by stopping the cycle of blaming others and oneself, and instead focusing on one's own actions that contribute to their emotional state.

## Key Teachings
- Anger is a habit of blaming others.
- The root cause of one's suffering or "beating" is often one's own actions.
- To achieve freedom from anger, it is crucial to stop blaming others and oneself.
- While others can hurt you once, the most significant hurt often comes from within.
- Individuals should focus on their own actions that contribute to their emotional state.
