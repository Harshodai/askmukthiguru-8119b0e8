# State of Expansion and Oneness

## Summary
This teaching guides the practitioner to experience the state of expansion and oneness by feeling a heartfelt desire as if it were being fulfilled in the present moment. It involves staying immersed in the space of a realized dream for a few breaths to cultivate an expansive state.

## Key Teachings
- To experience the state of expansion, feel your heartfelt desire as if it were being fulfilled at this very moment.
- Experience the state you would be in when you live your dream and stay immersed in that space for a few more breaths.
- When ready, take a deep breath and open your eyes.

## Quotes
> "Feel your heartfelt desire as if it were being fulfilled at this very moment. Experience the state you would be in when you live your dream. Stay immersed in that space for a few more breaths. Whenever you are ready, you may take a deep breath and open your eyes. Congratulations, you did it. For a few moments, I love your heart to swell in joy." — Ekam / O&O Academy
