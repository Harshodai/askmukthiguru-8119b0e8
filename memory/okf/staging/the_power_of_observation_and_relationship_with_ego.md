# The Power of Observation and Relationship with EGO

## Summary
This video discusses the psychological impact of failure, particularly the manifestation of failure patterns in the physical world. The speaker emphasizes the importance of observing one's actions and the consequences of those actions, which can help in transforming negative patterns into positive ones.

## Key Teachings
- **Failure Patterns**: The video explains how failure patterns manifest in the physical world due to the ego's influence. It suggests that by observing these patterns, one can understand the root cause and change the behavior.
- **Observation and Transformation**: The speaker encourages the audience to observe their actions and the outcomes, which can lead to a deeper understanding of their ego and the need to transform it.

## Quotes
> "Failure patterns are manifestations of the ego's influence. By observing these patterns, one can understand the root cause and change the behavior."
- "The ego is the root cause of failure patterns. By observing these patterns, one can understand the root cause and change the behavior."

## Related Concepts
- **Failure Patterns**: The manifestation of failure patterns in the physical world due to the ego's influence.
- **Ego**: The speaker emphasizes the importance of observing the ego and transforming it, as it is the root cause of failure patterns.
- **Transformation**: The process of changing negative patterns into positive ones through observation and understanding.
