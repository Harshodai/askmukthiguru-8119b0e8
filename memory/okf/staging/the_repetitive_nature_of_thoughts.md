# The Repetitive Nature of Thoughts

## Summary
This teaching highlights that a significant portion of our daily thoughts are repetitions of previous thoughts, suggesting a pattern in our mental processes.

## Key Teachings
- Thoughts tend to repeat over and over again. (Ekam / O&O Academy)
- Approximately 80% of the thoughts one has today are thoughts that were previously thought. (Ekam / O&O Academy)

## Quotes
> "Thoughts tend to repeat over and over again. That is 80% of your thoughts that you have today are the thoughts that you thoug" — Ekam / O&O Academy
