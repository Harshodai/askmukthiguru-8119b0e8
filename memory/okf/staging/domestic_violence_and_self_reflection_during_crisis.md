# Domestic Violence and Self-Reflection During Crisis

## Summary
The current global crisis has led to an increase in domestic violence, which is not an inherent part of human nature but a learned behavior. This violence is a personal tragedy and a loss of prosperity for the family. The key to preventing and overcoming it lies in self-reflection and forgiveness.

## Key Teachings
- Domestic violence is not an inherent part of human nature but is often a learned behavior.
- The current global crisis has led to an increase in domestic violence, particularly in families where women are disrespected and violated.
- This violence is not only a personal tragedy but also a loss of prosperity for the family.
- The key to preventing and overcoming domestic violence is self-reflection and forgiveness.

## Related Concepts
- Stressful State: A condition characterized by feelings of anxiety, tension, or overwhelm, which can contribute to increased domestic violence during a crisis.
- Chaos: A state of disorder, confusion, or randomness, which can be exacerbated during a global crisis and potentially contribute to domestic violence.
