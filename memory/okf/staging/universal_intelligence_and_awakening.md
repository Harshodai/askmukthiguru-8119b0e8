# Universal Intelligence and Awakening

## Summary
The concept of God consciousness is described as an awakening to the connection with the soul and the pervasive field of intelligence throughout the universe. This Universal Intelligence is a living, evolving being present in all aspects of existence, from atoms to life forms. Observation is a practice that allows one to perceive beyond the senses, leading to direct, intuitive experiences.
