# Cosmic Significance of Food and Consciousness

## Summary
The universe is likened to a fire sacrifice (Yagna), emphasizing the cosmic significance of food and consciousness as a sacred mystery. This perspective includes the ancient belief that the black hole at the center of the Milky Way will eventually consume the galaxy.

## Key Teachings
- The universe is compared to a fire sacrifice, or Yagna. (Unknown Channel says)
- Food and consciousness hold cosmic significance and are considered a sacred mystery. (Unknown Channel says)
- Ancient beliefs suggest the black hole in the center of the Milky Way will consume the galaxy in the distant future. (Unknown Channel says)

## Quotes
> "The black hole in the center" — Unknown Channel

## Related Concepts
- Yagna: A fire sacrifice, used as a metaphor for the universe.
- Black hole: A celestial object whose gravitational pull is so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape. In this context, it is associated with the eventual consumption of the Milky Way galaxy.
