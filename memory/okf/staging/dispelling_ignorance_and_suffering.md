# Dispelling Ignorance and Suffering

## Summary
This teaching emphasizes the importance of dispelling suffering and the ignorance that prevents individuals and their loved ones from being free of suffering. It connects this idea to the spiritual significance of an enlightened festival.

## Key Teachings
- The darkness called suffering must leave you. (Unknown Channel says)
- The ignorance that you cannot be free of suffering, that your loved ones cannot be free of suffering, must also leave you. (Unknown Channel says)

## Quotes
> "The darkness called suffering must leave you. The ignorance that you cannot be free of suffering, that your loved ones cannot be free of suffering," — Unknown Channel
