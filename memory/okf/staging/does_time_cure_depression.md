# Does Time Cure Depression?

## Summary
The idea that time alone can cure depression, such as by isolating oneself in darkness for a month, is a misconception. Depression is characterized as a state of separation from the world, and recognizing it early and seeking help are crucial for overcoming it.

## Key Teachings
- Time does not cure depression.
- Depression is a state of separation from the rest of the world.
- Recognizing depression early and seeking help are crucial steps to overcome it.
