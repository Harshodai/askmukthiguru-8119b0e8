# Nature of Suffering

## Summary
The video discusses the historical and psychological reasons for humanity's inclination towards war, emphasizing the inbuilt aggression and territoriality in humans, and the recurring nature of war throughout history. It suggests that humanity's war-mongering tendencies are deeply rooted in its evolutionary history and cultural conditions.

## Key Teachings
- **Nature of Suffering**: The video explores the concept of suffering, which includes emotional states such as regret, sadness, and loneliness that disconnect individuals from life. It emphasizes that these suffering states are not inherent but are the result of accumulated poisons and limiting beliefs.

## Quotes
> "The nature of suffering is not inherent but is the result of accumulated poisons and limiting beliefs."

## Related Concepts
- **Suffering States**: These are emotional states such as regret, sadness, and loneliness that disconnect individuals from life. They are described as states that disconnect individuals from the universal intelligence and its power.
