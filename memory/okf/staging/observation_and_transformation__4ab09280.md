## Summary
Recognizing and understanding the addictive states of the human mind, such as loneliness, depression, and anxiety, is crucial. The key is to recognize these states and the cycle of pleasure leading to suffering. The human mind can get addicted to painful states like anger, loneliness, depression, and anxiety. These are easy addictions that many youth are getting addicted to, and they often move into pleasurable fantasies to escape these states. The key is to observe these addictive states and the cycle of pleasure leading to suffering.
