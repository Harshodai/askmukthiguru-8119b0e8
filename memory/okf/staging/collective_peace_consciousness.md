# Collective Peace Consciousness

## Summary
This teaching encourages nurturing collective peace consciousness by coming together as a unified force for peace. It emphasizes the role of individual peacemakers in contributing to a larger, shared state of awareness and compassion, aligning with the broader concept of collective human consciousness as a site for awakening sensitivity and compassion.

## Key Teachings
- "Let us come together and nurture peace consciousness." — Prachiti (Unknown Channel)
- "I am with compelled peace. I am a peacemaker, you." — Prachiti (Unknown Channel)
- Collective human consciousness is the shared awareness of humanity that can be impacted to awaken sensitivity and compassion towards Mother Earth.
- Collective human consciousness receives peace energies and is the site of the Great Awakening.

## Quotes
> "Let us come together and nurture peace consciousness. I am with compelled peace. I am a peacemaker, you." — Prachiti

## Related Concepts
- Collective Human Consciousness: The shared awareness and mindset of humanity, which can undergo phase transitions through mass awakening and is the site of the Great Awakening.
- Peace Energies: A force that flows into collective human consciousness, directed towards healing and blessing those affected by or perpetrating war.
- I-Consciousness: A state of awareness and connection to one's own being, which can be expanded to include others, contributing to a more connected human consciousness.
- Enlightenment State: The highest state of consciousness, characterized by complete understanding and unity, which is the ultimate goal of the human spiritual journey.
