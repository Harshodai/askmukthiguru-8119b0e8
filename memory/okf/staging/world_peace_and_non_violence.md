# World Peace and Non-Violence

## Summary
The core teaching of the "Speak Peace Spread Peace" initiative, supported by Ekam for World Peace, is that global harmony begins with individual spiritual responsibility. To foster a peaceful world, every person must first cultivate inner peace, becoming a "peaceful human being" who serves as a "gift to their family" and a "powerful force for good in the world." This foundational shift from external conflict to internal tranquility is the ultimate answer to bridging diverse cultural and linguistic divides.

## Key Teachings
- The ultimate answer to bridging diverse cultural and linguistic divides lies in shifting from external conflict to internal tranquility.
- To foster a peaceful world, every person must first cultivate inner peace.
- A "peaceful human being" serves as a "gift to their family" and a "powerful force for good in the world."

## Related Concepts
- **Ekam**: An organization working for world peace, supported by multiple individuals.
- **Peace**: A multifaceted concept that encompasses a state of harmony, love, and unity. It can be achieved through various means, including inner calmness, collective awareness, and adherence to the principles of the Golden Ratio.
- **Peaceful human being**: An individual who has cultivated inner peace and serves as a positive force in their family and the world.
- **Meditation for World Peace**: The fourth pillar of the Temple of Peace, involving collective meditations to transform human consciousness towards peace.
- **Peace Education for Children**: The third pillar of the Temple of Peace, emphasizing the importance of teaching peace and compassion in schools.
