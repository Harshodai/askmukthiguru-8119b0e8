# The Nature of Enlightenment

# The Nature of Enlightenment

## Summary
Enlightenment is defined as a state of unperturbed peace and inner equanimity that allows one to overcome and conquer life's challenges.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Preethaji teaches that enlightenment is unperturbed peace.
- Enlightenment provides the inner capacity to conquer every challenge faced in life.

## Quotes
> "Enlightenment is unperturbed peace. It is to conquer every chal" — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- moksha: Liberation or enlightenment characterized by unperturbed peace.
