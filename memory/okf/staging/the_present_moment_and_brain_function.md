# The Present Moment and Brain Function

## Summary
Being present is crucial for solving problems and for the brain to shift from the amygdala to the prefrontal cortex, which is important for mental health and positivity.

## Key Teachings
- It is only by being in the present that any problem can be solved (Ekam / O&O Academy says).
- The shift from the amygdala to the prefrontal cortex can only happen if one is present (Ekam / O&O Academy says).

## Related Concepts
- Consciousness: a multifaceted and complex entity that encompasses various aspects of human experience, including awareness of one's thoughts, emotions, and surroundings.
