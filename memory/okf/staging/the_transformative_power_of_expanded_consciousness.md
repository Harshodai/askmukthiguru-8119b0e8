# The Transformative Power of Expanded Consciousness

## Summary
Expanded consciousness allows an individual to move beyond judgment and analysis of others, enabling them to support and transform those around them. This shift in consciousness can lead to positive changes in others that were previously unattainable.

## Key Teachings
- When one's consciousness expands, the ability to influence others positively changes. (Sri Preethaji says)
- Before expanded consciousness, attempts to change others (e.g., "Can he be a little more warm? Can she be a little more loving?") were unsuccessful. (Sri Preethaji says)
- With expanded consciousness, the individual can facilitate transformation in others. (Sri Preethaji says)

## Quotes
> "Can he be a little more warm? Can she be a little more loving? But it did not happen then you couldn't change that person But now with your consciousness" — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- Consciousness: a multifaceted and complex entity that encompasses various aspects of human experience, including awareness, perception, and the sense of self. It can be expanded to influence others positively.
