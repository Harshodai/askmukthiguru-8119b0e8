# Karma, Awakening, and Oneness

## Summary
Karma is described as the energetic accumulation of pain or joy given to others. Negative karmic debt requires clearing through awakening and oneness. The concept highlights the importance of moving beyond prejudice towards oneness to break free from negative karma.

## Key Teachings
- Karma refers to the energetic accumulation of pain or joy that we may have given to others.
- Negative karmic debt must be cleared through awakening and oneness.
- Awakening is necessary to move beyond prejudice and towards oneness.
- Oneness is essential for breaking free from negative karma.

## Related Concepts
- Consciousness: A multifaceted and complex entity encompassing various aspects of human experience, including awareness and perception, and is essential for achieving a Beautiful State.
- Spirituality: The pursuit of spiritual growth, self-awareness, and connection with a higher power or the universe.
