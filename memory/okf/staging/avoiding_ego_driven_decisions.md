# Avoiding Ego-Driven Decisions

## Summary
A good leader avoids making decisions driven by ego, instead focusing on the present and future, ensuring safety and well-being, connecting with others, fostering unity, and caring for the environment.

## Key Teachings
- A quality of a good leader is avoiding ego-driven decisions. (Sri Preethaji)
