# Self-Realization and Purpose

## Summary
This teaching explores the profound transformation from a state of disconnection, judgment, and lack of motivation to a renewed sense of purpose, creative drive, and deep calm. It highlights the emergence of self-discovery as an internal awakening that reveals one's true nature and unique life mission, providing the driving force for meaningful existence.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Preethaji says... A profound transformation can occur where one moves from feeling disconnected and judgmental to experiencing deep calm, creative drive, and a renewed sense of purpose.
- Purpose is the unique calling or mission that emerges when one awakens to a state of connection, such as celebrating existence itself.
- Self-discovery reveals the awakening of the soul, indicating that the most profound knowledge is not external but an internal transformation.

## Quotes
> "Purpose is the answer to why one should work hard, endure challenges, and keep finding solutions in life." — Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Purpose: The unique calling or mission that emerges when one awakens to a state of connection, providing the driving force for meaningful existence and innovation.
- Self-Discovery: The journey of realizing one's true nature and connecting with a higher truth, which is an internal transformation rather than external knowledge.
- Limitless Field: A concept representing 'boundlessness,' associated with the teachings of Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji.
