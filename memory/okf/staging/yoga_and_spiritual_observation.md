# Yoga and Spiritual Observation

## Summary
Yoga is a profound practice that transcends mere physical exercise. It should be approached with reverence and intention, not as a means to achieve superficial physical changes or physical perfection. Yoga is a journey of self-discovery and transformation, leading to the ultimate goal of achieving Samadhi, or the absolute dissolution of ignorance and separation. This journey begins with correcting irregular breathing patterns and culminates in the absolute dissolution of ignorance and separation.

## Key Teachings
- **Spiritual Observation**: Yoga is not just about physical postures; it is a practice that involves observing the world around you, leading to direct, intuitive experiences like mystic visions. This practice is distinct from mere noticing or witnessing, as it involves a deliberate and critical examination of one's environment and surroundings.
