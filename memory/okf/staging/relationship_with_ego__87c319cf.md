# Relationship with EGO

## Summary
The third langanam, fasting hurtful and pessimistic speech, is discussed in the context of enhancing one's vaksakti and bringing about positive thoughts and a break from negative thinking.

## Key Teachings
- The third langanam, fasting hurtful and pessimistic speech, is discussed in the context of enhancing one's vaksakti and bringing about positive thoughts and a break from negative thinking.

## Quotes
> "Fasting hurtful and pessimistic speech is a very important langanam. It is important to break away from these negative thoughts and speak positively."

## Related Concepts
- **Vaksakti**: The ability to speak and express oneself effectively.
- **Negative Childhood Impressions**: These are negative experiences or thoughts that can manifest as negative behaviors or attitudes.
- **Limitless Field**: In spiritual teachings, the concept of "limitless field" represents the idea of boundlessness, associated with the teachings of Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji, where it is described as a state of being free from limitations.
