# Nature of Suffering

## Summary
In the context of celebrating Ugadi or Gudi Padwa, Sri Preethaji emphasizes the cyclical nature of life, birth, death, and the movement between order and disorder. He highlights the importance of accepting all aspects of life's experiences, including the emotional pain of suffering, as a reminder of the essence of Ugadi or Gudi Padwa.

## Key Teachings
- **Acceptance of Suffering**: Sri Preethaji emphasizes the acceptance of all aspects of life's experiences, including birth, death, and the movement between order and disorder, as a reminder of the essence of Ugadi or Gudi Padwa.

## Quotes
> "Acceptance of all aspects of life's experiences, including the emotional pain of suffering, as a reminder of the essence of Ugadi or Gudi Padwa."

## Related Concepts
- **Suffering States**: Emotional states such as regret, sadness, and loneliness that disconnect individuals from life. These states can be emotionally painful and disconnect individuals from the universal intelligence and its power.
