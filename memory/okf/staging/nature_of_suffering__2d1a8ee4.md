## Summary
The speaker discusses the phenomenon of division and separation stemming from ignorance, emphasizing how the more ignorant one becomes, the more they engage in dividing themselves from others, leading to feelings of superiority and the loss of kindness, respect, and value for fellow humans. The speaker also mentions the concept of the "Limitless Field," which represents the idea of boundlessness and the teachings of Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji, where it is described as a state of being that transcends limitations and suffering.
