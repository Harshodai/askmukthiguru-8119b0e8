# Observation and Change

Observation and Change

The teachings emphasize the critical role of parents and educators in nurturing children's emotional well-being. They highlight the importance of a loving and attentive presence, which is essential for the future of the planet and the awakening of consciousness. Children need your loving presence, and they need your attention. If you are a parent, do you really feel your child? This question underscores the significance of ensuring children feel seen and heard, especially in challenging situations. 

Key Teachings:
- The importance of a loving and attentive presence in nurturing children's emotional well-being.
- Ensuring children feel seen and heard, especially in challenging situations.

Quotes:
> "If you are a parent, do you really feel your child?" — Sri Preethaji

Related Concepts:
- Presence: The concept of being present and attentive towards others.
- Prosperity: The awakening of consciousness and the creation of wealth and prosperity through the power of consciousness.
