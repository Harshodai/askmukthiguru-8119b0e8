# The Nature of Suffering Caused by Environmental Destruction

## Summary
Human activities have led to significant environmental damage, including massive deforestation and the extinction of numerous vertebrate species. This destruction highlights a form of suffering inflicted upon the planet and its inhabitants.

## Key Teachings
- Humanity has caused the deforestation of 50% of rainforests ("Unknown Channel says...").
- Approximately 59% of Earth's vertebrates have gone extinct in the last three decades due to human actions ("Unknown Channel says...").

## Quotes
> "We have caused deforestation of 50% of the rainforests. We have made almost 59% of the vertebrates on Earth go extinct, just in the last three decades." — Unknown Channel

## Related Concepts
- Suffering States: emotionally painful states of being that disconnect individuals from the universal intelligence and its power.
- Pain: a feeling of suffering or distress caused by physical or emotional harm.
- Chaos: a state of disorder, confusion, or randomness.
