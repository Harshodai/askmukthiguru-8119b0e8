# The Nature of Suffering

## Summary
In this teaching, Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji explore the nature of suffering, emphasizing the importance of understanding it as a part of the human experience. They discuss the prevalence of depression in women and suggest that ageless beauty is attainable through empowerment and self-acceptance. The speakers emphasize the need to recognize suffering as a natural part of life and to find ways to cope with it effectively.

## Key Teachings
- **Sri Preethaji says:** "Suffering is a natural part of life, and it is important to recognize and accept it as such."
- **Sri Krishnaji says:** "The beauty of age is not in its physical appearance but in the wisdom and experiences that come with it."

## Quotes
> "Sri Preethaji says, 'Suffering is a natural part of life, and it is important to recognize and accept it as such.'"

## Related Concepts
- **Ageless Beauty:** The concept of ageless beauty, which is attainable through empowerment and self-acceptance.
- **Empowerment:** The importance of empowerment in overcoming suffering and achieving ageless beauty.
- **Self-acceptance:** The need to accept one's imperfections and embrace oneself to attain ageless beauty.
