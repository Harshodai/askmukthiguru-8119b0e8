# Karma and Suffering

## Summary
Karma is often perceived as problems in life, but it is understood as manifesting through one's consciousness and actions. The state of one's consciousness and actions determine their life, and spreading hurt or anger will lead to problems related to those emotions.

## Key Teachings
- Karma is often seen as problems coming into one's life ("We see karma as problems coming to our life."). — Unknown Channel
- One's consciousness and actions determine their life ("you must understand that karma here, f"). — Unknown Channel
