# Abundance Mindset Teachings

## Summary
The topic of abundance mindset teachings focuses on the shift from a mindset of scarcity to one of abundance. This shift is facilitated by a conscious and enlightened state of mind, which attracts positive energy and prosperity. The teachings emphasize the importance of cultivating a peaceful and content consciousness, which is the opposite of a complaining mind that invites misfortune.

## Key Teachings
- Cultivating a peaceful and content consciousness invites abundance and prosperity.
- A complaining mind creates a negative energy field, attracting misfortunes.
- An enlightened state is peaceful and content, attracting positive energy and abundance.

## Quotes
> "An enlightened state is peaceful and content, attracting positive energy and abundance."

## Related Concepts
- **Consciousness-Based View**: A view that wealth and prosperity are influenced by the state of one's consciousness, emphasizing the importance of a peaceful and content mind.
- **Abundance Consciousness**: A mindset that focuses on prosperity rather than scarcity, characterized by a sense of void and fullness.
- **Consciousness**: The interconnected state that binds individuals together, influencing each other's destinies and characterized by awareness of thoughts, emotions, and surroundings.
- **Awakened Child**: A child with a brain and nervous system attuned to peace, naturally feeling connection and love, and experiencing a deep sense of Oneness.
- **Divine Drama**: The unfolding of the mystical planes of consciousness before a child is born into the world.
- **Conscious Parent**: A parent who is aware of the divine drama that unfolds during conception and pregnancy, and can invite a great consciousness to flow into their world.
- **Ekam Academy**: A philosophy and meditation school founded by Sri Preethaji and her husband for transforming human consciousness.
- **Conscious Leadership**: A leadership approach that involves mentoring leaders in conscious leadership and transforming human consciousness.
