# Understanding Confusion and Conflict in Parenting and Childhood

## Summary
This teaching highlights that the phase of parenting and child development can be difficult and confusing for both parents and children.

## Key Teachings
- This phase of parenting can be difficult and confusing for most parents. (Sri Preethaji)
- This phase is also a time of confusion and conflict for children. (Sri Krishnaji)

## Quotes
> "This phase can be difficult. It can be confusing for most parents. It is also a phase of confusion and conflict for children too." — Sri Preethaji
