# Realizing Oneness and the Illusion of Separation

## Summary
Enlightenment involves transcending the illusion of separation and coming to the realization of one's fundamental oneness with all of existence.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Preethaji teaches that enlightenment is the dissolving of the illusion of separation.
- It is the direct realization of oneness with all.

## Quotes
> "...the illusion of separation and it is realizing the one is with all." — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- Oneness: The realization that one is not separate from all of existence.
