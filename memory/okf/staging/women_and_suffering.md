# Women and Suffering

# Title

## Summary
In a Beautiful State, women are in their natural state, manifesting the four powers of female consciousness, which in India are called the four goddesses. These powers include Shakti, the power to create, infuse life, and move static and lifeless things to make them happen. In a Beautiful State, women are able to inspire others and maintain abiding calm. When they move into a Suffering State, their Shakti becomes distorted, leading to aggression and control.
