# Evolving Beyond Rigid Views

## Summary
This reflection highlights the importance of moving beyond rigid views, particularly in relationships, to foster deeper connection and love. It suggests that such an understanding can prompt individuals to transcend their ego-driven perspectives.

## Key Teachings
- An understanding can spur individuals to go beyond their rigid views. (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji)

## Related Concepts
- Ego: Ego is a state of self-centeredness and separation that can lead to conflict. It is the sense of self that separates one from others and the world.
