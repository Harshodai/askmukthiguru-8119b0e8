# Conscious Wealth Creation and the Ego

## Summary
Conscious wealth creators approach wealth and success from a "Beautiful State of connection," rather than from a place of disconnection and anxiety. This implies a shift in one's internal state and relationship with the ego when pursuing material success.

## Key Teachings
- Conscious wealth creators pursue wealth and success from a "Beautiful State of connection" (Unknown Channel).
- This approach contrasts with pursuing wealth from "disconnection and anxiety" (Unknown Channel).

## Related Concepts
- Abundance: a state of being that encompasses a harmonious existence, interconnectedness with others, and the creation of wealth and prosperity through the power of consciousness.
