# Suffering and Bliss in Consciousness

# Suffering and Bliss in Consciousness

## Summary
This teaching suggests that every experience within one's consciousness, both mental and sensory, can be perceived as bliss when one is in an enlightened state.

## Key Teachings
- "Every experience in your consciousness, mental experiences and sensory experiences, is bliss in the state." — Unknown Channel

## Related Concepts
- Ananda: Ananda is a state of bliss and happiness that is often experienced in spiritual practices.
- Enlightenment: Enlightenment is a state of profound understanding characterized by a state of consciousness free from suffering, with intense awareness and no compulsive attachment to the past or future.
