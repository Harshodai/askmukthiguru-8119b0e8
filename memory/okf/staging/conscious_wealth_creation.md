# Conscious Wealth Creation

## Summary
Conscious wealth creation involves shifting from a disconnected and anxious approach to wealth to a connected state. This leads to extraordinary leadership and a beneficial impact on the ecosystem by nurturing a purpose larger than self-interests.

## Key Teachings
- Conscious wealth creators pursue wealth and success from a Beautiful State of connection, rather than from disconnection and anxiety. (Unknown speaker)
- This approach leads to extraordinary leadership that benefits the ecosystem. (Unknown speaker)
- It involves nurturing a purpose larger than self-interests and focusing on creating a beneficial impact on the ecosystem. (Unknown speaker)

## Related Concepts
- Abundance: a state of being that encompasses a harmonious existence, interconnectedness with others, and the creation of wealth and prosperity through the power of consciousness.
