# Enlightenment as a State

# Enlightenment as a State

## Summary
Enlightenment is described as a state.

## Key Teachings
- Enlightenment is a state. (Ekam / O&O Academy says)

## Related Concepts
- Enlightenment: Enlightenment is a state of profound understanding, characterized by a state of consciousness where one is free of suffering, with intense awareness and no compulsive attachment to the past or future.
- Suffering States: Suffering States refer to emotional states such as regret, sadness, and loneliness that disconnect individuals from life.
