# Impact of Consciousness on Others

## Summary
This teaching highlights how an individual's consciousness can impact those around them, particularly loved ones. It suggests that as one's consciousness evolves, it influences the people in their life.

## Key Teachings
- Your consciousness impacts the people around you. (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji)
- Your consciousness impacts your loved ones. (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji)
