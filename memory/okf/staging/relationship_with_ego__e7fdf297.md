# Relationship with EGO

## Summary
A meditation sequence from O&O Academy that guides the practitioner to focus on the concept of "I am the limitless conscious" and visualize a unitary field of consciousness, followed by a gentle touch and a visualization of light spreading around them.

## Key Teachings
- The practice emphasizes focusing on the concept of "I am the limitless conscious" as a means to relate with the ego.
- It involves visualizing a unitary field of consciousness to enhance awareness and spiritual connection.
