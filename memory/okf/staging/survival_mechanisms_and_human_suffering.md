# Survival Mechanisms and Human Suffering

## Summary
Human consciousness has evolved through fear and survival instincts, leading to a state structured for psychological suffering, unlike animals which lack psychological obsession.

## Key Teachings
- Unlike humans, animals do not possess psychological obsession or the same patterns of mental suffering.
- Human consciousness has evolved through survival mechanisms and fear, networking itself in ways that generate suffering and creating a need for inner transformation.

## Quotes
> "They do not have obsession. They do not have all those things." — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Human Consciousness: Evolved through fear and survival instincts, requiring inner transformation.
