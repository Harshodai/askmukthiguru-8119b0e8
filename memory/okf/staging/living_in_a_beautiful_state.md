# Living in a Beautiful State

## Summary
The world requires a fundamental transformation in human consciousness rather than new religions or additional sets of rules to prevent heading toward a troubled world.

## Key Teachings
- Without internal transformation and a shift in consciousness, humanity is heading towards a troubled world.
- What the world needs today is not yet another religion or another set of codes and rules, but a change in state.
