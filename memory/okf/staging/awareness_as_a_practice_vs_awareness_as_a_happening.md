# Awareness as a Practice vs. Awareness as a Happening

## Summary
The journey towards spiritual growth and enlightenment involves a crucial distinction between awareness as a practice and awareness as a happening. Practicing awareness is a starting point, preparing an individual for the more profound experience of awareness as a happening, where Consciousness and awareness become indistinguishable.

## Key Teachings
- The distinction between awareness as a practice and awareness as a happening is crucial for spiritual growth and enlightenment.
- Awareness as a practice is the starting point for an individual's spiritual journey.
- Awareness as a happening is a more profound experience where there is no difference between Consciousness and awareness.
- The practice of awareness prepares an individual for the experience of awareness as a happening.

## Related Concepts
- moksha: A state of liberation or release from the cycle of rebirth, often associated with enlightenment.
- consciousness: The state of being aware of one's own existence and surroundings.
- enlightenment: A state of profound understanding characterized by a state of consciousness free from suffering, with intense awareness and no compulsive attachment to the past or future.
