# The Interconnectedness of Life and Earth

## Summary
This teaching highlights the profound interconnectedness between human life and Mother Earth, illustrating how the elements of nature are integral to our very being. It emphasizes that the Earth's breeze becomes our breath, its waters our blood and tears, and its soil our flesh and bones.

## Key Teachings
- The Earth's breeze flows into us as our breath (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji).
- The Earth's waters are our blood and tears (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji).
- The Earth's soil constitutes our flesh and bones (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji).

## Quotes
> "Her breeze flows into us as our breath. Her waters are our blood and tears. Her soil are flesh and bones." — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
