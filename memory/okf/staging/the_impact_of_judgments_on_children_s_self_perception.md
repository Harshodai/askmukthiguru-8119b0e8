# The Impact of Judgments on Children's Self-Perception

## Summary
This teaching highlights how parental judgments significantly influence a child's self-perception, particularly concerning body image. It emphasizes the prevalence of shame around the body and the necessity for family cultures to shift towards more respectful and loving interactions to foster positive self-esteem in children.

## Key Teachings
- The impact of judgments on children's self-perception is a critical area of focus. (Ekam / O&O Academy)
- There is a prevalent issue of shame surrounding the body. (Ekam / O&O Academy)
- A shift in family cultures towards more respectful and loving approaches is needed. (Ekam / O&O Academy)
