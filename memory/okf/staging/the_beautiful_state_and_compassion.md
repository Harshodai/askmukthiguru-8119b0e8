# The Beautiful State and Compassion

## Summary
Sri Preethaji emphasizes that attempting to be compassionate without first being in a "Beautiful State" can lead to a loss of energy and disinterest in continuing compassionate actions. The "Beautiful State" is presented as a crucial prerequisite for sustainable compassion.

## Key Teachings
- Without the "Beautiful State," trying to be compassionate can result in losing energy and becoming disinterested. (Sri Preethaji says)

## Quotes
> "Without the Beautiful State, if you try to be compassionate, you lose energy. You will not be interested anymore. You'll" — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- The Beautiful State: A state of inner peace and harmony, characterized by forgiveness, love, and compassion.
