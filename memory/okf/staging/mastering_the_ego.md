# Mastering the Ego

## Summary
Mastering the ego involves cultivating an inner "Beautiful State" of consciousness, which arises from seeing the truth of one's own being. This inner state of joy, calm, and truth naturally transforms external reality.

## Key Teachings
- True, lasting success and prosperity are achieved by aligning with an elevated consciousness rather than chasing external goals or fixing surface-level problems. (Sri Preethaji says...)
- A Beautiful State of consciousness automatically emerges when one sees the truth of their own being. (Sri Preethaji says...)

## Related Concepts
- Beautiful State: An inner state of joy, calm, and truth that arises from seeing the truth of one's being and transforms external reality effortlessly.
