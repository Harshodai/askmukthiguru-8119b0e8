# Nature of Suffering

## Summary
In this teaching, Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji explore the nature of suffering, emphasizing the importance of understanding it as a manifestation of the ego and the need to dissolve negative childhood impressions. They discuss the concept of "Ekam" as a powerful spiritual tool for transforming suffering into a transformative experience.

## Key Teachings
- **Understanding Suffering as Ego Manifestation:** The speakers emphasize that suffering is not an inherent part of the universe but a manifestation of the ego. They suggest that by dissolving negative childhood impressions, one can transform the experience of suffering into a transformative one.
- **Dissolving Negative Childhood Impressions:** The teachings encourage seekers to work on resolving negative childhood impressions, which are often the root cause of suffering. They provide techniques and practices to help individuals heal these impressions.

## Quotes
