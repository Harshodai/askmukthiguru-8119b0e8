# Enlightenment and Freedom from Suffering

## Summary
Enlightenment is described as a state of intense awareness where suffering is eliminated. In this state, thoughts exist as information without emotional charge, and one's focus shifts from the past and future to the present moment, leading to expansiveness and freedom from suffering.

## Key Teachings
- Enlightenment is free of suffering. (Unknown Channel)
- It is a state where you are free of suffering, or every tendency or any root that causes suffering. (Unknown Channel)

## Related Concepts
- Enlightenment (concept): Enlightenment is a state of profound understanding, characterized by a state of consciousness where one is free of suffering, with intense awareness and no compulsive attachment to the past or future.
- Suffering States (concept): Suffering States refer to emotional states such as regret, sadness, and loneliness that disconnect individuals from life.
- Pain (concept): Pain is a feeling of suffering or distress caused by physical or emotional harm.
