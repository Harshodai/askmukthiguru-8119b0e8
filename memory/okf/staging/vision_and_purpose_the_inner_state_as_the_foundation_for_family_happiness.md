# Vision and Purpose: The Inner State as the Foundation for Family Happiness

## Summary
An individual's inner state of consciousness directly impacts and transforms the family unit. Focusing inward on one's inner state is more powerful for creating a happy family than focusing on external achievements like career, wealth, or health.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Preethaji says that the inner state of an individual directly impacts and transforms the family unit.
- Focusing on external achievements such as career, wealth, or health is less powerful for creating a happy family than focusing inward on one's inner state of consciousness.

## Quotes
> "When an individual is" — Sri Preethaji
