# Relationship with EGO

## Summary
The third langanam, fasting hurtful and pessimistic speech, is discussed, emphasizing its importance in enhancing one's vaksakti and bringing about positive thoughts and a break from negative thinking.

## Key Teachings
- **Teaching Point 1:** The third langanam, fasting hurtful and pessimistic speech, is discussed, emphasizing its importance in enhancing one's vaksakti and bringing about positive thoughts and a break from negative thinking.

## Quotes
> "Fasting hurtful and pessimistic speech is a language that is not beneficial for the soul. It is important to speak positively and break away from negative thinking."

## Related Concepts
- **Concept Name:** Fasting Hurtful and Pessimistic Speech
  - **Description:** This concept refers to speech that is harmful and pessimistic, which is not beneficial for the soul. It is important to speak positively and break away from negative thinking.

This teaching is based on the teachings of Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji, emphasizing the importance of positive speech and a break from negative thinking.
