# Kundalini Awakening

## Summary
Kundalini is described as a mystical force present in every human being that can be awakened through various practices and experiences, including meditation. Its awakening is considered a divine grace that can lead to a profound understanding of the self and the universe.

## Key Teachings
- Kundalini is a mystical force that burns within every human being.
- Kundalini can be awakened through various practices and experiences, including meditation.
- The awakening of Kundalini is a divine grace.
- Kundalini awakening can lead practitioners to a profound understanding of the true nature of self and the universe.
- Kundalini can rise during meditation, practices, and experiences such as fasting, prayer, or in the presence of mystic powers.
- Kundalini can also rise during a crisis or in phases of extraordinary beauty.

## Related Concepts
- meditation: A practice through which Kundalini can be awakened.
