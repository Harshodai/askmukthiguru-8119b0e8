# Relationship with EGO

In the teachings of Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji, the relationship with the EGO is emphasized as a crucial aspect of spiritual growth and self-discovery. The speakers guide individuals to recognize and dissolve the EGO, leading to a deeper connection with the self and the universe. This process involves practices such as meditation, yoga, and the cultivation of a peaceful and loving consciousness. By aligning with the divine drama and the awakening of the child within, one can transcend the ego and experience a state of enlightenment, where the self is no longer bound by the limitations of the EGO. This journey is a transformative path towards a more harmonious and interconnected existence, where the individual is in harmony with the universe and others.
