# Relationship with EGO

## Summary
Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji point to the innate wisdom needed to dissolve separation and divisive tendencies. Drawing on the awakening of the four goddesses—Shakti, Amba, Kali, and Parvati—they describe how these forces can move between a Beautiful State and a Suffering State, highlighting that the full integration of feminine aspects is vital for women’s wholeness and for making a genuine contribution to the world.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji emphasize that we carry the wisdom to dissolve separation and divisive tendencies.
- The four goddesses—Shakti, Amba, Kali, and Parvati—symbolize different facets of femininity that can awaken in a Beautiful State or fall into Suffering States.
- Integrating all aspects of femininity is essential for inner wholeness and for the betterment of the world.

## Quotes
> "You have the wisdom to dissolve separation and divisive tende" — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Awakening: A transformative process of realizing one’s true nature and disidentifying from the illusion of separation and egoic conditioning.
- Spiritual Process: A journey of awakening and conscious evolution.
- Limitless Field: The notion of boundlessness in spiritual practice, associated with the teachings of Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji.
