# Self-Centricity versus Other-Centricity

# Self-Centricity versus Other-Centricity

## Summary
A teaching contrasting the self-centric nature of ambition with the transformative power of love and presence.

## Key Teachings
- Ambition is rooted in feeling one's superiority over others.

## Quotes
> "Ambition is all about feeling your superiority." — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Ambition: A self-centric drive focused on feeling superior to others.
- Love: A state of presence contrasted with self-centric ambition.
