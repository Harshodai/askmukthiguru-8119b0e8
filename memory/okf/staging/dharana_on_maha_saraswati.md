# Dharana on Maha Saraswati

## Summary
This teaching describes a dharana practice involving the visualization of Maha Saraswati in one's Chidakasha, accompanied by a mantra recitation, to invoke her blessings and wisdom.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji instruct participants to "close your eyes and do dharana upon her form in your Chidakasha or the dark sp" as part of a meditation practice.

## Quotes
> "Please close your eyes and do dharana upon her form in your Chidakasha or the dark sp" — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Chidakasha: A concept related to the space of consciousness, often visualized as a dark space.
- Dharana: A spiritual practice involving focused concentration or observation.
