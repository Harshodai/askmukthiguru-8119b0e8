# Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji: Unveiling the Relationship with EGO

# Topic: Relationship with EGO

## Summary
The teachings of Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji emphasize the importance of an unagitated consciousness, which is the seat of abundance and fortune, and how it leads to unexpected fulfillment and a path of least resistance in life. This is contrasted with a complaining mind that is rooted in judgment and dissatisfaction, which invites energies opposite to abundance.

## Key Teachings
- **Sri Preethaji says:** "An unagitated consciousness is the seat of abundance and fortune. It is rooted in judgment."
- **Sri Krishnaji says:** "A complaining mind is rooted in judgment. It invites energies opposite to abundance."
