# Love and Self-Transformation

In this teaching, Sri Preethaji emphasizes the transformative power of love in overcoming suffering and the importance of trust in experiencing and giving love. He highlights the difficulty in experiencing love compared to pain and other negative emotions, and the importance of trust in relationships and interactions with others. The speaker suggests that individuals are responsible for experiencing and giving love, and that it is a duty for them to transform and awaken to powerful states of love. This teaching is a significant step towards spiritual awakening and the development of a more harmonious and loving existence.
