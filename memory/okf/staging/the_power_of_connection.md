# The Power of Connection

## Summary
The ability to connect with others and feel their experience is presented as a transformative and miraculous experience, leading to healing rather than attempting to change the other person.

## Key Teachings
- What works in relationships is "your ability to connect" (Unknown Channel).
- "It would be a miraculous experience for you to feel the other and connect to the other" (Unknown Channel).

## Related Concepts
- Observation: Observation is the act of paying attention to and examining something.
