# Diwali Teachings: Inner Growth & Relationships

## Summary
Diwali, as celebrated in South India, is a festival that highlights the importance of reconnecting with loved ones and finding joy in the celebration of light. The story of Vishnu and Naraka is interpreted as a metaphor for the separation of human consciousness from nature and Mother Earth. The lighting of lamps on Diwali is believed to dispel this darkness and separation, fostering a return to connection with Mother Earth.

## Key Teachings
- Diwali emphasizes reconnecting with loved ones and finding joy in celebration.
- The story of Vishnu and Naraka is seen as a metaphor for the separation of human consciousness from nature and Mother Earth.
- The lighting of lamps on Diwali is believed to dispel darkness and separation in consciousness.
- Lighting lamps helps bring consciousness back to its connection with Mother Earth.
