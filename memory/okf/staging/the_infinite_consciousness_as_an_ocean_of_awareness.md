# The Infinite Consciousness as an Ocean of Awareness

## Summary
The infinite consciousness is likened to an ocean, where every life form is a wave of subjective awareness that rises and subsides within this vastness.

## Key Teachings
- The infinite consciousness is an ocean. (Unknown Channel says)
- Every life form is a wave of subjective awareness. (Unknown Channel says)
- These waves of awareness rise and subside within the vast ocean of infinite consciousness. (Unknown Channel says)

## Quotes
> "The infinite consciousness is the ocean, in which every life form is but a wave of subjective awareness that rises and subsides." — Unknown Channel

## Related Concepts
- Suffering States: emotional states such as regret, sadness, and loneliness that disconnect individuals from life.
