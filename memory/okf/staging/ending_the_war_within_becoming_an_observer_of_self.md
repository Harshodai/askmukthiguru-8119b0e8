# Ending the War Within: Becoming an Observer of Self

## Summary
This teaching describes the process of transforming inner conflict, where one becomes their own enemy, into a state of calm and wholeness by becoming an observer of one's inner state. This shift makes internal commentaries redundant and leads to a healed self.

## Key Teachings
- To end the internal war within oneself means to become an observer of oneself. (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji)

## Quotes
> "You become your own enemy. To end this war within yourself means to become an observer of you" — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- inner conflict: a state where one feels at war with oneself.
- self-observation: the act of becoming an observer of one's own inner state.
