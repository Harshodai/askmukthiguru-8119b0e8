# Conscious Smiling as a Spiritual Practice

# Conscious Smiling as a Spiritual Practice

## Summary
A brief guided exercise incorporating conscious smiling with exhalation to involve the lips and cheeks in a meditative breath practice.

## Key Practice Steps
- **Breathing and Smiling**: Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji guide a practice of consciously smiling as you exhale for three breaths.
- **Involving the Face**: With each exhalation, bring a smile to your lips and allow your cheeks to join in the smile as you breathe out.

## Quotes
> "For the next three breaths, we will consciously smile as we exhale. Inhale. As you exhale, smile along with your lips. Allow your cheeks to join the smile. Inhale." — Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji
