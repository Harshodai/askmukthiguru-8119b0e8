# Dissolving Negative Thoughts in the Limitless Field

## Summary
The limitless field is a spiritual space where work is done on the mind to dissolve negative thoughts and beliefs that create problems and obstacles in one's life.

## Key Teachings
- In the limitless field, work is done on your mind. (Ekam / O&O Academy says)
- Negative thoughts and beliefs that create problems and obstacles are dissolved. (Ekam / O&O Academy says)

## Quotes
> "In the limitless field, we work on your mind and dissolve negative thoughts and beliefs that create problems and obstacles." — Ekam / O&O Academy

## Related Concepts
- Limitless Field: a concept in spiritual teachings, representing the idea of 'boundlessness,' associated with the teachings of Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji.
