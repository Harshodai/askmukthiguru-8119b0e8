# The Habit of Incessant Commentary

## Summary
Sri Preethaji describes how an incessant habit of commentary, which manifests as self-criticism and judgment of various aspects of one's life, is a central root cause of depression. This constant inner dialogue creates inner conflict and a feeling of being at war with oneself.

## Key Teachings
- The habit of incessant commentary drives people into inner conflict and a state of war with themselves (Sri Preethaji says).
- This commentary often manifests as self-criticism and judgment concerning one's appearance, family, home, and work (Sri Preethaji says).
- This habit of commentary is central to the root cause of depression (Sri Preethaji says).

## Related Concepts
- The Power of Observation: The ability to observe and understand the world around us, which can help in recognizing and addressing the habit of incessant commentary.
