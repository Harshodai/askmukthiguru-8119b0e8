# The Body's States: Survival vs. Thriving

## Summary
The human body operates in two primary states: survival and thriving. In the survival state, the body prioritizes immediate threats over functions like digestion or reproduction. To transition to a thriving state, where the body naturally restores its intelligent balance and health, peace is crucial.

## Key Teachings
- In the survival state, the body does not prioritize functions like the digestive or reproductive systems ("In survival state your body does not care for how good your digestive system is or how good your reproductive system is. All it wa") — Ekam / O&O Academy
