# Swadharma and its Relationship with Ego

## Summary
Swadharma, or one's true path, is central to creating positive change in society and the environment. It requires deep self-knowledge and alignment with one's true calling. Swadharma is not driven by self-centeredness, money, or external validation, which are often associated with the ego. Instead, it inspires others and leads to well-being and purpose.

## Key Teachings
- Swadharma is one's true path, aligning with core nature and passion, and is essential for creating social, political, cultural, and environmental change. (Unknown speaker)
- To discover swadharma, one must deeply and truly know oneself and align with one's true calling. (Unknown speaker)
- Swadharma is not self-centered, nor is it pursued for money or external validation. (Unknown speaker)
- Swadharma is a path that inspires others and the earth, leading to well-being and a sense of purpose. (Unknown speaker)

## Related Concepts
- Ego: Ego is a state of self-centeredness and separation that can lead to conflict and violence. It is also a self-centered attitude that can influence a leader to make decisions that benefit themselves alone. Ego is a concept referring to the sense of self, identity, and the tendency to identify with thoughts and emotions. In Hindu philosophy, it represents the egoistic nature of the self.
