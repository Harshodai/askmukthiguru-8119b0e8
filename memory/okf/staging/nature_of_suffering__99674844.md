# Nature of Suffering

# Nature of Suffering

## Summary
The nature of suffering is often a result of division and separation stemming from ignorance. The more ignorant one becomes, the more they engage in dividing themselves from others, leading to feelings of superiority and the loss of kindness, respect, and value for fellow humans. This division can manifest as judgment, leading to a complaining mind and fault-finding, ultimately causing suffering and separation.

## Key Teachings
- **Sri Preethaji says:** "The more ignorant you are, the more you divide."
- **Sri Krishnaji says:** "The more ignorant you are, the more you divide."

## Quotes
> "The more ignorant you are, the more you divide."
— Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- **Division and Separation:** A root cause of suffering, leading to judgment and a complaining mind.
- **Judgment:** A belief or opinion based on limited or biased information, leading to suffering and separation.
- **Complaining Mind:** A manifestation of judgment, causing suffering and separation.
- **Fault-Finding:** A manifestation of judgment, leading to suffering and separation.
