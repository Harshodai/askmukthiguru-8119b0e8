# Shared Earth and Observation

# Shared Earth and Observation

## Summary
The Earth is shared with millions of other species, many of whom arrived before humans. This implies a need to acknowledge and respect their presence and habitats.

## Key Teachings
- We share the Earth with millions of other species. (Unknown Channel)
- Many species have been on Earth much longer than humans. (Unknown Channel)

## Related Concepts
- The Power of Observation: The ability to observe and understand the world around us.
- Observation: A practice that involves simply noticing and acknowledging one's thoughts and emotions without judgment; the act of paying attention to and examining something, and also the practice of Observation and Spiritual Insight, which allows one to perceive beyond the senses.
