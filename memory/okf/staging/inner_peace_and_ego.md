# Inner Peace and EGO

## Summary
True inner peace is not merely the absence of thought or a passive virtue, but a profound state of self-ease and awakened consciousness. Rather than viewing the mind as a battleground of conflicting desires, this state represents total clarity where one is fully at ease with oneself. This spiritual liberation is symbolized by Ravana severing his heads to destroy his nine inner demons of ego, pride, arrogance, and craving.

## Key Teachings
- True inner peace is a profound state of self-ease and awakened consciousness, serving as a great divine healing gift.
- The state represents total clarity where one is fully at ease with oneself, rather than viewing the mind as a battleground of conflicting desires.
- Spiritual liberation is symbolized by Ravana severing his heads to destroy his nine inner demons of ego, pride, arrogance, and craving.

## Related Concepts
- Inner Peace: A state of inner tranquility and calm within oneself, often associated with meditation, spiritual practices, and the nature of the mind and consciousness. It is described as a state of being that must be found within oneself to achieve happiness and abundance, and is a state of ease with oneself.
- Enlightenment: A profound state of spiritual awakening and the ultimate goal of the human spiritual journey, characterized by a consciousness free from suffering, intense awareness, and liberation.
- I-Consciousness: The sense of self, which can be experienced as separate and disconnected or expanded to include others.
- Consciousness: The awareness of one's own existence and the ability to perceive the world and oneself, described as the most amazing aspect of the human body as it allows the body to heal itself.
