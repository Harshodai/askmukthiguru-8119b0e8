# Nature of Suffering

## Summary
The topic discusses the nature of suffering, emphasizing that it is not about being bold but about being what one has never been before. The speaker highlights the prevalence of failure in everyone's life and the importance of purging failure impressions to prevent them from becoming patterns that hinder personal growth and happiness.

## Key Teachings
- **Sri Preethaji says:** "The nature of suffering is not about being bold but about being what one has never been before. It is not about being fearless but about being courageous."

## Quotes
> "The nature of suffering is not about being bold but about being what one has never been before."

## Related Concepts
- **Concept Name:** Suffering States
  - **Description:** Suffering States refer to emotional states such as regret, sadness, and loneliness that disconnect individuals from life. It is a concept related to the teachings of Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji, representing the idea of emotionally painful states of being that disconnect individuals from the universal intelligence and its power.
