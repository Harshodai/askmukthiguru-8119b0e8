# Relationship with EGO

### Relationship with EGO

The manifestation of failure patterns in the physical, mind, and energy bodies is a common manifestation of negative childhood impressions. This failure impression can manifest as a recurring pattern of failure, leading to a lack of confidence and a tendency to avoid challenges. The spiritual courage to manifest's role in liberating individuals from these patterns and achieving success is crucial.

### Key Teachings

- **Manifest's Role in Liberating from Failure Patterns:** Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji emphasize the importance of purging these negative impressions to prevent them from recurring and manifesting as failure. This spiritual courage is essential for breaking the jinx of failure patterns and achieving success.
