# Understanding Sadness versus Depression

## Summary
Key signs that sadness may have transitioned into depression include feeling stuck in negative thought loops and experiencing existential questioning, such as asking why one is existing or why things are happening.

## Key Teachings
- Signs of depression settling in include persistent existential thoughts and questioning one's existence, such as asking "Why is this happening?" and "Why am I existing?"
- Feeling stuck in negative thought loops distinguishes clinical depression from ordinary sadness.

## Quotes
> "Why is this happening? Why am I existing?" — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Depression vs. Sadness: Recognizing existential questioning and negative thought loops as indicators of depression.
