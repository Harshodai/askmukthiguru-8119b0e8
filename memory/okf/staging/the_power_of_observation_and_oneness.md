# The Power of Observation and Oneness

# The Power of Observation and Oneness

## Summary
This teaching highlights how moving away from oneness occurs when one begins to perceive others as separate or different.

## Key Teachings
- "You move away from oneness when you begin to see the other as" — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- Consciousness: the state of being aware of one's thoughts, emotions, and surroundings, and the interconnected state that binds individuals together.
- Vasudhaiva Kutumakam: a theme emphasizing unity and oneness among nations.
