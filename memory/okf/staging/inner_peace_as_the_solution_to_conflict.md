# Inner Peace as the Solution to Conflict

## Summary
The speaker emphasizes that inner peace is the only true solution to conflicts between communities, races, and individuals, suggesting that it can resolve division and hate. It is ironic that other solutions are sought when peace itself is the answer.

## Key Teachings
- The speaker emphasizes the power of inner peace in resolving conflicts between communities, races, and individuals.
- Inner peace is suggested as the only true solution to problems of division and hate.
- If communities and races are divided and the flames of hate and violence burn, all they need is peace to solve their differences.
- It is ironic that other solutions are sought for these problems, rather than awakening to inner peace.
