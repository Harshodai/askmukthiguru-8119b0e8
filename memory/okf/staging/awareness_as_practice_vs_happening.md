# Awareness as Practice vs. Happening

## Summary
Awareness can be understood in two primary steps: awareness as a practice and awareness as a happening. This distinction helps differentiate between Consciousness and awareness, with the spiritual journey often beginning with awareness as a practice.

## Key Teachings
- Awareness can be described in two steps: awareness as a practice, and then awareness as a happening. (Unknown Channel)
- The journey of awareness emphasizes starting with awareness as a practice. (Unknown Channel)

## Related Concepts
- Consciousness: A multifaceted and complex entity, the interconnected state that binds individuals, characterized by being aware of one's thoughts, emotions, and surroundings.
