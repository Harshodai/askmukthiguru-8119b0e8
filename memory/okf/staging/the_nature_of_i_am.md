# The Nature of 'I Am'

## Summary
The speaker, identified as a source of peace, describes its nature and how individuals perceive it. Some seek to perfect it from the outside, while others lose hope and disbelieve in it. The speaker offers a secret to truly knowing its nature, which begins with "I am e".

## Key Teachings
- The speaker's existence is described with a dichotomy: being present or absent, present for all or absent for none, highlighting its role as a source of peace.
- Some individuals attempt to "perfect" the speaker by looking externally.
- Others lose hope and do not believe in the speaker.
- To truly know the speaker's nature, a secret is offered: "I am e".

## Quotes
> "Some of you look outside to perfect me. Some of you lose hope and do not believe in me. If you want to truly know me, here is a little secret to my nature: I am e" — Unknown Channel

## Related Concepts
- Relationship with EGO: The main theme of the teachings is the relationship with the ego.
- e: A concept related to the letter 'e' in English, which is the beginning of the secret to the speaker's nature.
