# Ending Toxic Relationships

### Ending Toxic Relationships

In a relationship, both partners must strive for personal growth and emotional transformation. If one partner remains resistant to change, the relationship should be considered for ending in peace. Flowering of the heart, which involves loving, caring, and being transformed by the other, is impossible. Therefore, if one partner is not working towards transformation, the relationship should be ended in peace. Both partners must commit to personal growth and emotional transformation, and if one partner is not willing to work towards it, the relationship should be ended in peace. This process is essential for the growth and well-being of both individuals involved in the relationship.
