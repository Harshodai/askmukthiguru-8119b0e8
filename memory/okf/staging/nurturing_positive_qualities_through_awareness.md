# Nurturing Positive Qualities Through Awareness

## Summary
This teaching emphasizes the importance of cultivating positive qualities in children, such as gratitude, compassion, kindness, joyfulness, and courage. It highlights that these qualities should be fostered through awareness, acceptance, and respect, rather than being imposed as mere values or virtues.

## Key Teachings
- The cultivation of positive qualities like gratitude, compassion, kindness, joyfulness, and courage in children is important (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji).
- These qualities should be nurtured through awareness, acceptance, and respect (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji).
- It is crucial to avoid imposing these as mere values or virtues (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji).
