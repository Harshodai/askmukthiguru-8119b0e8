# Transcending the EGO

## Summary
The teachings of Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji emphasize that transcending the ego is essential for moving from a Suffering State to a Beautiful State. Suffering arises from self-obsession, which disconnects us from ourselves and the world. By cultivating self-awareness and dissolving this self-obsession, one can access inner calm, joy, and clarity.

## Key Teachings
- Suffering is fundamentally rooted in self-obsession, which severs our connection to ourselves and the world (Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji).
- The "Beautiful State" emerges when one is not engrossed with oneself, leading to inner calm, joy, and connection (Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji).
- Overcoming suffering is a prerequisite for accessing true intelligence and effective problem-solving (Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji).

## Related Concepts
- Beautiful State: A state of inner calm, joy, and connection that arises when one transcends self-obsession.
- Suffering State: A state defined by self-obsession, leading to inner turmoil and disconnection.
