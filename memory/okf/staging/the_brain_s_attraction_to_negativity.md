# The Brain's Attraction to Negativity

## Summary
The human brain has a strong attraction to negative stimuli, which plays a significant role in shaping mental health. Shifting focus from the amygdala to the prefrontal cortex is crucial for improving mental well-being and fostering practical thinking.
