# Relationship with EGO

## Summary
This teaching explores the relationship with the ego, emphasizing that true intelligence and effective action emerge from a state of inner fulfillment and universal connection rather than from rigid, self-obsessed ideals. It contrasts mechanical, habitual actions with spontaneous, spiritually connected actions, highlighting the need to dissolve states of suffering to access the intelligence required to solve life's challenges.

## Key Teachings
- Ideals are mechanical and hinder true spiritual action. (Unknown Channel)
- Acting from states of suffering prevents access to the intelligence needed to solve life's challenges. (Unknown Channel)
- True, effective action arises from inner fulfillment and connection rather than habitual, self-obsessed ideals. (Unknown Channel)

## Quotes

## Related Concepts

- Suffering State: A state of self-obsession that hinders true spiritual action and intelligence.
