# Yoga and Spiritual Healing

## Summary
Yoga transcends physical fitness, embracing spiritual practices aimed at achieving enlightenment and union with the divine. It emphasizes the importance of breath control (pranayama) and ultimately leads to a state of blissful union. This practice is distinct from other forms of meditation or spiritual practices, focusing on the integration of physical, mental, and spiritual aspects to achieve a deeper connection with the divine. To effectively integrate yoga practices into daily life, one should follow the steps outlined in the "Soul Sync Meditation" series.

## Key Teachings
- **Soul Sync Meditation Step 3**: This step involves integrating the breath control practices from the previous steps into daily life, leading to a deeper spiritual connection and enlightenment.
