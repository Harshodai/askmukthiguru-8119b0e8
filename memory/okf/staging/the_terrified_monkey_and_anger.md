# The Terrified Monkey and Anger

## Summary
Sri Preethaji uses the metaphor of a terrified monkey to illustrate the nature of anger. Just as a monkey, once terrified, does not easily return to the village, an angry mind holds onto its anger. This metaphor highlights the lasting impact of anger and the difficulty in letting it go.

## Key Teachings
- The experience of anger can be likened to a terrified monkey that does not easily return to its normal state or environment (Sri Preethaji says).
- Holding onto anger has consequences, similar to the monkey's reluctance to return to the village (Sri Preethaji says).

## Quotes
> "The terrified monkey does not easily come back into the village after that. That is how it is with anger. You hold on to your a" — Sri Preethaji
