# The Root Cause of Division and the Sickness of the Human Mind

## Summary
Sri Preethaji discusses how the educational system and career aspirations contribute to division in society. This division has become more pronounced with advancements in civilization and science, leading to an excessive focus on uniqueness and individuality, which ultimately creates a "sickness in the human mind."

## Key Teachings
- The root cause of division in society is deeply rooted in the educational system and career aspirations (Sri Preethaji says).
- This division has become more pronounced with advancements in civilization and science (Sri Preethaji says).
- An excessive focus on uniqueness and individuality leads to a "sickness in the human mind" (Sri Preethaji says).
