# Relationship with EGO

# Topic: Relationship with EGO

## Summary
The speaker emphasizes that individuals are responsible for experiencing and giving love, as evidenced by scientific research on heartbreak and the impact of love on the brain. It is highlighted that the lack of a state of love is the reason for responsibility.

## Key Teachings
- Individuals are responsible for experiencing and giving love.
- The reason for responsibility is the lack of a state of love.

## Quotes
> "It is also due to the lack of a state of love."

## Related Concepts
- Love: A feeling of affection, admiration, and fondness for someone or something.
- Ego: The part of the self that is identified with the body and the desires and needs of the body.
- Transformation: The process of changing or improving oneself by changing one's beliefs, attitudes, or behaviors.
- Love: The feeling of affection, admiration, and fondness for someone or something.
