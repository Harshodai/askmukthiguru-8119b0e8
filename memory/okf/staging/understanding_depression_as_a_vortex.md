# Understanding Depression as a Vortex

## Summary
When life challenges lead to depression, it acts as a deep vortex that pulls an individual in, making it difficult to find a way out and leading to a loss of interest in life.

## Key Teachings
- Depression acts as a deep vortex that sucks a person in, leaving them feeling unable to find a way out.
- Unlike ordinary sadness, it induces a state where one experiences a loss of interest and deep emotional entanglement.

## Quotes
> "It is a vortex that has sucked you in so deeply that you really do not know how to get out of it." — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- Depression: A state described as an inescapable vortex impacting an individual's interest in life and ability to find a way out.
