# The Four Dimensions of the Human Mind

## Summary
Ancient sages recognized the human mind as having four dimensions: the thinking mind (manas), the sense of ego (ahankara), awareness (chitta), and the ultimate state of wisdom (vijnana). Enlightenment is understood as a state where these dimensions are transcended, leading to wisdom and intelligent decision-making.

## Key Teachings
- The human mind has four dimensions: the thinking mind (manas), the sense of ego (ahankara), awareness (chitta), and the ultimate state of wisdom (vijnana).
- The thinking mind (manas) is characterized by constant trains of thoughts and emotions.
- The sense of ego (ahankara) identifies and owns thoughts and emotions.
- Enlightenment is a state where these four dimensions are transcended, resulting in wisdom and intelligent decision-making.

## Related Concepts
- Enlightenment: A state of profound understanding, characterized by a state of consciousness where one is free of suffering, with intense awareness and no compulsive attachment to the past or future.
- Consciousness: The state of being aware of one's thoughts, emotions, and surroundings, and a multifaceted and complex entity that encompasses various aspects of human experience.
