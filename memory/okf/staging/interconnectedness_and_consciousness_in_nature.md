# Interconnectedness and Consciousness in Nature

# Interconnectedness and Consciousness in Nature

## Summary
A brief reflection on human evolution toward a connected state and how animals, such as elephants, experience suffering to a certain extent.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji point out that human evolution involves living in a connected state.
- While psychological suffering is distinct in humans, animals do experience suffering to some extent, with elephants noted as an example.

## Quotes
> "We might just be people who are just living in a connected state. As far as animals go, they do have suffering to some extent, especially elephants." — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Connected state: A state of consciousness in human living contrasted with psychological suffering.
