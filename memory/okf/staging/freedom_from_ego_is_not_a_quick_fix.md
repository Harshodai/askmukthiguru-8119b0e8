# Freedom from Ego is Not a Quick Fix

## Summary
Freedom, particularly from the grip of the ego, is not something achieved in a day or even a week. It requires continuous effort and is a process that unfolds over time.

## Key Teachings
- Freedom is not a one-day affair, or weeks affair. (Sri Krishnaji says)

## Quotes
> "Lastly, freedom is not a one-days affair, or weeks affair." — Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Relationship with EGO: The main theme of the teachings is the relationship with the ego.
- EGO and Suffering: The relationship between the ego and suffering.
