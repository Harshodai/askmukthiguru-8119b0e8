# Dignity and Spiritual Power of Women

## Summary
Achieving world peace requires dissolving the violence experienced towards women, which is only possible when we recognize their spiritual power and dignity. Women are described as half of humanity, creators who birth the future, and possess immeasurable spiritual power to influence families, organizations, and nations.

## Key Teachings
- We can contribute towards world peace only when we dissolve the violence that we experience towards women.
- Women are described as half of humanity who have endured violence, injustice, and inhumanity, and possess immeasurable spiritual power.
- Dignity is described as something women are crying for.
- Women are described as half of humanity, creators who birth the future, and a group needing collective awakening for peace and power.
- Spiritual power is described as immeasurable in women, enabling them to influence families, organizations, and nations.
