# The Mind's Dimensions and Enlightenment

## Summary
The concept of *chitta* is introduced as the seat of wisdom, where self-observation leads to intelligence and wise decision-making. Enlightenment is presented as a necessary state to break free from entanglement with the four dimensions of the mind, allowing for a broader perspective and more intelligent actions.

## Key Teachings
- The *chitta* is the seat of wisdom. (Ekam / O&O Academy)
- Self-observation leads to intelligence and wise decision-making. (Ekam / O&O Academy)
- Enlightenment is necessary to break free from entanglement with the four dimensions of the mind. (Ekam / O&O Academy)
- Enlightenment allows for a broader perspective and more intelligent action. (Ekam / O&O Academy)

## Related Concepts
- Enlightenment: a state of profound understanding, characterized by a state of consciousness where one is free of suffering, with intense awareness and no compulsive attachment to the past or future.
