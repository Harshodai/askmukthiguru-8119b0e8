# Relationship with EGO

In the teachings of Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji, the relationship with the EGO is emphasized as a crucial aspect of spiritual growth. The essence of this relationship is not about being addicted to work or other external pursuits but about being present and engaged in every aspect of life. A balanced and joyful human being is one who is fully present in every moment, focusing on the essence of life rather than being consumed by external distractions. This teaching underscores the importance of cultivating a deep connection with the self, leading to a more fulfilling and harmonious existence.
