# Relationship with EGO

# Topic: Relationship with EGO

## Summary
The speaker discusses the experience of encountering someone who had previously expressed romantic interest in them, leading to a series of interactions and reflections on their feelings and past experiences. The speaker reflects on the nature of the ego and how it can influence our perceptions and interactions with others.

## Key Teachings
- **Teaching Point 1**: The speaker acknowledges the role of the ego in shaping our perceptions and interactions with others. They emphasize that the ego often leads us to judge others based on our own desires and experiences, rather than seeing them as they truly are.
- **Teaching Point 2**: The speaker suggests that by recognizing and understanding the ego, we can begin to dissolve its influence and cultivate a more authentic and compassionate relationship with others.

## Quotes
> "The ego is the root of all our problems. It is the source of our fears and insecurities. By understanding and accepting our ego, we can break free from its grip and live a more fulfilling life."
- Sri Krishnaji
