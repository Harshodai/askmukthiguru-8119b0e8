# Healing Through Empathy

## Summary
A "great parent" is defined not by material provision, but by the profound capacity to connect, feel, and heal. This emotional attunement carries immense potential to heal a child, compensating for life’s imperfections and fostering resilience. When this deep connection is present, it prevents the escalation of a child’s inner strife into obsessive, self-centric thinking. Conversely, the absence of this healing dynamic leaves a child vulnerable to turning their struggles inward.
