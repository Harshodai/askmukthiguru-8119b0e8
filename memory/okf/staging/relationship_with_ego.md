# Relationship with EGO

## Summary
The teachings emphasize the importance of changing the fuel for aspiration, focusing on ascribing to oneself what one wants to do rather than comparing oneself to others. It highlights the negative effects of social media and judgment on aspiration and suggests a mind free from comparison and judgment can lead to fulfillment of aspirations without stress.

## Key Teachings
- **Teaching Point 1**: Changing the fuel for aspiration from comparison to ascribing to oneself what one wants to do.
- **Teaching Point 2**: The negative effects of social media and judgment on aspiration and the importance of a mind free from comparison and judgment for fulfillment of aspirations without stress.
