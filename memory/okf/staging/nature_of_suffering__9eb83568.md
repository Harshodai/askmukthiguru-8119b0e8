# Nature of Suffering

## Summary
The nature of suffering is often misunderstood and can be a source of immense pain and confusion. Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji emphasize that suffering arises from the attachment to the ego and the pursuit of desires. They teach that the key to overcoming suffering is to recognize the illusion of the self and to let go of the attachment to the ego. By understanding the illusion of the self, one can dissolve the suffering and attain peace.

## Key Teachings
- **Attachment to the Ego:** The ego is the source of suffering. It is the attachment to the self that leads to suffering. Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji emphasize that the ego is the root cause of all suffering.
- **Letting Go of the Ego:** To overcome suffering, one must let go of the attachment to the ego. This involves recognizing the illusion of the self and understanding that the self is not a fixed entity but a constantly changing illusion.
- **Understanding the Illusion of the Self:** The self is an illusion, and the true nature of reality is beyond the ego. By recognizing this illusion, one can dissolve the suffering and attain peace.

## Quotes
> "The ego is the root cause of all suffering." — Sri Preethaji
> "Letting go of the ego is the key to overcoming suffering." — Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- **Illusion of the Self:** The concept of the self as a fixed, permanent entity is an illusion. Understanding this illusion is crucial in overcoming suffering.
- **Boundlessness:** The concept of the Limitless Field, associated with Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji, represents the idea of 'boundlessness.' It is a concept that helps in understanding the true nature of reality and dissolving the suffering.
