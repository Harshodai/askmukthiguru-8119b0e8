# Seclusion as a Path to Self-Discovery and Spiritual Growth

## Summary
Seclusion and solitude offer a significant opportunity for self-discovery and creative breakthroughs, especially in contrast to the predictable routines of modern life. Choosing seclusion intentionally, as encouraged by figures like Jesus, Buddha, and ancient sages, provides a space for introspection and spiritual growth, leading to a more authentic and meaningful existence.
