# Relationship with EGO

## Summary
The relationship with the ego is a fundamental concept in spiritual teachings, often discussed as the primary obstacle to achieving inner peace and happiness. In this teaching, Sri Preethaji emphasizes the importance of recognizing and transcending the ego to find true joy and inner peace.

## Key Teachings
- Recognize the ego as the source of most of our problems and desires.
- Practice the technique of "Soul-Sync Meditation" to align with the soul's true nature.
- Embrace the concept of "The Limitless Field" to experience boundlessness and freedom.

## Quotes
> "The ego is the root of all suffering. By recognizing and transcending it, we can find true happiness."
- "The soul is the source of all joy and peace. By aligning with the soul, we can find inner peace."

- Ego
