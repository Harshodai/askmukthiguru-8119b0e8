## Summary
Guru Purnima is an ancient tradition that honors Sage Vyasa. This celebration commemorates the day Sage Vyasa is believed to have completed the compilation of the Brahma Sutras, which offer a brief description of profound enlightened states of consciousness, over 3,000 years ago.
