# Compassion, Kindness, and the Beautiful State

## Summary
In the "Beautiful State," compassion is understood as an intelligence distinct from mere kindness. This intelligence allows acts of kindness to be performed without the need for external validation or expectation of reward.

## Key Teachings
- In the "Beautiful State," compassion is not always about giving to beggars or performing acts of charity.
- Compassion involves recognizing an intelligence that is distinct from mere kindness.
- This state allows for acts of kindness to be performed without the need for external validation or expectation of reward.

## Related Concepts
- The Beautiful State: The Beautiful State is a state of inner peace and harmony, characterized by forgiveness, love, and compassion.
- Compassion and Kindness: Compassion and kindness are essential qualities for human beings to come home to.
