# Preventing Depression Through Ancient Wisdom

## Summary
The text discusses five "yukthis" or practices that are effective in preventing depression. These practices, which include storytelling, connecting with nature, and quieting a disturbed mind, are rooted in both ancient spiritual wisdom and modern scientific understanding. Engaging in these yukthis helps individuals foster a sense of belonging and connection, which are vital for emotional well-being and reducing loneliness.

## Key Teachings
- The five yukthis for preventing depression are based on both spiritual wisdom and scientific understanding.
- Practices like storytelling, connecting to nature, and quieting a disturbed mind have been used for thousands of years to promote emotional well-being and reduce loneliness.
- Engaging in these practices helps individuals find a crucial sense of belonging and connection, which is important for preventing depression.
