# The Pervasiveness of Suffering

## Summary
Suffering is widespread among humanity, including children, and is connected to the "Suffering State." Overcoming suffering and living in an enlightened state is important for contributing to human collective consciousness.

## Key Teachings
- Suffering is prevalent in humanity, even among children. (Unknown Channel says)
- Suffering is linked to the "Suffering State." (Unknown Channel says)
- Overcoming suffering and living in an enlightened state is crucial for contributing to human collective consciousness. (Unknown Channel says)

## Related Concepts
- Suffering States: emotional states such as regret, sadness, and loneliness that disconnect individuals from life; a downward spiral of chaos and problems caused by accumulated poisons and limiting beliefs; emotionally painful states of being that disconnect individuals from the universal intelligence and its power.
- Enlightenment: the state of living in an unperturbed state of calm, leading to states of bliss, stillness, love, and peace; a state that can be achieved by utilizing the intelligence.
- Enlightenment State: the highest state of consciousness, characterized by complete understanding and unity.
- Overcoming Suffering: a concept related to spiritual teachings, focusing on the alleviation of suffering; a concept related to emotional turmoil and moral conflict.
