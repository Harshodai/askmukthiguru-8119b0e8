# Addressing Domestic Violence

## Summary
Domestic violence is a global issue that affects individuals across all demographics, including men, women, children, and the elderly in every country.

## Key Teachings
- Domestic violence is not isolated to a specific demographic; it is experienced by men, women, children, and the elderly across every country.

## Quotes
> "Domestic violence is experienced by men, by women, by children and elderly in every country." — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Peaceful Families: Creating home environments free from abuse and domestic violence.
