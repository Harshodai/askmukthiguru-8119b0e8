# The Chataka Bird and the Suffering State

## Summary
The Chataka bird, a mythical creature, symbolizes a refusal to engage with suffering. Despite extreme thirst, it will not drink from ponds or lakes, waiting instead for rain. This analogy highlights the importance of rejecting the "Suffering State" and choosing a life free from it.

## Key Teachings
- The Chataka bird refuses to drink water from ponds or lakes, however thirsty it is, waiting for rain. (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji)
