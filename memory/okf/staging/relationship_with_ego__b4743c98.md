## Summary
In the context of the video "Peace To our Borders |  Evolution During Crisis -58 with Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji," the speaker emphasizes the importance of personal desire for peace and the role of meditation in preventing global conflict. The video discusses the historical and cultural context of peace as a universal aspiration and the current call for global unity in seeking peaceful solutions.

## Key Teachings
- **Sri Preethaji says:** "The relationship with EGO is crucial in understanding the impact of individual consciousness on societal peace. Meditation is essential for personal peace and thus for preventing global conflict."
