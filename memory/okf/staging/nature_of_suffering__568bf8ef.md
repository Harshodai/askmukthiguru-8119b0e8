# Nature of Suffering

# Nature of Suffering

## Summary
Most people live mediocre lives, and the struggle to be anything different disappears when a struggle to be anything different disappears. An ordinary day with morning coffee, evening walk, and the many daily chores can be a beautiful experience.

## Key Teachings
- **Sri Preethaji says:** "Most people live mediocre lives. I use the word mediocre and not ordinary, because there's a great beauty in being ordinary without a struggle to be special, without a struggle to be noticed. That is a great beauty to life. When a struggle to be anything different disappears, an ordinary day with your morning coffee, evening walk, and the many daily chores can be a beautiful experience."

## Related Concepts
