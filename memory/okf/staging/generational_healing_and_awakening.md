# Generational Healing and Awakening

## Summary
The repetition of mistakes made by parents is often due to subconscious imprints from past experiences. Generational healing and awakened parenting involve recognizing and releasing these imprints to create a new, positive cycle of growth.

## Key Teachings
- Repeating parental mistakes often stems from subconscious imprinting of past experiences.
- Awakening the divine dimension of Amba is key for generational healing.
- This awakening involves recognizing and releasing subconscious imprints.
- Releasing these imprints allows for a new, positive cycle in parenting and generational growth.

## Related Concepts
- Trauma: Trauma is a painful Samskara that can influence a person's choices and decisions.
- Cycle of Pain: Hurt human beings perpetuate a cycle of pain and suffering.
- Emotional wounds: Emotional wounds are a topic related to emotional resolution.
- Suffering: Suffering is a multifaceted concept that encompasses various aspects of human experience. It can be described as a state of conflict and ideals, characterized by intense awareness and a lack of compulsive attachment, which can be weeded out through enlightenment.
