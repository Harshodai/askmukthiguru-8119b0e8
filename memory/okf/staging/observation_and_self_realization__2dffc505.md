# Observation and Self-Realization

# Title

## Summary
The teachings emphasize the distinction between ambition and passion as states of becoming successful. Passion leads to reduced self-obsession and greater success, while ambition is self-obsessive and can lead to harmful actions. The key difference lies in the state from which one is becoming successful.
