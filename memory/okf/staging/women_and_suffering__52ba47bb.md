# Women and Suffering

## Summary
Women naturally reside in a Beautiful State, where they manifest the four powers of female consciousness—known in India as the four goddesses—including Shakti, the creative force that infuses life and moves static things to happen. In this state, women inspire others and keep an abiding calm. When they enter a Suffering State, their Shakti becomes distorted, causing aggression and control, and shifting power dynamics.

## Key Teachings
- In a Beautiful State, women express four powers of female consciousness called the four goddesses: Shakti, the power to create, infuse life, and move static and lifeless things to make them happen.
- In this natural state, women are able to inspire others and maintain abiding calm.
- A shift into a Suffering State distorts Shakti, leading to aggression and control and altering power dynamics.

## Quotes
> "When they move into a Suffering State, their Shakti becomes distorted, leading to aggression and control." — both

## Related Concepts
- Beautiful State: A natural state for women where they manifest the four powers of female consciousness, inspiring others and maintaining abiding calm.
- Suffering State: An emotionally painful state that disconnects individuals from the universal intelligence and its power, causing a downward spiral of chaos and problems from accumulated poisons and limiting beliefs; in women, it distorts Shakti into aggression and control.
- Shakti: The power to create, infuse life, and move static and lifeless things to make them happen; becomes distorted in a Suffering State.
- Women: Female individuals who, in their natural state, manifest four powers of female consciousness and whose state determines whether they operate from creative inspiration or distorted aggression.
