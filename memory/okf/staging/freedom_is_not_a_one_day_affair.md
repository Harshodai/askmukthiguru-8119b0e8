# Freedom is Not a One-Day Affair

## Summary
Spiritual freedom, particularly in overcoming challenges like addiction, is not a quick or easy process. It requires continuous effort and is not achieved in a single day or even a few weeks.

## Key Teachings
- Freedom is not a one-day affair, or weeks affair. — Sri Krishnaji

## Quotes
> "Lastly, freedom is not a one-days affair, or weeks affair." — Sri Krishnaji
