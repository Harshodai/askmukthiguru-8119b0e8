# Relationship with EGO

### Relationship with EGO

In the teachings of Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji, the relationship with the EGO is emphasized as a crucial aspect of spiritual growth and transformation. The teachings highlight the importance of cultivating a balanced and joyful human being, present in every aspect of life, rather than being addicted to work or other external pursuits.

Key Teachings:
- **Sri Preethaji says:** "An addicted person is one who is ignoring every other aspect of life. They will ignore that aspect and only be focused on their work."
- **Sri Krishnaji says:** "The EGO is the source of our suffering. By aligning with our true nature, we can break free from its grip."
