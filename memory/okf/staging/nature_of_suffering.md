# Nature of Suffering

## Summary
The topic of suffering is explored through the teachings of Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji, emphasizing the root of suffering as self-centered consciousness and the need for addressing fundamental self-centered thinking to achieve peace. The speakers discuss the historical and contemporary prevalence of conflict and violence, highlighting their origins in self-centeredness and the importance of transforming one's consciousness to end such conflicts.

## Key Teachings
- **Sri Preethaji says:** "The roots of suffering lie in the self-centered consciousness of humanity. We perpetuate conflict and violence due to our separation from others."
- **Sri Krishnaji says:** "The cycle of pain and suffering is perpetuated by hurt human beings. It is essential to understand the spiritual origin of suffering to address it effectively."
