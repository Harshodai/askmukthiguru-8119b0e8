# True Self vs. Illusionary Self

## Summary
Humanity's true identity and legacy lie in embodying Oneness, contrasting with the misconception of being a separate, suffering, and stifled self.

## Key Teachings
- Living and embodying Oneness is the true human legacy and represents who you truly are.
- The perception of being a separate, suffering, and stifled little self is not your true identity.

## Quotes
> "And to live it is a true human legacy. That is who you are. Not this separate, suffering, stifled little self." — Ekam Teachings

## Related Concepts
- Oneness: The true state of identity beyond the isolated ego.
- Illusionary Self: The belief in being a separate, stifled, and suffering individual.
