# Beyond Rigid Views

## Summary
This reflection highlights the importance of moving beyond rigid views to foster deeper, more loving relationships. It suggests that such understanding can prompt individuals to transcend their fixed perspectives, leading to a more profound connection with others.

## Key Teachings
- Understanding can spur individuals to go beyond their rigid views. (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji)

## Quotes
> "Such an understanding will spur you to go beyond your rigid views and prompt" — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Ego: Ego is a state of self-centeredness and separation that can lead to conflict and violence. It is the sense of self that separates one from others and the world.
