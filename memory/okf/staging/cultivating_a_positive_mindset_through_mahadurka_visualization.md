# Cultivating a Positive Mindset Through Mahadurka Visualization

## Summary
This practice emphasizes cultivating a positive and peaceful mindset, particularly during the Navratri period, by visualizing the goddess Mahadurka. Through chanting a mantra in Namaskara Mudra at the heart and focusing on the goddess's blessings, one can purify the mind and foster positive qualities. The practice also includes asking the goddess to dissolve anger and greed from consciousness and replace them with peace and generosity.

## Key Teachings
- The importance of cultivating a positive and peaceful mindset during Navratri. (Unknown speaker)
- Visualizing the goddess Mahadurka can help purify the mind and cultivate positive qualities. (Unknown speaker)
- Chanting a mantra in Namaskara Mudra at the heart while focusing on Mahadurka's blessings is a key part of the practice. (Unknown speaker)
- One can ask the goddess to remove anger and greed from their consciousness and fill them with peace and generosity. (Unknown speaker)
