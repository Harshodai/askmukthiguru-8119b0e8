# The Ancestral Roots of Suffering

## Summary
The suffering experienced by humanity is attributed to the collective consciousness inherited from ancestors, which flows back into the human collective consciousness and impacts future generations.

## Key Teachings
- The suffering in humanity is linked to what "your grandparents, your great-grandparents, your ancestors" leave behind in the human collective consciousness. (Unknown Channel)
- This ancestral legacy impacts future generations. (Unknown Channel)

## Related Concepts
- Suffering: a multifaceted concept that encompasses various aspects of human experience, including unhappiness and pain.
- Ego: a concept referring to the sense of self, identity, and the tendency to identify with thoughts and emotions.
