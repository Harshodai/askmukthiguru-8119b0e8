# Cruelty and the Need for Empathy

## Summary
This teaching highlights the cruelty humans exhibit towards other living beings and Mother Earth, emphasizing that such actions are unacceptable simply because the victims cannot retaliate. It implicitly calls for empathy and ethical behavior towards all life.

## Key Teachings
- Humans should not inflict cruelty on other beings or Mother Earth just because those beings cannot penalize them (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji).
