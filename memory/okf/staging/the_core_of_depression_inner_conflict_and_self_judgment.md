# The Core of Depression: Inner Conflict and Self-Judgment

## Summary
The fundamental cause of depression is identified as the continuous internal commentary and self-judgment that leads individuals into inner conflict and a state of war with themselves. This habit of self-criticism extends to various aspects of life, including one's appearance, family, home, and work, and is central to both the root cause of depression and the necessity for self-healing.

## Key Teachings
- The core of depression stems from incessant commentary and self-judgment, which drives people into inner conflict and a war with themselves. (Unknown speaker)
- This habit of self-criticism and judgment of various life aspects (appearance, family, home, work) is central to the root cause of depression and the need for self-healing. (Unknown speaker)
- This habit is what leads individuals to inner war. (Unknown speaker)
