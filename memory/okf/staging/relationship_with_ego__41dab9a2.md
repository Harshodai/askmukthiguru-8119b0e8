# Relationship with EGO

## Summary
The manifestation of failure patterns in the physical, mind, and energy bodies is discussed, emphasizing the importance of purging these impressions to prevent them from recurring and manifesting as failure. This context highlights the spiritual courage manifest's role in liberating individuals from these patterns and achieving success.

## Key Teachings
- Purging failure impressions is crucial for preventing them from recurring and manifesting as failure.

## Quotes
> "Purging failure impressions is the key to preventing them from recurring and manifesting as failure."
--- Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- **Failure Impression**: An impression or thought that leads to failure in the physical, mind, and energy bodies.
- **Spiritual Courage**: The strength and courage needed to purify negative impressions and manifest positive ones.
- **Limitless Field**: A concept in spiritual teachings representing the idea of 'boundlessness,' associated with the teachings of Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji.
