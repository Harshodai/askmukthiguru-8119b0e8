# Holistic Dimensions of Health

## Summary
Health and vitality extend beyond the boundaries of the physical body, encompassing broader dimensions of human experience.

## Key Teachings
- The experience of health is not restricted solely to the physical or biological body.

## Quotes
> "The experience of health is not limited to the physical body." — Ekam / O&O Academy

## Related Concepts
- **Yogic Vitality**: A holistic dimension of health that moves beyond mere physical existence.
