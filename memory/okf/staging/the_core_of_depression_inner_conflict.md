# The Core of Depression: Inner Conflict

## Summary
The core of depression is identified as an internal battle with oneself, characterized by constant self-criticism and commentary. This inner conflict creates a state of division and war within, stemming from a failure to make peace with oneself. This leads to criticizing every aspect of one's life and dividing experiences into "should be" or "should not be."

## Key Teachings
- The core of depression is a battle with oneself, manifesting through constant self-criticism and commentary. (Unknown speaker)
- This inner conflict leads to a state of inner war and division. (Unknown speaker)
- The root cause of depression is a battle with oneself, and if we do not make peace with ourselves, we will criticize everything about ourselves, including our appearance, status, home, family, and life itself. (Unknown speaker)
- This habit of incessant commentary divides every experience of our lives into "should be" or "should not be." (Unknown speaker)

## Related Concepts
- Depression: a mental health condition characterized by persistent feelings of sadness, hopelessness, and disconnection from oneself and others.
- Stressful State: a condition characterized by feelings of anxiety, tension, or overwhelm.
- Chaos: a state of disorder, confusion, or randomness.
