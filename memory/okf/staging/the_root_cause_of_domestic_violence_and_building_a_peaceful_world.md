# The Root Cause of Domestic Violence and Building a Peaceful World

## Summary
Domestic violence stems from a lack of love and the presence of cruelty, which act as a disease affecting the human heart. Overcoming this requires addressing the absence of love to nurture peace.

## Key Teachings
- The absence of love and the resulting cruelty is a disease that silently invades the human heart and leads to domestic violence.

## Quotes
> "The absence of love and the resultant cruelty is a disease that can silently invade the human heart and pushes us to domestic violence." — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- **Absence of Love**: A condition that allows cruelty to take hold in the human heart, driving domestic violence.
