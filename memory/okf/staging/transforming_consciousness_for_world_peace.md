# Transforming Consciousness for World Peace

## Summary
Humanity is currently heading towards a troubled world. To address this, what the world requires today is not a new religion or another set of rules and codes, but an inner transformation in individual consciousness.

## Key Teachings
- Without a change in consciousness, humanity continues moving toward a troubled world.
- The world does not need another religion or additional sets of codes and rules; true change comes from individual transformation.

## Quotes
> "Otherwise, as I always keep mentioning, we are heading towards a troubled world. What the world needs today is not yet another religion, not yet another set of codes and..." — Ekam / O&O Academy

## Related Concepts
- **Troubled World**: The state humanity is heading towards if individual consciousness remains unchanged by relying merely on external rules or religions.
