## Summary
The EGO Transformation Challenge is a seven-day journey designed to cleanse the brain of toxins through Yognitra, culminating on the International Day of Yoga on June 21st. This challenge aims to awaken the mystique fire Kundalini and access hidden realms of consciousness, leading to true bliss. By cleansing the brain, participants can prepare their minds for a deeper spiritual journey. The expected benefits include enhanced mental clarity, improved emotional balance, and a deeper connection with the divine.
