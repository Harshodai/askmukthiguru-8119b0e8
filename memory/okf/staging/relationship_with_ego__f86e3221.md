# Relationship with EGO

# Title

## Summary
This teaching explores the concept of the relationship with the ego, emphasizing the importance of recognizing and transcending the ego in order to achieve spiritual growth and enlightenment.

## Key Teachings
- Recognizing the ego as a barrier to spiritual growth and self-awareness.
- Techniques to transform the ego into a servant of the soul, leading to a deeper connection with the infinite consciousness.
- The role of meditation and practices such as soul sync and oneness meditation in enhancing awareness and spiritual connection.

## Quotes
> "The ego is the source of all our suffering and limitations. It is time to let go of the ego and embrace the infinite consciousness."
- "Meditation is not just a practice; it is a journey of self-discovery and enlightenment."
