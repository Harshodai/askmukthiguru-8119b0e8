# Overcoming Limiting Beliefs: The EGO Relationship

### Title

In this teaching, we explore the relationship between the EGO and limiting beliefs that hinder wealth creation and prosperity. By identifying and challenging these beliefs, we can transcend the obstacles and emerge as conscious wealth creators. Join Sri Preethaji on this transformative journey.

### Key Teachings

1. **Identifying Limiting Beliefs**: The first step in overcoming the EGO relationship is recognizing the limiting beliefs that prevent you from achieving wealth. These beliefs often stem from past experiences and unconscious programming.

2. **Challenging Limiting Beliefs**: Once identified, these beliefs need to be challenged. This involves questioning their validity and exploring alternative perspectives that align with your true desires and aspirations.

3. **Transcending the EGO**: By challenging these limiting beliefs, you can transcend the EGO relationship, leading to a more conscious and prosperous existence. This journey involves embracing the power of consciousness and the journey towards freedom and resolution.
