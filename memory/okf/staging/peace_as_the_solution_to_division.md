# Peace as the Solution to Division

## Summary
When communities and races are divided and experience hate and violence, the only solution to resolve their differences is peace.

## Key Teachings
- If communities and races are divided against each other and the flames of hate and violence begin to burn, all they need is just peace to solve their differences. (Sri Preethaji)
