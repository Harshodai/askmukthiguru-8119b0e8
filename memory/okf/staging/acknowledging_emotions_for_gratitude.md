# Acknowledging Emotions for Gratitude

## Summary
This teaching highlights the importance of recognizing and acknowledging one's emotions, even negative ones like anger, hate, and ingratitude, as a step towards cultivating gratitude. It suggests that true gratitude can emerge when one is honest about their inner state.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Preethaji says that even when practicing gratitude daily, one might still feel anger, hate, and ingratitude, especially when reflecting on past experiences like a tough upbringing.
- Sri Preethaji emphasizes the importance of acknowledging these feelings, stating, "I know I have to be grateful to her for who I am today. If not for a push, I w[ould not be who I am today]." This implies that recognizing the impact of past experiences, even difficult ones, can lead to gratitude.
