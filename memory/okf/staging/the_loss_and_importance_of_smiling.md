# The Loss and Importance of Smiling

## Summary
There is a dramatic contrast between how frequently individuals smile as children compared to when they become adults. 

## Key Teachings
- As a child, an individual smiles on average around 400 times a day.
- As an adult, people smile less than 15 times a day.

## Quotes
> "As a child, on an average, you have smiled around 400 times a day. As an adult, we smile less than 15 tim..." — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- smiling: A frequent expression during childhood that dramatically diminishes in adult life.
