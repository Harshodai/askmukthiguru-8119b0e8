# Prayer and Namaste Mudra

## Summary
Prayer is described as a profound, universal conversation with the universe that transcends religious boundaries, representing a state of openness and trust where one asks from the deepest resources of the heart and receives with gratitude. To facilitate this sacred connection, the Pranam or Namaste Mudra is highly recommended.

## Key Teachings
- Prayer is a profound, universal conversation with the universe that transcends religious boundaries, representing a state of openness and trust where one asks from the deepest resources of the heart and receives with gratitude.
- To facilitate this sacred connection, the Pranam or Namaste Mudra is highly recommended.
- By joining the palms at the heart center, practitioners unite their intellect and emotion, symbolizing the harmonious merging of individual consciousness with universal consciousness.

## Related Concepts
- Pranam (practice): A gesture of reverence, equivalent to the Namaste Mudra, used to facilitate a connection with the divine.
- Namaste Mudra (practice): The physical gesture of joining the palms at the heart center to unite intellect and emotion.
- Universal Consciousness (concept): The harmonious merging of individual consciousness with a universal, cosmic awareness.
