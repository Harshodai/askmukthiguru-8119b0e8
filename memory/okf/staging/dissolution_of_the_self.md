# Dissolution of the Self

## Summary
The dissolution of the self is a profound mystical state where the illusory sense of individual identity dissolves, leading to a state of oneness with one's surroundings. This state is illustrated through an example of gamma brainwave activity and a state referred to as "Tatami," which can be induced through Sri Krishnaji's deeksha.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Krishnaji explains that deeksha can push a practitioner into enlightened states, such as the dissolution of the self.
- A state of "Tatami" represents a profound experience where the illusory self dissolves.
- This dissolution is accompanied by gamma brainwave activity, indicating a high level of conscious integration.
- In this state, the practitioner becomes one with their surroundings, transcending the fragmented sense of "I-Consciousness."
