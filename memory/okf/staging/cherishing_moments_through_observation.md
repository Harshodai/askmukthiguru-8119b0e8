# Cherishing Moments Through Observation

## Summary
This teaching emphasizes the importance of observing and acknowledging moments of love and gratitude, encouraging individuals to feel, honor, cherish, and be grateful for these experiences.

## Key Teachings
- To truly live, one must "Feel them and honour them. Cherish them and be grateful for" moments of love and gratitude. (Ekam / O&O Academy)

## Quotes
> "Feel them and honour them. Cherish them and be grateful for" — Ekam / O&O Academy

## Related Concepts
- The Power of Observation: The ability to notice and understand the world, emphasizing the importance of appreciating and being grateful for experiences and opportunities.
