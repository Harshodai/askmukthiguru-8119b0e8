# Healing Through Connection: Releasing Hurt and Disappointment

## Summary
Deep connection in a relationship can be a profoundly healing experience, allowing for the release of emotional pain and disappointment. This process is likened to a dragon shedding its scales, signifying a transformation where hurt and disappointment fall away.

## Key Teachings
- A deep connection in a relationship is very healing for that relationship (Unknown Channel says).
- This healing experience is described as "the dragon pulling out his scales" (Unknown Channel says).
- When this deep connection occurs, "hurt falls off" and "disappointment falls off from the consciou" (Unknown Channel says).

## Quotes
> "That experience is very healing for that relationship. It is the dragon pulling out his scales. That is when hurt falls off, that is when disappointment falls off from the consciou" — Unknown Channel

## Related Concepts
- Ego: Ego is a state of self-centeredness and separation that can lead to conflict and violence.
- Suffering: Suffering is a concept referring to pain, distress, and hardship, often arising from attachment and ignorance, and can be alleviated through spiritual practices.
