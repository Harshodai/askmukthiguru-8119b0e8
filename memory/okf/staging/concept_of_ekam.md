# Concept of Ekam

## Summary
Ekam is a multifaceted spiritual entity, organization, and location situated in Andhra Pradesh, India, between Tirupati and Chennai. It is described as a mystic powerhouse, a living spiritual center, and a global meditation festival dedicated to personal growth, self-discovery, and collective transformation. The organization, also known as One World Academy, was co-created by Shri Priji and Shri Sri Krishnaji with the vision to establish 80,000 engaged, enlightened people globally, aiming to transition individuals into awakened states of consciousness through developed mystic technology. Its physical campus features Asia's largest pillarless hall and is conceptualized as a location where the Field of Awakening is brought, housing a temple dedicated to the worship of the divine.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Krishnaji established the spiritual universe of Ekam for humanity's enlightenment, beginning the construction of its monumental sanctum at the age of 19.
- Ekam is conceptualized as a location where the Field of Awakening is brought, serving as a hub for spiritual exploration, courses, meditation, and spiritual circles designed to facilitate effortless spiritual upliftment, enlightenment, and the resolution of life's challenges.
- The physical campus of Ekam is characterized by mystical architecture, including a hall designed to facilitate human awakening through the principles of the Golden R.
