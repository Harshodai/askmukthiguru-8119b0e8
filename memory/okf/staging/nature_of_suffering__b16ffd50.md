# Nature of Suffering

## Summary
A teaching from Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishna on the nature of suffering, exploring how a refusal to change and resistance to adapting to new circumstances—such as during a pandemic—can lead to negative consequences and collective disorder.

## Key Teachings
- A refusal to change and resistance to adapting to changing circumstances can result in suffering and problems. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- Following basic rules and adapting to changing conditions is important to avoid the negative consequences of non-adaptation. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Suffering States: Emotionally painful states of being that disconnect individuals from the universal intelligence and its power, or a downward spiral of chaos and problems caused by accumulated poisons and limiting beliefs.
- Enlightenment: A state of profound understanding characterized by consciousness free from suffering, with intense awareness and no compulsive attachment to the past or future.
