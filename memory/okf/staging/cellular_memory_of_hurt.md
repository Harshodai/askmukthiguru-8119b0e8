# Cellular Memory of Hurt

## Summary
This teaching addresses the universal psychological trauma of non-acceptance, manifesting in cultural issues such as gender discrimination. It highlights that humanity-created problems require healing through a transcendental journey to overcome inherited and stored hurt at the cellular level.

## Key Teachings
- The concept of cellular memory refers to the unconscious, long-term storage of traumatic experiences that can affect an individual's physical and emotional well-being.
- Emotional hurt is a state that can make a person reactive and lead them to disconnect from others, creating a subjective or objective pain that impacts balance, decisions, and actions.
- Humanity-created problems, such as the discrimination against the girl child and boy child in India, are cultural manifestations of the universal psychological trauma of non-acceptance.
- Healing these inherited and stored hurts requires a transcendental journey to move beyond the reactive state and overcome the trauma stored in the body and mind.
