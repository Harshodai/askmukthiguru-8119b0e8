# Cultivating Gratitude Through Awareness

## Summary
Gratitude arises spontaneously when one becomes aware of the contributions of others in their life. Nurturing this awareness is crucial for awakening gratitude.

## Key Teachings
- Gratitude is an awareness that arises spontaneously when one becomes aware of the contributions of all the people who have played a role in their life. (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji)
