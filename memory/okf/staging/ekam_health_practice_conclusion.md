# Ekam Health Practice Conclusion

## Summary
The concluding steps of the Ekam health practice involve holding the breath, performing Shavasana (corpse pose), and ending with gratitude and daily practice reminders. This process is designed to enhance the practice's effectiveness by focusing on the body's energy channels, the flow of prana and ojas, and fostering a positive mindset through gratitude.

## Key Teachings
- The Ekam health practice concludes with specific steps: holding the breath, lying in Shavasana, and expressing gratitude. (Unknown speaker)
- Gratitude is a crucial component of the practice, acknowledging blessings of health and promoting a positive mindset and well-being. (Unknown speaker)
- Shavasana is beneficial for overall health. (Unknown speaker)
