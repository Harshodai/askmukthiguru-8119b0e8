# Relationship with EGO

# Relationship with EGO

## Summary
The video discusses the characteristics of an addicted person, emphasizing their focus on work to the exclusion of other aspects of life, contrasting with a balanced, joyful human being who is present in every aspect of life.

## Key Teachings
- An addicted person focuses solely on work, ignoring other aspects of life.
- A balanced, joyful human being is present in every aspect of life.

## Quotes
> "An addicted person is one who is ignoring every other aspect of life. They will ignore that aspect and only be focused on their work."

## Related Concepts
- **Addiction**: The state of being unable to stop engaging in a behavior or habit, often leading to negative consequences.
- **Balance**: A state of harmony or equilibrium, where all aspects of life are considered and valued equally.
- **Joyfulness**: A state of happiness and contentment, characterized by a sense of well-being and satisfaction.
- **Presence**: The state of being fully engaged in the present moment, without distractions or preoccupations.
- **Limitless Field**: A concept in spiritual teachings representing the idea of 'boundlessness,' associated with the teachings of Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji, where it is described as a state of being free from limitations and boundaries.
