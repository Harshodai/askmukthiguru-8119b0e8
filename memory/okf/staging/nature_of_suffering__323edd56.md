# Nature of Suffering

## Summary
This teaching explores the concept of suffering and its nature, drawing from the teachings of Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji. The speakers discuss the origins of suffering, its manifestations, and how it can be overcome through spiritual practices.

## Key Teachings
- **Sri Preethaji says:** "Suffering arises from the attachment to the impermanent and the pursuit of happiness in the transient."
- **Sri Krishnaji says:** "Suffering is the manifestation of the ego and the desire for permanence."

## Quotes
> "Sri Preethaji says: 'Suffering arises from the attachment to the impermanent and the pursuit of happiness in the transient.'"

## Related Concepts
- **Ego:** The concept of the ego is discussed in relation to the origin of suffering, highlighting the role of the ego in perpetuating suffering.
- **Desire for Permanence:** The speakers emphasize that suffering is a manifestation of the desire for permanence, which is rooted in the ego.
- **Limitless Field:** The concept of the Limitless Field is introduced, representing the idea of 'boundlessness' in spiritual teachings, associated with the teachings of Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji, where it is described as a state of being free from suffering.
