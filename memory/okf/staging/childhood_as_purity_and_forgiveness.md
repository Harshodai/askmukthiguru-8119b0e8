# Childhood as Purity and Forgiveness

## Summary
Spiritual teachings emphasize childhood as a state of purity and forgiveness, highlighting that these qualities are intrinsic to the soul and heart. This perspective challenges the common view of childhood as a period of vulnerability, instead focusing on the transformative power of innocence and forgiveness in shaping character. The importance of this concept is further emphasized within the context of the Evolution Series.
