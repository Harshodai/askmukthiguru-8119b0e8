# Maha Saraswati and the Journey to Unity Consciousness

## Summary
The celebration of Navaratri, particularly the divine play of Maha Saraswati on the ninth day, symbolizes the seeker's journey towards enlightenment and the manifestation of divine wisdom. Maha Saraswati, representing the great goddess of wisdom, embodies unity consciousness and the transcendence of separation and ignorance.
