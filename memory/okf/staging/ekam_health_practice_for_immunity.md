# Ekam Health Practice for Immunity

## Summary
The Ekam health practice is a morning routine designed to maintain high immunity throughout the day. It can also be performed at other times.

## Key Teachings
- The Ekam health practice is best done in the morning to keep immunity high throughout the day (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji).
- The practice can also be done at other times (Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji).

## Related Concepts
- Ekam Mti: An organization co-created by Guru Sri Priya G, promoting unity and oneness.
