# Nature of Suffering

# Nature of Suffering

## Summary
The phenomenon of division and separation is a result of ignorance, where the more ignorant one becomes, the more they engage in dividing themselves from others. This leads to feelings of superiority and a loss of kindness, respect, and value for fellow humans. The speaker emphasizes the importance of recognizing and overcoming ignorance to break the cycle of division.

## Key Teachings
- Ignorance is the root cause of division and separation.
- The more ignorant one becomes, the more they engage in dividing themselves from others.
- This division leads to feelings of superiority and a loss of kindness, respect, and value for fellow humans.

## Quotes
> "The more ignorant you are, the more you divide."
> — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- **Division and Separation**: The phenomenon of dividing oneself from others due to ignorance.
- **Kindness, Respect, and Value for Fellow Humans**: The loss of these qualities when one becomes more ignorant.
- **Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji**: The spiritual teachers who emphasize the importance of recognizing and overcoming ignorance to break the cycle of division.
