# Relationship with EGO

## Summary
The third langanam, fasting hurtful and pessimistic speech, is discussed, emphasizing its importance in enhancing one's vaksakti and bringing about positive thoughts and a break from negative thinking.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Preethaji says, "Your thoughts begin to become more positive. You can more easily break away from negative thinking."
- Sri Krishnaji adds, "Your thoughts begin to become more positive. You can more easily break away from negative thinking."

## Quotes
> "Your thoughts begin to become more positive. You can more easily break away from negative thinking."
> — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- **Positive Thinking**: Enhancing positive thoughts and breaking away from negative thinking.
- **Vaksakti**: The power of speech, which is enhanced by fasting hurtful and pessimistic speech.
- **Negative Childhood Impressions**: Dissolving negative childhood impressions through spiritual practices.
- **Limitless Field**: A concept in spiritual teachings representing boundlessness, associated with the teachings of Sri Krishnaji and Sri Preethaji.
