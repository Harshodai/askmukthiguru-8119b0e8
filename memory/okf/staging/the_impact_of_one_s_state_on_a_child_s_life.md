# The Impact of One's State on a Child's Life

# The Impact of One's State on a Child's Life

## Summary
This teaching highlights how a parent's internal state, particularly a "bad state," can directly contribute to suffering in their child's life. It suggests that the consciousness or state from which a child is conceived or raised impacts their future.

## Key Teachings
- If one lives in a "bad state" and has a child from that state, they will contribute suffering to the child's life. (Unknown Channel)

## Quotes
> "Now, if you are going to live in that bad state and assume that you are going to have a child from that state, so what are you going to contribute to the child's life? Exactly, suffering." — Unknown Channel

## Related Concepts
- karma: A concept related to the flow of consciousness and its impact on an individual's life.
- Conscious Parent: A parent who is aware of the divine drama that unfolds during conception and pregnancy, and who can invite a great consciousness to flow into their world.
