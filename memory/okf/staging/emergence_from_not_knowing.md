# Emergence from Not Knowing

# Emergence from Not Knowing

## Summary
The provided text suggests that true novelty and emergence can only arise from a state of "not knowing," rather than from existing knowledge or familiar patterns. This implies that periods of uncertainty or seclusion can be opportunities for profound self-discovery and creative breakthroughs.

## Key Teachings
- Only from a state of "not knowing" can something entirely new emerge. (Unknown Channel)
- Newness does not arise from the "old ground of knowing." (Unknown Channel)

## Quotes
> "Only from not knowing can something totally new emerge, not from old ground of knowing." — Unknown Channel

## Related Concepts
- Suffering States: emotionally painful states of being that disconnect individuals from the universal intelligence and its power.
