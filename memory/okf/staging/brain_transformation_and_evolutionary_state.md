# Brain Transformation and Evolutionary State

## Summary
The human body—including the brain, neural structure, heart, and spine—is composed of intelligence. Moving into a Beautiful State rewires neural connections and allows evolved consciousness to be passed down to future generations.

## Key Teachings
- **Body Intelligence**: The brain, neural structure, heart, and spine are all forms of intelligence within the human body.
- **Rewiring Neural Connections**: Personal transformation into a Beautiful State alters neural connections, helping address the human body's state of disconnection and passing an evolved state of consciousness to future generations.

## Quotes
> "Today, our brain, our neural structure, our heart, our spine, which are all intelligence..." — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- **Beautiful State**: A state of consciousness that rewires neural connections and promotes an evolved state of consciousness.
