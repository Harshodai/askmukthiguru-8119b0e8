# Enlightenment and the Mind's Dimensions

## Summary
Enlightenment is essential to free oneself from entanglement with the four dimensions of the mind, leading to a broader perspective and more intelligent actions. This process involves self-observation, which cultivates intelligence and wise decision-making, with the *chitta* serving as the seat of wisdom.

## Key Teachings
- Enlightenment is necessary to break free from the entanglement with the four dimensions of the mind. (Ekam / O&O Academy)
- Enlightenment helps achieve a broader perspective and more intelligent action. (Ekam / O&O Academy)
- The *chitta* is the seat of wisdom. (Ekam / O&O Academy)
- Self-observation leads to intelligence and wise decision-making. (Ekam / O&O Academy)

## Related Concepts
- Enlightenment: a state of profound understanding, characterized by a state of consciousness where one is free of suffering, with intense awareness and no compulsive attachment to the past or future.
