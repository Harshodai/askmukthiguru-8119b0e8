# Significance of Pranamudra (Namaste Pose)

## Summary
The posture of joining both palms at the heart center is a timeless gesture that brings togetherness during prayer.

## Key Teachings
- Joining both palms at your heart center is a timeless posture that brings unity and connection.

## Quotes
> "There is something timeless to this posture when you join both palms at your heart center." — Ekam

## Related Concepts
- **Pranamudra / Namaste Pose**: Joining both palms at the heart center during prayer.
