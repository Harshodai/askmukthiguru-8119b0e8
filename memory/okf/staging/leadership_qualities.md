# Leadership Qualities

## Summary
A good leader is characterized by vision, purpose, responsibility, and empathy, inspiring others and avoiding ego-driven or self-serving actions. In contrast, a bad leader is driven by aggressive ambition, self-interest, and exploitation. A true leader is responsible and aware of the consequences of their decisions.

## Key Teachings
- A good leader possesses vision, purpose, responsibility, and empathy.
- A good leader inspires others and has a clear sense of purpose.
- A good leader is responsible and avoids ego-driven or self-serving leadership.
- A bad leader is driven by aggressive ambition, serves themselves, and exploits others.
- A true leader is responsible and aware of the consequences of their decisions.
