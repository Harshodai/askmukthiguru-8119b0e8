# Ahankara and Identification

## Summary
Ahankara is described as an ignorant tendency to constantly identify. This concept is part of understanding the layers of the mind matrix, which also includes buddhi (intellect) and chitta (awareness), and how these contribute to the mind's conditioning.

## Key Teachings
- Ahankara is an ignorant tendency to constantly identify. (Ekam / O&O Academy)

## Quotes
> "Ahankara is that ignorant tendency to constantly identify." — Ekam / O&O Academy

## Related Concepts
- Ahankara: The ignorant tendency to constantly identify, a component of the mind matrix.
- Buddhi: The intellect, another layer of the mind matrix.
- Chitta: Awareness, a layer of the mind matrix.
- Enlightenment: A state of profound understanding, free of suffering, with intense awareness and no compulsive attachment to the past or future, which is related to understanding the mind's conditioning.
