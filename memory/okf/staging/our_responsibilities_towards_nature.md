# Our Responsibilities Towards Nature

## Summary
This teaching emphasizes the significant impact of human activities on the environment and highlights the necessity of respecting and preserving the natural habitats of various life forms, particularly animals. It calls for conscious and compassionate living towards the Earth.

## Key Teachings
- Humans consume and take much from the Earth. (Unknown Channel)
- Our homes cannot be safe when the rest of the Earth is burning. (Unknown Channel)

## Quotes
> "How much we consume and take from the Earth. We cannot have our homes safe when the rest of the Earth is burning" — Unknown Channel
