# EGO and Healthy Relationships

## Summary
In a relationship, the initial attraction often stems from euphoria and excitement. However, this initial attraction is not meant to be the foundation of a long-term, enduring, and loving relationship. To build a lasting connection, one must evolve in consciousness, becoming a resilient individual who can overcome hurt and disappointment. This means being able to walk out of negative experiences and maintain a positive outlook. By focusing on personal growth, one can transform their ego and open their heart to connect with people.

## Key Teachings
- **Evolution in Consciousness**: To build a lasting connection, one must evolve in consciousness, becoming a resilient individual who can overcome hurt and disappointment.
- **Personal Growth**: Focusing on personal growth is crucial for building a healthy relationship.

## Quotes
> "To build a lasting connection, one must evolve in consciousness, becoming a resilient individual who can overcome hurt and disappointment." — Sri Preethaji

## Related Concepts
- **Ego**: The identification of oneself with the mind and the body, the sense of self or identity, the distinction between the self and the ego, and the idea of self-centeredness or a sense of separateness.
- **Transformation**: What is being asked about in the input text.
- **Healthy Relationships**: A relationship that is built on mutual respect, trust, and understanding, rather than on the initial attraction or ego.
