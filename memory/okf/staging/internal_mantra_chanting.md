# Internal Mantra Chanting

## Summary
Internal mantra chanting is the fourth phase of the soul sync meditation, which follows the observation of the natural pause between breaths. In this phase, the practitioner transitions into internally chanting a mantra while exhaling, as part of a structured eight-breath sequence designed to guide the practitioner through progressive stages of meditation.
