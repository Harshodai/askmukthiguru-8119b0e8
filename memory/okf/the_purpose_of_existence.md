# The Purpose of Existence


## Summary
This teaching explores the concept of purpose, using the example of a tree to illustrate that while something may not have a conscious "meaning," it can still serve a vital purpose. It touches upon the idea that the search for meaning is an existential quest.

## Key Teachings
- A tree may not have a meaning, but it does serve a purpose. (Unknown Channel)
- The tree is necessary for the earth, and its existence is important. (Unknown Channel)

## Quotes
> "The tree may not have a meaning, but it is true that it does serve a purpose." — Unknown Channel
