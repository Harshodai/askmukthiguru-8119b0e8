# Importance Of Awareness

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
It is important that you bring attention to it, not put it under the carpet and say it is okay. You have to practice awareness. You have to see suffering. You have to see the truth of your suffering.

Every time you experience an inner disturbance, you need to bring attention to your state. If you do not bring attention to your suffering state, it is going to build. Unconsciously, you're building your state of suffering, you're fueling it if you do not bring attention. If you're able to bring attention, then it doesn't build.
