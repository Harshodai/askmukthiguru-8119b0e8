# Ego Relationship

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Spirituality according to many may be a concept or a belief but for us spirituality is a mystic science of consciousness and it's awakening and this journey in consciousness leads you to your enlightenment the more you learn about awakening of consciousness and enlightenment you will know the why of your unhappiness the why of your suffering you will discover the how of becoming liberated and living joyfully you will learn the where to live a fulfilling life i see spirituality as a two-fold purpose to transform your personal experience of life make it more beautiful to make it free of stress free of fear free of frustration regardless of your circumstances the second purpose is to awaken to enlightened states of consciousness with which you flew in life and you influence the external circumstances to create greater oneness
