# The Importance of Careful Speech


## Summary
This teaching emphasizes the critical importance of being mindful and careful about what one says, as speech can have significant impacts on others, including children and friends.

## Key Teachings
- What you speak requires great care. (Ekam / O&O Academy says)
- Children and friends are observing and listening to what you say. (Ekam / O&O Academy says)
