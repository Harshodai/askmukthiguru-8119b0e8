# Generational Healing And Awakening

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
In the coming months manifest, this manifest is going to be about manifest generational healing and manifest awakened parenting. Why do you think you are repeating the same mistakes your mother did or your father did? Why are you shocked when you realize your son or your daughter or your grandson or your granddaughter are exhibiting the traits of your parents whom they probably have never met?

There is a continuity of experiences that flow from one generation to another. There is a continuity of experiences that sometimes even skip a couple of generations but then yet continue to flow. And this continuity is happening in your physical body, in your energy body and in your mind body. This coming manifest I will be awakening the divine dimension of Amba or the compassionate healing force of the universe in your consciousness to heal your children, to heal your grandchildren, your nieces or nephews or even your students and many generations.
