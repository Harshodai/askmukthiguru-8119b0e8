# Guided Peace Prayer Practice

## Summary
Peace is the cessation of internal conflict, leading to a profound state of inner restfulness and lightness in one's being.

## Key Teachings
- Inner peace occurs when the entire internal experience of conflict comes to an end within an individual.
- Peace is experienced as a deep sense of inner restfulness.

## Quotes
> "Peace is when the entire internal experience of conflict comes to an end within you. It is a deep sense of inner restfulness" — Ekam / O&O Academy

## Related Concepts
- **Inner Peace**: The state where internal conflict ends and deep restfulness begins.
