# The Meaning and Nature of Prayer

## Summary
Prayer is defined as a universal experience that goes beyond religion. It is a simple, beautiful experience and represents an open conversation with the universe.

## Key Teachings
- Prayer is not restricted to any religion; it is a simple and beautiful experience open to everyone.
- Prayer is essentially a conversation with the universe.

## Quotes
> "Prayer is beyond religion. It is a simple and a beautiful experience." — Ekam / O&O Academy
> "Prayer is a conversation with the universe." — Ekam / O&O Academy

## Related Concepts
- Prayer: A universal conversation with the divine or universe beyond religious boundaries.
