# When Love is Absent in the Home

## Summary
Without love, homes and families lose their capacity to be safe havens and instead turn into environments of deep emotional and physical suffering.

## Key Teachings
- Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji point out that when families and homes are broken by domestic violence and lack love, environments that should offer safety become transformed into spaces of suffering.
- The absence of love in the home leads to severe pain and agony for those within it.

## Quotes
> "They are places of pain and agony." — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Absence of Love: The condition that transforms places of potential safety into environments of agony and suffering.
