# Defying Physical and Emotional Gravity

## Summary
Young children and young adults naturally experience growth and a surging energy that defies gravity as they continue to grow.

## Key Teachings
- Young children and young adults are in a continuous state of growth, surging into the skies and defying physical gravity.

## Quotes
> "They are growing and they are surging into the skies. Young children and young adults are defying gravity. They are still growing." — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Defying Gravity: The natural state of growth and upward movement seen in young children and young adults.
