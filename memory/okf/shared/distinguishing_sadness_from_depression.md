# Distinguishing Sadness from Depression

## Summary
Addressing the inquiry regarding how to differentiate between feeling sad and being depressed, noting that individuals are sometimes uncertain whether they are experiencing temporary sadness or have moved into depression.

## Key Teachings
- It can be difficult to discern whether an emotional state is simply sadness or if it has progressed into depression.

## Quotes
> "So, we have a question on how do the difference between being sad and being depressed? Because sometimes we're not sure whether we're sad or we're really into depression" — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Sadness versus Depression: Discerning between experiencing natural sadness and being in depression.
