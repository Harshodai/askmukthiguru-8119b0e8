# Returning to Inner Beauty

## Summary
The teaching contrasts the "beautiful state" and the "stressful state" through the fable of monks Yesmi and Nomi. Yesmi embodies the beautiful state—a state of connection, joy, love, compassion, vitality, and presence—while Nomi represents the stressful state, marked by agitation, guilt, and preoccupation with self.

## Key Teachings
- The beautiful state fosters clarity, simplicity, and effective action without attachment.  
- The stressful state is characterized by agitation, guilt, and preoccupation with self, leading to ineffective action and inner turmoil.  

## Related Concepts
- The Beautiful State: A state of inner peace and harmony, characterized by forgiveness, love, and compassion.  
-**Suffering States** ([[suffering_states]]): Emotional and mental conditions that disconnect individuals from life, universal intelligence, and its power, often resulting in chaos, confusion, conflict, anger, fear, and loneliness.
