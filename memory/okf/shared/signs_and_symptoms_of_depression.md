# Signs and Symptoms of Depression

## Summary
A brief context on distinguishing between ordinary sadness caused by life's disappointments and depression, which is described as an inescapable state of deep sadness.

## Key Teachings
- Normal sadness can result from life's disappointments, such as when life does not present expected opportunities or prevents one from meeting people they wish to meet.
- Depression is characterized as an inescapable state of deep sadness, distinguishing it from temporary or understandable reactions to life's events.

## Quotes
> "If life is not throwing up those opportunities or not allowing you to meet those people whom you really want to meet..." — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- **Sadness vs. Depression**: Normal sadness stems from external disappointments, whereas depression manifests as an inescapable state of deep sadness.
