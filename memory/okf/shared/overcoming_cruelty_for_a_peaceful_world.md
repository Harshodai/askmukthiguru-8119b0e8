# Overcoming Cruelty for a Peaceful World

## Summary
Domestic violence stems from a disease within the human heart characterized by the absence of love and resultant cruelty. Cultivating love and security is essential for creating a peaceful world.

## Key Teachings
- The absence of love and the resulting cruelty acts as a disease that can silently invade the human heart, driving individuals toward domestic violence.
- Creating a peaceful world requires addressing this internal state and restoring love and security.

## Quotes
> "The absence of love and the resultant cruelty is a disease that can silently invade the human heart and pushes us to domestic violence." — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- **Absence of Love**: A state that leads to cruelty and domestic violence within human hearts and homes.
