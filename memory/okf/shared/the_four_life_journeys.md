# The Four Life Journeys

## Summary
In The Four Sacred Secrets, Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji present Four Life Journeys that correspond to the core arenas of human experience: childhood healing, inner integration, intimate partnership, and purposeful wealth creation.

## The Four Life Journeys
1. **The First Life Journey: Heal the Wounded Child**
   - Heals childhood emotional wounds, feelings of unworthiness, lack, and abandonment that form the subconscious script for adult anxieties.
   - Restores emotional innocence and self-love.

2. **The Second Life Journey: Dissolve the Inner Divide**
   - Transcends the "warring self" and self-criticism.
   - Replaces inner conflict, self-punishment, and rigid ideals with self-acceptance, wholeness, and peace.

3. **The Third Life Journey: Become a Heartful Partner**
   - Heals the shadow of hurt in intimate relationships and marriages.
   - Dissolves judgment, blame, and defensive withdrawal, enabling partners to listen from a beautiful state and nurture genuine love.

4. **The Fourth Life Journey: Emerge into a Conscious Wealth Creator**
   - Redefines success and abundance. Transforms wealth creation from a pursuit driven by scarcity, status, and greed into a joyful, spiritual contribution that benefits all of humanity.

## Key Teachings
- Unhealed childhood wounds manifest as adult control, fear, and emotional volatility.
- Inner division must dissolve before outer unity can be established in relationships.
- A heartful partnership requires experiencing the other person as precious and irreplaceable, rather than an object to satisfy our needs.
- Conscious wealth is created from a state of abundance and connection, creating value for the larger ecosystem.
