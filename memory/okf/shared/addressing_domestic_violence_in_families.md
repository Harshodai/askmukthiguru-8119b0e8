# Addressing Domestic Violence in Families

## Summary
Domestic violence is a pervasive global issue that affects individuals across all age groups, genders, and nations, standing in contrast to the vision of peaceful and loving family environments.

## Key Teachings
- Domestic violence is not limited to any single demographic; Sri Preethaji and Sri Krishnaji point out that it is experienced globally by men, women, children, and the elderly in every country.

## Quotes
> "Domestic violence is experienced by men, by women, by children and elderly in every country." — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- peaceful family: Creating a secure home environment free from domestic abuse and violence across all demographics.
