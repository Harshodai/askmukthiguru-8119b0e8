# Oneness and Non-Separation

## Summary
The ultimate miracle in life is residing in a state of consciousness that is fully awakened to Oneness with all living beings and all of life.

## Key Teachings
- Living with a consciousness awakened to Oneness with all life is defined as the truest miracle.

## Quotes
> "The truest miracle is to live with a consciousness that is awakened to Oneness with all life." — Ekam / O&O Academy

## Related Concepts
- awakened consciousness: A state of being that perceives and experiences Oneness with all life.
