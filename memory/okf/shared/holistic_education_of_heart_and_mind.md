# Holistic Education of Heart and Mind

# Holistic Education of Heart and Mind

## Summary
True education must engage both the intellect and the heart. Rather than desperately chasing after success, individuals are encouraged to conquer their fear of failure so they can approach life with playfulness.

## Key Teachings
- Do not chase after success; instead, focus on conquering your fear of failure.
- Moving past the fear of failure allows a person to play through life rather than acting out of desperation.
- Authentic and complete education requires engaging both the heart and the intellect.

## Quotes
> "Do not chase after success, but conquer your fear of failure so you can play through life." — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji

## Related Concepts
- Playfulness vs. Desperation: Approaching goals and life without the fear of failure, leading to a state of play rather than desperate pursuit.
- Education of Heart and Mind: An approach to learning and growth that integrates intellectual engagement with emotional and spiritual awareness.
