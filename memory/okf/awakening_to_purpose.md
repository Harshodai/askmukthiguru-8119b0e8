# Awakening To Purpose

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
The rest of the frogs in the well croakingly agreed. The majority of human lives are lived in painful limitations. We live little measurable lives. We identify ourselves as being our bodies alone and live in fear of sickness, old age and death. Or we identify ourselves as our past histories and achievements alone and live in pride or humiliation. To awaken to purpose requires us to get out of our will of separation.

The discovery of purpose is a single step journey. Only when we break free of our limiting preoccupation with ourselves and awaken to a state of connection will we find our purpose unveiled. So what then is connection? Connection is gratitude. It is the awareness that we are the result of innumerable efforts of life forms and people we know and may never know.

## Key Teachings
- To awaken to purpose requires us to get out of our will of separation. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- If you do not have a clear purpose, we just go through the motions. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- The discovery of purpose is a single step journey. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
