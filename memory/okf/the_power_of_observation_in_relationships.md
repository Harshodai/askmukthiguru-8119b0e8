# The Power Of Observation In Relationships

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
In today's world, true cooperation and partnership is all based on legal frameworks. Now, it's surprising to see that even today, married couples want to first enter into an agreement before they get married, so that when they are going to divorce, it's all written how they're going to divorce. So, it's very funny. So, there's really no true cooperation anywhere now. It's all predetermined, and everything is running on wealth, everything is running on performance, everything is running on importance and significance and deliverance, which is all good. But we have completely forgotten to really connect, and we have forgotten that there is an aspect, which is the consciousness aspect. Where, if you are coming from a transformed state of consciousness, which is from a beautiful state, then there's a lot more magic in your cooperation and in your partnership.

Actually, you don't need these predetermined agreements, which are happening for marriages, because you are actually working towards living a life, which is a purpose, which is of meaning. And actually, you're enjoying every moment of your life, because both of you now have total trust, and both of you know why you're doing what you're doing, why you both are together, and what's the journey forward. So, there is no trust today, even in businesses, amongst business partners, business cooperation, because there are a few leaders who are great leaders, who run on trust and who run from a transformed state. But there are many, many people in the world who have absolutely no trust, and, in fact, they have no vision at all. It's just about having a very short vision, which is trying to find fault every day, and living in a state of fear and insecurity - what's going to happen? What's going to happen? And when you are in that state, you're obviously going to break the process of progress. You're obviously going to face failure much faster than you expected.
