# Discovering Your Swadharma


## Summary
The teaching emphasizes the importance of discovering and living one's *swadharma*, which is the true path aligned with one's core nature and passion. Following one's *swadharma* is presented as essential for creating social, political, cultural, and environmental change, particularly highlighting that young people are uniquely positioned to initiate revolutions by focusing on their true calling.

## Key Teachings
- *Swadharma* is the path that aligns with one's core nature and passion (Ekam / O&O Academy says).
- Discovering and following one's *swadharma* is crucial for creating social, political, cultural, and environmental change (Ekam / O&O Academy says).
- Young people are uniquely positioned to initiate revolutions by focusing on their true calling (Ekam / O&O Academy says).
