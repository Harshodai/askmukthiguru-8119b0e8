# Ending Toxic Relationships

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Giving someone and letting go of your hurt is absolutely necessary for your mental and emotional health and well-being. Let alone to keep your relationship going. That's secondary. Only people who can let go and move on are emotionally healthy and they alone can achieve anything worthwhile. Now, coming to partner relationships, it is absolutely necessary that both of you who are engaged in the relationship will have to put in the work.

Because no human being is perfect. I'm sure all of you will agree with this. To expect that someone who is an incarnation of love and perfection will come into your life is a fantasy. You know, I meet a lot of youngsters, they'll go, oh, this perfect person who must only think about me, who must only love me, must do nothing else. Is that all possible? If someone has to only think about you and only love you, then be very, very sure that person is 80% mad.
