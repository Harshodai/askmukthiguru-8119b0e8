# Healing Hearts And Family Transformation

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
I want you to know the secret of consciousness: in whichever family the woman is disrespected and violated, in whichever family a girl child is violated, that family can never prosper. They will lose their abundance; they will not receive the grace of the divine. If you really want to transform, take this step today. Sit together with your family, hold each other's hands without shyness, ask the divine to bless you and your family, to live with peace and love, and to heal your hearts of all hunger and bitterness.

In whichever family the woman is disrespected and violated, in whichever family a girl child is violated, that family can never prosper. They will lose their abundance. Namaste.

## Key Teachings
- Sit together with your family, hold each other's hands without shyness, ask the divine to bless you and your family, to live with peace and love, and to heal your hearts of all hunger and bitterness. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- In whichever family the woman is disrespected and violated, in whichever family a girl child is violated, that family can never prosper. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- Take the time, heal the hearts of people you have hurt. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
