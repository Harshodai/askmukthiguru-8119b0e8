# Living In A Beautiful Inner State

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
Neither is good time permanent nor is the bad time permanent. Winning and losing, progress and passivity, strength and weakness all will move in cycles. When you are spiritually awakened, you celebrate the wholeness of life. Without denying anything, you become a stita pregna who is unperturbed in triumphs and defeats, attachments, fears or frustrations. You live in a beautiful inner state.

Ugaadi is celebrated on the first day of the lunar calendar of Chaitramasa. Ugaadi is the day you create your new inner universe. You create it with a sacred sankalpa to flow gracefully and gratefully through every season of the year, be it the spring, spring of abundance or the summer of hardships or the rainfall of relief with the fullest knowing that life will indeed go through many of these cycles and nothing lasts forever.

## Key Teachings
- You live in a beautiful inner state. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- Ugaadi is the day you create your new inner universe. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- In this state, the bitter, the sweet, the sour, the spice, the bland and the astringent, all tastes of life are experienced. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
