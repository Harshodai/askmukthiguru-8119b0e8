# Inner Peace And Ego

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
When Ravana was unable to please Lord Shiva even after performing the toughest of meditations, he clearly sees his ego, pride, arrogance, craving for power, lust, nine different suffering states, all hurting him. He sends the lack of inner peace and serenity and choose to become free of it. That is what is symbolized in the severing of heads. He experiences this state of inner ease and becomes a peaceful being.

Well, rest of us find it very hard to manage with one, right? Imagine what would happen if your mind could stop thinking for a little while and you were able to expand into stillness and peace. How would you feel if you were not dwelling on problems, fears and worries instead enjoyed restful moments of peace? Would that peace not be a great healing gift from the divine to you?

## Key Teachings
- Peace is a state of inner calm where you are at ease with yourself. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- He sends the lack of inner peace and serenity and choose to become free of it. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- Imagine what would happen if your mind could stop thinking for a little while and you were able to expand into stillness and peace. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
