# The Experience Of Love And The Sacredness Of Connection

## Verbatim Discourse Excerpts
You feel a deep sense of sacredness being around the child, and spontaneously you bring total presence to them. You feel this preciousness when you know a loved one is passing. It strikes you that this person once gone is irreplaceable in your life. But this awareness of the sacred preciousness of love that happens in the beginning of life and at the end of life is completely lost in between.

Life itself is lived like a blur. Remember, any day when you come upon love, you feel you are the presence of the sacred. When your heart realizes that the other with all their imperfections is irreplaceable in life, you experience love. You bring your attention and presence to the beauty of the other. You recognize someone's kindness or patience or strength, courage, forgiveness or joyfulness.

## Key Teachings
- When your heart realizes that the other with all their imperfections is irreplaceable in life, you experience love. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- In a fundamental sense, love is a failing of preciousness. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
- Love is where you feel the other to be a sacred gift from the universe. — Sri Preethaji & Sri Krishnaji
